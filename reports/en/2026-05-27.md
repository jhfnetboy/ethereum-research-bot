## Key topics

### 1. Transactions and gas: EIP-2780
**What happened**
- [EIP-2780: precompile target and account-warming charge changes](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11735) (rakita), submitted: a precompile as `tx.to` is metered like any other account, removing carve-outs such as the "warm at tx start, charged zero" rule.
- [EIP-8146: update the BAL sidecar spec and align with ePBS](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11736) (nerolation), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For users: fixed transaction costs computed uniformly by the resources accessed make gas estimation more consistent across wallets and clients, and leave fewer special paths to exploit.

### 2. Accounts: keyed nonces and signatures
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Async Nonces on EVVM, experience to improve EIP-8250 Keyed Nonces](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/async-nonces-on-evvm-experience-to-improve-eip-8250-keyed-nonces/28642), 23:35 (ariutokintumi).
- [ERC-7964: tight signature encoding spec](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1775) (ernestognw), submitted and merged.
- [StarkWare: on Starknet, upgrading from a regular wallet to a post-quantum safe one will take one click](https://x.com/StarkWareLtd/status/2059410518331289957), 06:05.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: Starknet accounts are contracts already, so switching signature schemes just means swapping validation logic; for Ethereum to get the same effect it relies on proposals like 7702 and frame transactions that make account validation programmable.
