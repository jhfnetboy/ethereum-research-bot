## Key topics

### 1. Transaction signatures: post-quantum verification lanes
**What happened**
- [Magicians: PQ on EVM, stop mixing native, ZK and protocol enforcement](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/pq-on-evm-stop-mixing-native-zk-and-protocol-enforcement/27680) (pipavlo82), 06:54: gas comparisons of Falcon, Dilithium and ML-DSA are structurally misleading unless native in contract verification, ZK proof verification and protocol enforcement are separated first.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: smart accounts can adopt post-quantum signatures in contracts today while protocol schemes wait for a hard fork, so separating the lanes is how to judge which ships first.
