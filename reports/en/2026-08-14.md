## Key topics

### 1. Gas and signature semantics in EIP-8141
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: add an explicit second dimension for state gas to frames](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12062) (lightclient), merged. It splits a frame's `gas_limit` into `execution_gas_limit` and `state_gas_limit`: execution gas continues to be (generally) a call-frame concept, while state gas is treated as a frame-level concept.
- [EIP-8141: clarify floor repricing, signature validation and frame.value gas](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12026) (AnkushinDaniil), merged. The author is implementing EIP-8141 in Nethermind and cross-testing against ethrex on a devnet; three places in the text could be read more than one way, and two had already caused real divergences. Also merged that day: [a frame receipt carries no transaction-level status](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12061).
- [EIP-8141: restrict SIGPARAM signer introspection to accounts with empty or delegated code](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12168) (Marchhill), submitted. EIP-8151 stops a migrated account's revoked ECDSA key being presented as authorization through `ecRecover`, but `SIGPARAM(0x00)` hands `resolved_signer` to EVM code with no way for the consumer to tell a live key from a revoked one. The companion [EIP-8151: state the revoked-key invariant](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12169) was submitted.
- [EIP-8141: relax the banned opcode list](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12167) (Marchhill), submitted. The trace rules permit `SSTORE` to `tx.sender`'s storage inside the first `deploy` frame, but the banned list bans `SSTORE` outright while carving out `CREATE`, `CREATE2` and `SETDELEGATE`; the PR adds the matching carve-out.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and account migration: once an EOA moves to a smart account or a post-quantum signature, the old ECDSA key has to stop working everywhere in the protocol, or a stolen old key can still pose as authorization. Splitting gas dimensions lets wallets estimate execution and state creation costs separately.

### 2. Glamsterdam repricing and multidimensional fees
**What happened**
- [ECH: Jochem Brouwer walks through benchmark results for EIP-2780, EIP-8037 and EIP-8038, confirming the repricing numbers are safe and final](https://x.com/ECHInstitute/status/2087975498102264068), 01:52.
- [An EIP-7999 update](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12170) (anderselowsson), merged; [Magicians: preserving censorship resistance for BAL data in EIP-7999](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/preserving-censorship-resistance-for-bal-data-in-eip-7999/29427), 20:05. EIP-7999 treats data as an unconditional resource under FOCIL, so a builder may not skip an IL transaction just because the block's data resource is full, which relies on each IL being capped at 8 KiB.
- [EIP-8261 moved to Review](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12164), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For contract developers: with the repricing numbers final, gas changes to contracts can be assessed against the final parameters.
