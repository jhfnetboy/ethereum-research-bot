## Key topics

### 1. Frame transactions and modular accounts
**What happened**
- [New ERC: Modular Accounts for Frame Transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1794) (chiranjeev13), merged as a Draft, ERC-8286. ERC-7579 stays the core module system, and this ERC adds the validation flow for EIP-8141 frame transactions: a validator module returns an approval mode that the account applies via the `APPROVE` instruction during a VERIFY frame.
- [EIP-8141: apply charged_gas to fee settlement](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11969) (soispoke), merged, so refunds, the calldata floor and block gas accounting all follow `charged_gas`.
- [EIP-7906: add POST_TX enforcement to security considerations](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12020) (forshtat), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For smart account developers: ERC-7579 modular accounts can keep their validator modules and reach native AA through this layer, which helps lower the cost of moving from 4337 to 8141.
