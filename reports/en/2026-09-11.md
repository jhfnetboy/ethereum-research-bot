## Key topics

### 1. Implementation progress on frames and aggregation
**What happened**
- [ethrex: EIP-8141 is now a Hegotá headliner](https://x.com/ethrex_client/status/2098440608922608110), 22:56; ethrex backed frames from the start, and its implementation now runs two public testnets, one dedicated to frame transactions.
- [Derek Chiang: EIP-8288 would be one of the most groundbreaking updates if shipped](https://x.com/decentrek/status/2098392867773509799), 19:47, explaining it plainly starting from how Ethereum scales today in execution (gas limit), blobs (data availability) and so on.
- [EIP-8250: preserve nested fees in the transaction payload](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12316) was merged (danceratopz), matching EIP-8141's list of three fee parameters as one field in the outer RLP.
- [EIP-8038: preserve the warm SELFDESTRUCT access exemption](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12317) (merged September 14): cold beneficiaries pay `COLD_ACCOUNT_ACCESS` and warm ones pay no access charge, consistent with EIP-2929 and Geth.
- [EIP-7805: skip blob transactions in the inclusion list check](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12319), a draft to gauge agreement.

**Why it matters**
- With the headliner set, clients are aligning the wire format and repricing details in practice. For wallets, transaction encoding should follow the merged spec, since these fixes affect signing and serialization.

### 2. Privacy products
**What happened**
- [koeppelmann: Bermuda launches, the first privacy solution that works fully with Safe](https://x.com/koeppelmann/status/2098426474223050910), 22:00, with privacy pool funds controlled by a Safe, so access to or recovery of the Safe means access to the funds.
- [StarkWare: Erebus is building private payments for AI agents](https://x.com/StarkWareLtd/status/2098412441101295924), 21:04, without exposing payees or transaction metadata.

**Why it matters**
- Combining privacy pools with multisig and social recovery lets institutional and team funds use privacy without relying on a single private key.
