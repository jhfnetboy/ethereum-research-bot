## Key topics

### 1. Hegotá: FOCIL is the headliner
**What happened**
- [ACDC #175](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdc/175), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #12](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-12/): Hegotá's consensus layer headliner is [EIP-7805](https://forkcast.org/eips/7805/) fork choice enforced inclusion lists (FOCIL); [soispoke: FOCIL was just SFI'd as the Hegotá CL headliner, so Ethereum is prioritizing censorship resistance and inclusion guarantees](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2024487767871299767), 21:14.
- [VitalikButerin: there is an important synergy between FOCIL and AA (EIP-8141, based on 7701); 8141 makes smart accounts, including multisig, quantum resistant signatures, key changes and gas sponsorship, first class citizens, and can do the same for privacy protocols](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2024523896360464791), 23:38.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts and privacy: without native AA, smart account and privacy protocol transactions go through bundlers or relayers that FOCIL cannot protect; shipping both extends censorship resistance to those users.
