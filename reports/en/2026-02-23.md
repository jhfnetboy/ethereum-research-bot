## Key topics

### 1. Privacy: private payments in the account model
**What happened**
- [Magicians: VOSA, Virtual One-time Sub-Account, a simplified privacy primitive for EVM](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/vosa-virtual-one-time-sub-account-a-simplified-privacy-primitive-for-evm/27809) (louisliu2048), 09:57: trades some fund flow privacy for simplicity and compliance, essentially UTXO without Merkle trees using O(1) spent markers with epoch cleanup, where addresses exist only as mapping keys and are used once.
- [ethresear.ch: Anonymous PGC, practical anonymous and confidential payment for account based blockchains](https://ethresear.ch/t/anonymous-pgc-practical-anonymous-confidential-payment-for-account-based-blockchains/24185) (yuchen1024), 16:26, accepted at EUROCRYPT 2026 and extending the 2019 PGC design that used Twisted ElGamal for confidential transfers, later adopted by Solana.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: Ethereum uses accounts and most privacy designs maintain a separate note tree; these two approach hiding amounts and links within the account model from the simplicity and cryptography ends.

### 2. Gas and Glamsterdam
**What happened**
- [ACDT #71](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdt/071/), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #13](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-13/): bal-devnet-3 is planned for March 4 with EIP-8037 hardcoded for 100M gas because dynamic cost is hard to implement in the testing framework; epbs-devnet-0 is also planned for March 4.
