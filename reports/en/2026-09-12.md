## Key topics

### 1. Gas and validation rules for frame and keystore transactions
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: define early rejection and payer revalidation rules](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12321) (sm-stack). The current rules can require expensive validation for transactions that already fail structural checks, or repeat validation across many pending transactions after one change to a shared payer or factory; the PR addresses four sources of amplification, starting with encoding and static checks before signature verification.
- [EIP-8130: apply the EIP-7623 calldata floor to intrinsic gas](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12320) (chunter-cb, merged September 16). An 8130 transaction's bytes live in `calls`, `account_changes` and other fields rather than `data`, and were priced at a flat 16/4 per byte with no floor, so on chains that adopted EIP-7623 the floor never bound.
- [EIP-8288: align with EIP-8141's current frame fields and instructions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12322) (ilitteri).

**Why it matters**
- Sponsors and privacy apps make many transactions depend on the same payer contract, and a single change could force nodes to revalidate all of them; these rules decide whether the public mempool can safely hold them. For L2s adopting 8130, the calldata floor affects transaction costs.
