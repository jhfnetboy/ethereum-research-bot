## Key topics

### 1. Accounts: EOA migration under the quantum threat
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Migration Strategies for EOAs under the Quantum Threat, breakages and open questions](https://ethresear.ch/t/migration-strategies-for-eoas-under-the-quantum-threat-breakages-and-open-questions/23864) (Marcolopeez), 21:18: the paper focuses on the execution layer, meaning EOA authorization, transactions and signature flows, and how they migrate once a quantum computer can break secp256k1 and ECDSA, leaving the consensus layer aside.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: an EOA's key is the account, so quantum migration cannot just swap keys; it needs EIP-7702 delegation, key deactivation or protocol AA, and this paper lists where things break.

### 2. Transaction privacy: the EIP-8105 encrypted mempool
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Hegotá headliner proposal EIP-8105 Universal Enshrined Encrypted Mempool (EEM)](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-proposal-eip-8105-universal-enshrined-encrypted-mempool-eem/27448) (jannikluhn), 18:24: an optional protocol level feature letting users submit transactions that stay encrypted until included, protecting against frontrunning, sandwich attacks and real time censorship.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: an enshrined encrypted mempool lets any wallet offer frontrunning protection by default without private relays.
