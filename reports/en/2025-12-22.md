## Key topics

### 1. Privacy: stealth address receiving
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Draft ERC, privacy preserving receiving via stealth addresses (ERC-8065 extension)](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/draft-erc-privacy-preserving-receiving-via-stealth-addresses-erc-8065-extension/27277) (ZyraV21), 16:31: adds ECDH stealth addresses to zero knowledge token wrappers, coauthored by doublespending.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: the wrapper hides balances and transfers and stealth addresses hide the recipient, and together they approach full onchain payment privacy.

### 2. Transactions: parallel nonces and recurring payments
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Nonce Bitmap, parallel transaction submission for a parallel blockchain](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/nonce-bitmap-enabling-parallel-transaction-submission-for-a-parallel-blockchain/27280) (thnhnv), 20:51: sequential nonces force traders to submit one at a time, and a bitmap stops transactions blocking each other; [Magicians: CMSP, a bounded consent primitive for non-custodial recurring payments](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/discussion-cmsp-a-bounded-consent-protocol-primitive-for-non-custodial-recurring-payments/27282) (dperezcabrera), 22:01.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: parallel nonces are a shared need of ERC-4337 two dimensional nonces and native AA, and recurring payments are a classic case for scoped session authority.
