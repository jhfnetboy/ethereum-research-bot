### 3. Privacy: local notifications
**What happened**
- [Magicians: serverless, privacy first local notifications for dApps](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/architecture-proposal-serverless-privacy-first-local-notifications-for-dapps/27429) (nicosampler), 20:35: monitors events such as a loan nearing liquidation client side with Service Workers and direct contract calls, with no central backend or third party messaging.
