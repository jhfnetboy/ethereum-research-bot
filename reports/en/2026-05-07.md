## Key topics

### 1. ACDE #236
**What happened**
- Call notes are from [Ethereal news #22](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-22/): bal-devnet-7 targets next week as the last planned EL devnet; glamsterdam-devnet-3 launched; EIP-8252 (EL reorg state retention window) and EIP-8254 (cap deposit requests per block) were proposed for inclusion and EIP-8246 (remove SELFDESTRUCT burn) considered for inclusion; for Hegotá, non headliners EIP-7709 (read BLOCKHASH from storage and update cost) and EIP-8253 (remove pre-Spurious-Dragon accounts) were proposed.
- [Magicians: can we completely remove SELFDESTRUCT?](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/can-we-completely-remove-selfdestruct/28464), 17:26, with a usage analysis; [EIP-8252 discussion](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8252-execution-layer-reorg-state-retention-window/28463), 13:29.

**Why it matters**
- For users: the last EL devnet means Glamsterdam's execution scope is largely settled, with testnets next.

### 2. Native AA: Hegotá and EIP-8130
**What happened**
- [EIP-8081: PFI EIPs 8131, 8146 and 8250 for Hegotá](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11615) (nerolation), submitted.
- [EIP-8130: fix four critical authorization and replay issues](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11612) (pochenai), submitted: for example limiting the implicit EOA authorization rule to the native secp256k1 path, since otherwise a generic verifier returning the from address could satisfy implicit authorization for almost every EOA without a written config.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy and accounts: proposing EIP-8250 for Hegotá opens the way for privacy protocols to share a sender; left unfixed, the EIP-8130 issue could let any verifier impersonate existing EOAs.
