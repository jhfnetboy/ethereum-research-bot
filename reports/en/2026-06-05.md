## Key topics

### 1. EIP-8037 and account related gas changes
**What happened**
- [lightclient: EIP-8037 is scheduled for Glamsterdam and will increase the gas cost of creating new accounts and writing new storage slots by 5 to 8x](https://x.com/lightclients/status/2062592768439202225), 00:50, asking which protocols hard-code state gas expectations and what might break.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and contracts: first transfers to new addresses, deploying smart accounts and writing new storage slots all become more expensive. Sponsoring contracts, paymasters and calls with fixed gas limits need new estimates or they may run out of gas.

### 2. EIP-8130 and wallet signing safety
**What happened**
- [EIP-8130: clean up, reorganize and finalize pass](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11767) (chunter-cb), submitted and merged; [EIP-8130: fixes and clarifications](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11769), submitted and merged.
- [Magicians: ERC-8283 On-Chain Registry for ERC-7730 Clear Signing Descriptors](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8283-on-chain-registry-for-erc-7730-clear-signing-descriptors/28717), 21:37 (alex-forshtat-tbk). ERC-7730 addresses blind signing but does not specify how the community can permissionlessly maintain and update the descriptor registry; this proposal fills that gap.
- [Magicians: post-quantum ERC-4337 wallet WOTS-39](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/post-quantum-erc-4337-wots-39-winternitz-one-time-signature-wallet-for-ethereum/28715), 11:50, based on Winternitz one-time signatures.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: clear signing depends on a trusted descriptor source, and an onchain registry decides who can publish and update descriptors. Post-quantum signatures can be tried in ERC-4337 contract accounts first without waiting for protocol changes.

### 3. Privacy: the Zcash bug and cross-protocol shielded notes
**What happened**
- [ameensol: zcashers should encourage everyone to exit the Orchard pool](https://x.com/ameensol/status/2062692764140310542), 07:27; right now there is no way to know whether the exploit was used other than a mass exit to see whether the last ones out get stuck (the turnstile enforces out = in). [Eli Ben-Sasson: if a hacker used the exploit we will likely know within days, as they rush to the turnstile to unshield](https://x.com/EliBenSasson/status/2062804412003705290), 14:51.
- [dankrad: this is a general problem with zk, exploits become impossible to detect](https://x.com/dankrad/status/2062829270913679414), 16:29, which is why he was always skeptical of zk rollups that only publish state changes. [pcaversaccio: Zcash has some of the best security and cryptography people](https://x.com/pcaversaccio/status/2062805325120696456), 14:54.
- [New ERC: Shielded Note Teleportation](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1800) (geovgy), submitted, with a [Magicians thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8290-shielded-note-teleportation/28721) at 23:39; this is ERC-8290. While exploring EIP-7503 zkwormholes, the author found a way to move assets between UTXO privacy protocols such as Tornado Cash, 0xbow's Privacy Pools and Railgun without publicly withdrawing and redepositing.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy infrastructure: a public withdrawal and redeposit exposes linkage, and cross-protocol transfers avoid it. The Zcash incident is also a reminder that supply constraints on shielded pools (the turnstile) and formal verification are the last line of defense in privacy systems.
