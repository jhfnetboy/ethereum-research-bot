### 2. Rollups and BALs
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Synchronous Composability Between Rollups via Realtime Proving](https://ethresear.ch/t/synchronous-composability-between-rollups-via-realtime-proving/23998) (jbaylina), 14:55: based rollups have no privileged sequencer, so anyone can propose blocks, and with realtime proving calls can compose across rollups within one slot.
- [ethresear.ch: Early Rejection of Adversarial BALs](https://ethresear.ch/t/early-rejection-of-adversarial-bals/23995) (Nero_eth), 13:23: EIP-7928 requires builders to declare all state locations a block touches, and the post covers spotting and rejecting false BALs before execution.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: synchronous composability lets one user transaction act across rollups without waiting on a bridge.
