## Key topics

### 1. Pluggable signatures for frame transactions
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Frame Authenticator Signatures (EIP-8397)](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12244) was merged (leekt). It is a companion Core EIP to EIP-8141. The signature is `key_id || proof`; the authenticator runs arbitrary cryptography in a state-independent, gas-fixed context, the authenticated key_id must equal the claimed key_id, and the transaction then proceeds to the VERIFY frame for account authorization as usual.
- [EIP-8141 PR: commit to elided signature lengths](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12502) (lightclient).
- [New EIP: EVM Verification Key Registry (EIP-8357)](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12055) was merged (lucadonnoh); [SSZ Compact Multiproofs](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12093) and [Retire 0x00 Validators](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12390) were merged the same day.

**Why it matters**
- Authenticators take signature verification out of the account contract and have the protocol run it at a fixed gas cost. Being state-independent and gas-fixed means nodes can pre-verify cheaply, without hardcoding a signature algorithm. New schemes, such as post-quantum signatures, can plug in as authenticators while account authorization logic stays in the VERIFY frame.
