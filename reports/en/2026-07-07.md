## Key topics

### 1. Redrawing transaction gas
**What happened**
- [EIP-2780: move state-dependent charges to runtime (cold surcharge, new-account state gas)](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11844) (rakita), merged. Intrinsic gas becomes the bare minimum needed to include a transaction in a block; account access splits into an intrinsic `WARM_ACCESS` (a state-independent floor) plus a runtime cold surcharge, and the create transaction's new-account state gas is charged conditionally at runtime.
- [EIP-8037: charge account creation conditionally at access](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11858) (chfast), merged. A conditional charge at the single destination access that also performs the EIP-7610 collision check (an access EIP-7928 already records) replaces the unconditional-charge-with-refill model for CREATE/CREATE2; creations failing balance, nonce or depth checks never read the destination and pay nothing, and pre-existing destinations (such as pre-funded deterministic deployment addresses) pay nothing either.
- [EIP-8141: nits](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11870) (lightclient), submitted and merged: calling frame-related opcodes outside a frame transaction context is an exceptional halt, and data is charged as actual data, not RLP encoded.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and bundlers: with intrinsic gas independent of state, whether a transaction can be included is easier to decide without reading state, and gas estimation must count cold access and new-account charges at execution time. The 8037 change also stops pre-funded deterministic deployments, common for smart account factories, from being temporarily under-budgeted.
