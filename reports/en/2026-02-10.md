## Key topics

### 1. Gas: the EIP-8037 reservoir model
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: add quantization, a reservoir model and refined gas accounting](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11292) (misilva73), merged after submission on February 9: cost_per_state_byte is quantized to its top 4 significant bits so it does not change in small steps as the gas limit moves, and revert based TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT enforcement is replaced by a reservoir model splitting execution gas into gas_left and state_gas_reservoir; [EIP-8007: add gas cost summaries](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11296) (misilva73), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: accounting state gas apart from regular gas prices state growth separately without eating compute capacity, and quantization keeps the state cost wallets estimate stable.

### 2. Native AA: EIP-8141 APPROVE
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: relax the requirement that APPROVE be called by the top level call frame](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11297) (lightclient), submitted: accounts previously had to exit the top level frame with APPROVE, which no current smart account supports since the opcode only exists with 8141; relaxing it lets proxy deployed smart accounts adopt 8141 by switching implementations.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: the many smart accounts already deployed in the ERC-4337 era can move to native AA smoothly without redeploying or changing address.
