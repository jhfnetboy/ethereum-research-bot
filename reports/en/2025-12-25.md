## Key topics

### 1. Accounts: signed transfers
**What happened**
- [Magicians: ERC-8112 Token Transfer With Signature](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8112-token-transfer-with-signature/27310) (wenzhenxiang), 12:38, and [ERC-8114 NFT Transfer With Signature](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8114-nft-transfer-with-signature/27312), 13:25: extend smart contract wallets so owners delegate transfers by EIP-712 signature submitted by any relayer, with nonce accounting and execution rules; the [ERC PR](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1428) was submitted.
