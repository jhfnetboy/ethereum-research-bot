## Key topics

### 1. Account signatures: ERC-7913 and EIP-7932
**What happened**
- [ERC-7913: move to Final](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1213) (Amxx), merged after submission on September 16, 2025; [EIP-7932: migrate to the new registry](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10927) (SirSpudlington), submitted and merged; [EIP-6404: move to the EIP-7932 registry](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10926) (SirSpudlington), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: ERC-7913 lets accounts verify keys without an Ethereum address, such as passkeys or post-quantum keys, and EIP-7932 unifies signature algorithms at the protocol layer, together paving the way to post-quantum accounts.
