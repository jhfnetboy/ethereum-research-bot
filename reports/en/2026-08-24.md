## Key topics

### 1. What Glamsterdam repricing does to contracts
**What happened**
- [EF blog: Glamsterdam repricing impact](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/08/24/glamsterdam-repricing-testing). EIP-8037 raises and harmonizes the cost of new state (new accounts, new storage slots, deployed bytecode) and meters it separately; EIP-8038 raises the cost of `SSTORE`, `SLOAD`, cold account access and `EXTCODESIZE`/`EXTCODECOPY`. State operation gas was last adjusted in Berlin in 2021; the new schedule is derived from a performance target supporting roughly 3x base throughput and is a prerequisite for raising the gas limit further.
- Replaying historical mainnet transactions under the new schedule leaves the large majority unchanged; direct outreach to the most-affected builders is under way.

**Why it matters**
- For contract maintainers: if a contract is fixable with a gas-limit increase, update the gas limits your frontend, infra or users supply; if it is potentially broken, review the flagged call sites and test the fix on Platåberget (glam-devnet-8). Even contracts not on the list should review code that hardcodes gas values or branches on remaining gas. For wallets: gas estimation has to run under the new schedule.
