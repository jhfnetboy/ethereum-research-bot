# Ethereum Research Daily 2026-10-01

Keywords: ACDC #188, quick slots (EIP-8198) CFI, EIP-8365 CFI, FOCIL devnet-0 live, EIP-8141 bounds cost with max_fee, zk.money private wallet

Summary: ACDC #188 moved quick slots (EIP-8198) and disallowing new 0x00 validators (EIP-8365) to CFI for Hegotá, withdrew EIP-8363 and EIP-8333, and declined EIP-8379. FOCIL devnet-0 went live. On accounts, lightclient tried introducing `max_fee` to the frame object instead of per-dimension resource limits, adapted from EIP-7999. zk.money launched a private, self-custodial, open source wallet on Aztec, and the EF congratulated it publicly.

## Highlights
1. **ACDC #188**: EIP-8198 and EIP-8365 moved to CFI, with post-quantum impact and testing concerns raised about quick slots.
2. **FOCIL**: devnet-0 is live, putting the censorship resistance headliner to real tests.
3. **Frame transaction fees**: one `max_fee` bounds cost instead of per-dimension resource limits.
4. **Privacy wallet**: zk.money uses a new address each time, with gas included.

## Key topics

### 1. Block speed and censorship resistance in Hegotá
**What happened**
- ACDC #188 at 21:00 (per Ethereal news #41): EIP-8365 (disallow new 0x00 validators) and EIP-8198 quick slots moved to CFI, with post-quantum impact and testing concerns discussed; EIP-8363 tapered issuance burn and EIP-8333 were withdrawn; EIP-8379 Top-up Sync was declined. [barnabemonnot: quick slots moved to CFI in today's ACD call](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2105688718057074919), 22:58.
- [soispoke: FOCIL devnet 0 is live](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2105630593602212136), 19:07.
- [Derek Chiang: Ethereum has had 100% uptime since inception, so why are Ethlabs and the EF redesigning its consensus protocol](https://x.com/decentrek/status/2105650945631957129), 20:28, starting from today's ebb-and-flow consensus.
- [EIP-8333 moves to Withdrawn](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12413), merged; [EIP-8411 fast execution payload broadcast](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12299) was merged as a draft today (submitted September 5).

**Why it matters**
- Hegotá now has three main threads: frame transactions, FOCIL and quick slots, meaning native AA and privacy, censorship resistance and faster blocks. For dApps, both confirmation times and guarantees of forced inclusion will improve.

### 2. Frame transaction fees and a privacy wallet
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: use max_fee to bound cost](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12414) (lightclient), trying out a `max_fee` on the frame object instead of per-dimension resource limits, adapted from EIP-7999.
- [EIP-7906: elaborate the rationale for events access](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12412) (forshtat), merged.
- [EF: congratulations to zk.money on launching a private, self-custodial, open source wallet](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2105689868873715720), 23:02; [Aztec: zk.money is live on Aztec, with a new address, gas included and nothing pointing back](https://x.com/aztecnetwork/status/2105697536229155080), 23:33.
- [kassandraETH: for privacy to become a core property of Ethereum, we need common wallet standards for privacy rather than siloed solutions](https://x.com/kassandraETH/status/2105699015983788330), 23:39.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets, a single `max_fee` means users set one fee cap, simplifying the signing screen; for privacy features to spread, wallets need shared standards.
