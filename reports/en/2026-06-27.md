### 1. How to evaluate privacy protocols
**What happened**

- [StarkWare: shielding is now available directly at the wallet level](https://x.com/StarkWareLtd/status/2070870371264254371), 21:02.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy users: if the trusted setup of a SNARK-based privacy pool is broken, an attacker can forge proofs and withdraw from nothing. The number of participants and the ceremony design determine that risk, and together with the recent discussions of admin visibility and post-quantum privacy, this forms several dimensions for evaluating privacy pools.
