## Key topics

### 1. Transactions and gas: EIP-7976 and EIP-7981
**What happened**
- [EIP-7976: clarify token abstraction](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11525) (nerolation), submitted and merged: the floor cost in EIP-7976 and EIP-7981 is stated as 64 gas per calldata or access list byte (1280 gas per address, 2048 gas per storage key), with the token abstraction kept only for consistency with EIP-7623.
- [ethresear.ch: The L2 Fee Vault, pricing L1 costs with feedback control](https://ethresear.ch/t/the-l2-fee-vault-pricing-l1-costs-with-feedback-control/24656), 19:00 (linoscope, Nethermind Research and the University of Bern).

**Why it matters**
- For users: stating prices directly per byte makes the cost of data heavy transactions easier for wallets and developers to estimate, and including access lists in the floor closes a path around calldata pricing.

### 2. Native AA and wallet privacy
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: tighten spec](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11521) (benaadams), merged; the PR was submitted on April 13.
- [Magicians: draft ERC for a wallet privacy paymaster capability](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/draft-erc-wallet-privacy-paymaster-capability/28255), 22:31 (karandeep), using EIP-5792 capabilities so apps can ask compatible wallets to fund gas through a user controlled, privacy preserving paymaster.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: the hardest part of a private transaction is where the gas comes from, and a standard wallet capability for privacy paymasters lets apps request that path without each building its own integration.
