## Key topics

### 1. Privacy pools go post-quantum
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: faster, aggregatable and quantum-safe privacy pools](https://ethresear.ch/t/faster-aggregatable-and-quantum-safe-privacy-pools/26163), 17:59 (Pierre, with winderica). Pools like Tornado Cash rely on Pedersen commitments and Groth16 proofs, both resting on the hardness of discrete log. The authors port it to Flock, a post-quantum binary-field SNARK, proving a depth-32 withdrawal over SHA-256 in under 30 ms, and describe a simple recovery protocol: as long as wallets derive note secrets from a seed, existing pools can survive a discrete log break.
- [Vitalik: precompiles and the EVM are both the wrong place to put most cryptography support](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2112032009413439704), 11:04, because the data they operate on has to be included in the chain, which realistically means block calldata.
- [EIP-8288 PR: move the recursive STARK into the execution payload](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12549) (nerolation).

**Why it matters**
- For privacy pool users, the recovery protocol assumes note secrets are derived from a seed, so it is worth checking that when choosing a wallet and a pool. For the protocol, verifying proofs outside the EVM and aggregating them recursively per block (EIP-8288) is what spares privacy pools the calldata and pairing precompile costs of every transaction.
