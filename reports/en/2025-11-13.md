## Key topics

### 1. Account abstraction: EIL and the Trustless Manifesto
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: EIL, trust minimized cross-L2 interop](https://ethresear.ch/t/eil-trust-minimized-cross-l2-interop/23437) (yoavw), 08:31: rollups scaled but fragmented UX, and EIL lets wallets execute across L2s through the account without trusted relayers or bridges; [ethereumfndn: the AA team and Vitalik publish the Trustless Manifesto at trustlessmanifesto.eth](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/1988796996401131742), 09:32; [VitalikButerin: signed the Trustless Manifesto](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/1988764537034616903), 07:23.

**Why it matters**
- For AA: cross-chain UX is handled by the user's own account instead of solvers holding funds, making smart accounts the base of interop.

### 2. Privacy: enterprises and institutions
**What happened**
- [ethereum: pbrody writes that 2026 will be the golden year of Ethereum privacy, especially for enterprises](https://x.com/ethereum/status/1988991966223511746), 22:26; [ethresear.ch: privacy pools on EBSI for institutional programmable privacy and compliance](https://ethresear.ch/t/implementing-privacy-pools-on-ebsi-for-institutional-programmable-privacy-compliance/23442) (EugeRe), 18:29: users withdraw only if their deposit is in an approved association set, tested with an institutional identity framework.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: association sets let privacy and compliance coexist, a key path for institutional onchain privacy.
