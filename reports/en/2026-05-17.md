## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: guarantors
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: extend with guarantors, flexible nonces and signer binding](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11681) (pedrouid), submitted. A guarantor is a payer primitive that admits a transaction to the public mempool even when the sender's `VERIFY` frame is unsafe to simulate; it adopts PR #11555's `APPROVE_GUARANTEE = 0x4` scope and canonical paymaster guarantor mode verbatim.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: native AA validation logic can be arbitrarily complex, so nodes struggle to know whether it will fail; with someone guaranteeing the gas cost of a failure, transactions from complex accounts can propagate in the public mempool like ordinary ones.

### 2. Transaction logs and privacy
**What happened**
- [EIP-7708: use EIP-8246 to simplify](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11634) (marioevz), merged, making EIP-8246 a dependency to remove the ETH burn log edge case completely.
- [EIP-7979: add a section on optional validation for new opcodes](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11680) (gcolvin), submitted and merged.
- [Eli Ben-Sasson: few people understand how hard privacy with composability is; STRK20 is basically a privacy layer that lets users do full DeFi operations, not just transfers](https://x.com/EliBenSasson/status/2056012473925287998), 21:02.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: EIP-7708 makes ETH transfers emit logs like ERC-20 tokens, so wallets and indexers can see ETH flows without tracing internal calls.
