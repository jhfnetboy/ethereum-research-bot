### 1. Security: the rsETH bridge incident
**What happened**
- [koeppelmann: the rsETH bridge hack caused about $300 million in direct losses that someone will have to eat, and has already wiped out more than $200 million in Aave market cap](https://x.com/koeppelmann/status/2045840215357403262), 19:21.
- [koeppelmann: as much as it pains him, for things he has influence over he will switch to the default of security over neutrality: a "pause everything" function, a daily limit, an extra safety layer every transaction must pass](https://x.com/koeppelmann/status/2045848120920088612), 19:52; [bkiepuszewski: agrees, assuming that does not default to a centralized entity that can rug anyone, which is the case for most protocols](https://x.com/bkiepuszewski/status/2045855060848988285), 20:20.
- [LefterisJP: wants boring and safe DeFi where ETH can be parked at 1.7% APR and left alone](https://x.com/LefterisJP/status/2045623278492852503), 04:59.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: protections like daily limits, delays and pauses fit most naturally in the user's own smart account, where the user rather than the protocol controls the switch, which is the core value of programmable accounts over EOAs.
