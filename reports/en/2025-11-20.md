## Key topics

### 1. Account signatures: Falcon and EIP-7932
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Precompile for Falcon support](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10560) (simonmasson), merged after submission on October 1, 20257, 2025; [Magicians: Open question to core devs about EIP-7932](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/open-question-to-the-core-devs-about-eip-7932/26636) (SirSpudlington), 05:38: if EIP-6404 interoperates with EIP-7932, should it become a registry plus precompile so AA can verify signatures for every algorithm efficiently.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: the leaner the signature algorithm registry, the easier it is for smart accounts to adopt post-quantum schemes such as Falcon.

### 2. Accounts: EOAs and native rollups
**What happened**
- [Add ERC: Operator contract for non delegated EOAs](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1148) (marcelomorgado), merged after submission on August 5, 2025; [Add EIP: Native rollups](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10794) (lucadonnoh), merged after submission on November 14, 2025.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: EOAs without EIP-7702 delegation can still get batching and delegated execution through an operator contract.
