## Key topics

### 1. Transaction privacy: the LUCID encrypted mempool
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Hegotá headliner LUCID encrypted mempool](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-lucid-encrypted-mempool/27658) (jflo, authors Anders Elowsson, Julian Ma, Justin Florentine), 09:37: FOCIL lets includers propagate lists of transactions that must land next slot for censorship resistance, but cannot protect transactions open to MEV extraction; companion post [ethresear.ch: LUCID, encrypted mempool with distributed payload propagation](https://ethresear.ch/t/lucid-encrypted-mempool-with-distributed-payload-propagation/24042), 08:10.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy and transactions: encrypting transactions before inclusion removes the basis for frontrunning and sandwiching, and paired with FOCIL extends censorship resistance to DeFi trades.

### 2. Gas and state
**What happened**
- [VitalikButerin: hyper-scaling Ethereum state by creating new forms of state](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2019437232315056267), 22:45, full text on [ethresear.ch](https://ethresear.ch/t/hyper-scaling-state-by-creating-new-forms-of-state/24052), 22:38: scaling over the next five years means scaling execution, data and state, toward 1000x on L1.
- [EIP-7976: increase the floor data cost to 64/64](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11264) (nerolation), submitted: from 15/60 to 64/64 gas per byte, raising a 10 MiB payload from about 105M to about 671M gas.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: the calldata floor sets worst case block size, so raising it controls bandwidth risk while the gas limit rises.

### 3. Calls: ACDC #174
**What happened**
- [ACDC #174](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdc/174), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #10](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-10/): Hegotá consensus headliner candidates are [FOCIL (EIP-7805)](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-proposal-focil-eip-7805/27604) and [partial reconstruction and 2D PeerDAS](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-partial-reconstruction-and-2d-peerdas/27652), with strong client support for FOCIL and a pick at the next ACDC; EIP-8136 cell level deltas are backwards compatible and ship behind a feature flag without an upgrade.
