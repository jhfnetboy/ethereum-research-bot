## Highlights

3. **Three EIP-8288 PRs**: bounded complete mempool wrappers, dependency digests bound to verification schemes, and proof-carrying inclusion lists.

## Key topics

### 1. Native AA's post-quantum path: frames-devnet-1 and configurable accounts
**What happened**
- [ethrex: frames-devnet-0 will soon give way to frames-devnet-1](https://x.com/ethrex_client/status/2108648101384757631), 02:57, adding EIP-8250, EIP-8272 and EIP-7906, with execution-spec tests ready.
- [Georgios: not worried about PQ safety in Tempo](https://x.com/gakonst/status/2108643910591680677), 02:40. Pat's team covers the consensus side, and on the execution side configurable accounts allow in-place key rotation and disabling non-PQ keys.
- EIP-8288 (Marchhill): [bounded complete mempool wrappers](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12473), [dependency digests bound to verification schemes](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12474) and [proof-carrying inclusion lists](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12475).

**Why it matters**
- This is the protocol's answer to the bunker mode debate of the past few days: if accounts can switch signature schemes, there is no need for a mass address migration. Tempo shows that when the account model allows adding and removing keys, going post-quantum is just a key rotation. Once EIP-8288 connects with FOCIL inclusion lists, transactions carrying post-quantum proofs also get censorship-resistance guarantees.
