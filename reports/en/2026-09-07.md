## Key topics

### 1. Hegotá priorities
**What happened**
- [The EF Protocol cluster published two posts](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2096976125511541198), 21:57: the [Hegotá EIP opinion post and tier list](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/09/07/protocol-hegota-eips) and [current and emerging priorities](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/09/07/protocol-priorities). About 60 people and 16 contributions produced 397 grades across 62 EIPs (S = 4 down to D = 0). EIP-8141 is called the "locked-in EL headliner" on security grounds: a path to PQ signature schemes without a fork per scheme, aggregation so PQ verification can be priced, and a route to retiring k1 keys; it ships with EIP-8250 and EIP-8272. FOCIL is the locked-in CL headliner and should ship with EIP-8369.
- [soispoke's tier list cheat sheet](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2096977735381889164), 22:03; [nero_eth: in the coming weeks we decide what ships in Hegotá, and native privacy is the bundle of 8141, 8250 and 8272](https://x.com/nero_eth/status/2096851469580275926), 13:42.
- [ethresear.ch: how Hegotá should approach gas repricing](https://ethresear.ch/t/how-hegota-should-approach-gas-repricing/25935), 17:54 (misilva73), an opinionated overview of the 10+ repricing EIPs based on the last fork, warning about compatibility risks.

**Why it matters**
- The tier list largely sketches the 2027 fork: native AA and censorship resistance first. For wallet and account infrastructure teams, 8141, 8250 and 8272 are the bundle worth starting to prepare for.

### 2. Validation and state gas details for frame transactions
**What happened**
- [EIP-8272: count recent root verification gas](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12302) (soispoke) was merged. The verifier frame was excluded from EIP-8141's `MAX_VERIFY_GAS`; now its `limits.execution` counts toward the same cap as signature verification and the other frames in the validation prefix, with no separate allowance.
- [EIP-8037: keep pre-execution state gas out of the frame rollback](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12303) (chfast, merged September 14). Last week's rollback fix covered only the reservoir, so a top-level rollback credited a pre-execution charge back to `gas_left` while the state it paid for stayed written; for a transaction with one EIP-7702 authorization the whole `STATE_BYTES_PER_AUTH_BASE` charge was refilled though the delegation stayed applied.
- [EIP-7906: add a frame exemption flag](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12304), letting writes from `DEFAULT` and `SENDER` frames survive a `POST_TX` revert, with examples including fee-payer repayment.
- [ethresear.ch: order-dependence as the classifying dimension for frame-transaction mempool admission](https://ethresear.ch/t/order-dependence-as-the-classifying-dimension-for-frame-transaction-mempool-admission/25934), 17:49 (AnkushinDaniil).

**Why it matters**
- Each of these is about who pays gas for what. The privacy validation budget gets tighter and state gas accounting stricter, and wallets need to keep up with these rules when estimating gas.
