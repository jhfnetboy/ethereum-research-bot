## Key topics

### 1. ACDE #244: native AA scheduled for Hegotá
**What happened**
- [ECH Institute: ACDE #244 at 21:00](https://x.com/ECHInstitute/status/2092970596607066269), 20:40, with Glamsterdam updates, deposit contract PQ readiness and Hegotá on the agenda.
- Per Ethereal news #37: native AA was scheduled for inclusion in Hegotá, with weekly breakouts to attempt to converge the EIP-8141 Frame transaction and Base's EIP-8130 account abstraction by account configuration; Derek Chiang wrote an 8141 versus 8130 explainer.
- [EIP-8081: SFI EIP-8141](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12251) (nixorokish), submitted and merged, per the ACDE 244 decision: SFI Frames with the understanding that AA will ship in Hegotá and the specific implementation may change, including the EIP number.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and AA infrastructure: this is the first time Ethereum L1 has committed to shipping native AA in a specific fork. The current 8141 text is not the final spec, so wallet teams should follow the AA breakouts rather than implement 8141 as written today.

### 2. New native AA proposals
**What happened**
- [New EIP: native account abstraction transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12248) (pedrouid), submitted, with [Magicians: composable native account abstraction](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/composable-native-account-abstraction/29526) at 20:16. Three composable Core EIPs that leave EIP-8130 unchanged: a portable Keystore defining actors, authenticators, account creation and configuration; a basic native AA transaction with batching, sponsorship and keyed nonces; and optional advanced controls adding policies, account locking and nonce-free transactions in the same envelope (`0x79` in the PR). The numbering differs between the PR and the forum thread; Ethereal news #37 lists them as EIP-8398, 8401 and 8402.
- [Magicians: arguments for ephemeral accounts and implementation approaches](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/arguments-for-ephemeral-accounts-and-implementation-approaches/29524), 13:44 (Helkomine). Ephemeral accounts are pure execution containers deleted at the end of the transaction; today they can be built with CREATE/CREATE2 plus SELFDESTRUCT, but account creation is prohibitively expensive.
- [EIP-8130: remove the CONTRACT_ESTABLISHED flag](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12250) (chunter-cb), submitted and merged, shifting the remaining account-state flags down one bit: `DEFAULT_EOA_REVOKED` `0x02` to `0x01`, `LOCKED` `0x04` to `0x02`, and `UNLOCK_INITIATED` `0x08` to `0x04`.
- [EIP-8141: add valid_after to the expiry verifier frame](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12252) (nerolation), submitted. Frame data grows from 8 to 16 bytes, `valid_after || expiry_timestamp`, and requires `valid_after <= block.timestamp <= expiry_timestamp`; the mempool does not hold not-yet-valid transactions, which must be resubmitted once the window opens.
- [EIP-8037: refill state gas for a SELFDESTRUCTed account](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12249) (chfast), submitted: an account created and self-destructed in the same transaction pays about 1.7M state gas for a 1 kB deployment, for state that never lands.

**Why it matters**
- For the 8130 camp: splitting 8130 into composable parts lets chains support native and advanced features step by step and lets the Keystore be discussed apart from the transaction format. For dapps: `valid_after` makes scheduled transactions a protocol feature, though they must be rebroadcast once the window opens.

### 3. Glamsterdam and history
**What happened**
- Per Ethereal news #37: Platåberget (glamsterdam-devnet-8) spam testing is ongoing; glamsterdam-devnet-9 (a non-finality devnet) launches when client trunk branches are ready; public testnets are proposed for Sepolia on September 28 and Hoodi on October 26, to be confirmed at next week's ACDC; mainnet is potentially early December. EIP-4444 is to be updated to prune at 33,024 epochs, and post-quantum options for the deposit contract were discussed.
- Hegotá non-headliners: EIP-8360 TCREATE was proposed for inclusion as a placeholder for an ephemeral contract solution replacing SELFDESTRUCT refill; EIP-7609 (lower TLOAD/TSTORE base cost) and EIP-8058 (contract bytecode deduplication discount, superseded by EIP-8298 SETCODEFROM) were declined with no champion present.

**Why it matters**
- For node operators: once EIP-4444 lands, execution clients keep only bounded history, so services relying on old blocks or receipts need a history provider or their own archive.
