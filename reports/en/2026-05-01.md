## Key topics

### 1. Transactions: EIP-8246 removes the SELFDESTRUCT burn
**What happened**
- [Add EIP-8246: Remove SELFDESTRUCT Burn](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11590) (chfast), submitted; [Magicians discussion](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-proposal-remove-selfdestruct-burn/28416), 17:01: EIP-6780 removed the common burn behavior, but contracts created in the same transaction can still burn ETH through selfdestruct(self) or by receiving ETH later in the transaction before deletion; the case is basically unused yet forces special handling in clients, specs and tests.

**Why it matters**
- For users: removing a rare path where ETH can unexpectedly vanish makes transaction outcomes easier to reason about and drops special logic from clients.
