## Key topics

### 1. Gas: the calldata floor
**What happened**
- [EIP-7976: increase the floor data cost to 64/64](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11264) (nerolation), merged: the calldata floor rises from 15/60 to 64/64 gas per byte, so a 10 MiB payload needs about 671M gas instead of about 105M.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: the floor only bites data heavy, compute light transactions, so ordinary ones are unaffected; it shrinks the worst case block size and makes room to raise the gas limit safely.

### 2. Accounts and networking
**What happened**
- [EIP-7851: move to Draft](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11316) (colinlyguo), submitted; EIP-7851 lets EOAs with delegated code deactivate their original key.
- [Add EIP: eth/71 Block Access List Exchange](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11307) (nerolation), merged after submission on February 12; [EIP-7773: add EIP-7975 and EIP-8159 to the CFI list](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11319) (nerolation), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: after EIP-7702 delegation the original key keeps full control, and 7851 offers a way to retire it, the last step from EOA to smart account.
