## Key topics

### 1. Extending EIP-8130, and Base's commitment
**What happened**
- [jessepollak: EIP-8130 is a simple, high-ROI native account, and Base will roll it out](https://x.com/jessepollak/status/2090844327748796902), 23:51, looking forward to working with Ethereum and other EVM teams to do the same.
- [New EIP: stateless accounts](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12221) (chunter-cb), submitted, with a [Magicians thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8387-stateless-accounts/29473) at 18:41. It requires 8130 and defines `account_changes` type `0x03`: a transaction-scoped account with 8130 create's `initial_actors` array, a 7702 delegation for this transaction only, and no Keystore write. One mechanism has two uses: a unique salt gives an ephemeral account, and reusing the same parameters with a config change gives a long-lived one.
- [New EIP: Keystore signatures for execution transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12222) (chunter-cb), submitted. It authenticates EIP-2930 and EIP-1559 transactions through the Keystore instead of treating secp256k1 `ecrecover` as the authority; 65-byte signatures stay on the k1 default-EOA path, and any other signature is `account || authenticator || data`, so those transaction types can be post-quantum and aggregatable.
- [New EIP: transaction events view](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12218) (chunter-cb), submitted, with a [Magicians thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8386-transaction-events-view/29472) at 18:17.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: if Keystore signatures land, an existing EOA could switch to passkeys or post-quantum signatures for ordinary transactions without changing address. Base's public commitment means 8130 will run on at least one large L2, a reality the L1 debate between 8141 and 8130 has to account for.

### 2. Spec clashes and slot time
**What happened**
- [EIP-8250: move TXPARAM_LEGACY_NONCE off 0x0C](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12219) (AnkushinDaniil), submitted. EIP-8141 already assigns `TXPARAM` index `0x0C` to `state_gas_left`, so a chain enabling both would have two claims on one index; the PR moves it to the free `0x11`. The same author submitted [EIP-8272: move RECENTROOTREFLOAD off 0xB5](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12220).
- [EIP-8141: bound chain_id to 2**64](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12223) (Marchhill), submitted; [EIP-7805: skip IL transactions needing data the IL cannot carry](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12224) (Marchhill), submitted, since an IL carries only canonical transaction encodings and not a blob transaction's blobs, commitments and proofs.
- [EIP-8198: target 10-second slots and align with the reference specs](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12217) (barnabemonnot), submitted. It replaces the single `SLOT_DURATION_MS` change with a `SLOT_DURATION_SCHEDULE` modeled on EIP-7892's `BLOB_SCHEDULE`, with exact ratio scaling of rewards and other parameters.
- Two EIP-8141 PRs merged: [charge the resolved target's access at frame entry](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12211) and [scope APPROVE failure modes to the current call frame](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12212), along with [dispatch a precompile targeted by a frame](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12157).

**Why it matters**
- 8141, 8250 and 8272 are likely to ship together, so their opcode and parameter numbers must not collide; fixes like these show the three are being designed together for the same fork.
