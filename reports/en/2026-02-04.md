## Key topics

### 1. Gas: multidimensional metering
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: aggregation functions for EIP-8037 under different elasticity regimes](https://ethresear.ch/t/analysis-of-different-aggregation-functions-for-eip-8037-under-different-elasticity-regimes/24033) (misilva73), 19:30: compares three aggregation functions including Sum, since aggregation decides how state gas and burst gas jointly set the base fee, which drives throughput and state growth.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: users' final price depends on this choice; Sum makes the two resources crowd each other out, while other functions can let state writes price on their own.
