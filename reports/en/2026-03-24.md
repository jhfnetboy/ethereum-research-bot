## Key topics

### 1. Post-quantum: pq.ethereum.org
**What happened**
- [ethereumfndn: several EF teams launch pq.ethereum.org](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2036464704235692454), 22:26, a dedicated resource for Ethereum's post-quantum security effort, grown from early STARK based signature aggregation research in 2018 into an all open source multi team effort; per [Ethereal news #17](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-17/), the site has an overview, roadmap, resources, FAQ and research retreat details; [adietrichs: Ethereum is leading in quantum preparedness](https://x.com/adietrichs/status/2036244068821242325), 07:49.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: the site lists signature migration as core work, and moving user accounts to post-quantum ultimately depends on transaction formats and account abstraction, the backdrop to the EIP-8141 debate.

### 2. Native AA and privacy
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141 frame transaction breakout #2 agenda](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/headliner-breakout-eip-8141-frame-transaction-2-march-25-2026/28058), 05:59; per Ethereal news #17 the topic is [transaction pool strategies](https://hackmd.io/@matt/frame-mempool).
- [EIP-8182 update](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11441) (RogerPodacter), submitted and merged, continuing work on enshrined private ETH and ERC-20 transfers.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: whether frame transactions make Hegotá hinges on the public mempool safely validating transactions with arbitrary VERIFY logic, and the pool strategy is the answer to that.
