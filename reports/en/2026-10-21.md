## Key topics

### 1. Side effects of Glamsterdam repricing
**What happened**
- [pcaversaccio: anyone who still thinks EIP-8037/8038 is a good idea should read the latest ChainSecurity article](https://x.com/pcaversaccio/status/2112949950464270463), 23:51. These EIPs can force certain contracts to upgrade just to restore their intended behavior, despite zero bytecode changes.
- [EIP-8279: check the floor against the execution-gas budget](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12560) and [remove the unsafe storage-value refund](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12526) were merged (nerolation).
- [EIP-2780: align the titles and descriptions of Glamsterdam EIPs](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12561); [corrections to the Glamsterdam activation tables in EIP-7773 and EIP-8081](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12557), merged (ralexstokes).

**Why it matters**
- Contract logic that relies on "it fails if there isn't enough gas", for example calling with a fixed gas amount to limit what the callee can do, may behave differently under the new pricing. Contract teams should replay critical call paths on Sepolia under Amsterdam rules; users should watch whether the mainnet date slips.

### 2. Account and transaction specs
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141 PR: price frame and signature entries at the floor](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12559) (nerolation).
- [EIP-7906 PR: say where storage-kept nonce changes appear](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12562) (AnkushinDaniil).
- [EIP-8298 PR: add adoption guidance to Security Considerations](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12564) (nezu511).
- [EIP-8209: how a REVEAL transaction pays without a payer](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12539), merged.

**Why it matters**
- Pricing frame transaction data at the floor directly sets how much more an AA transaction with several signatures or frames costs than an ordinary one, which matters even more given the size of post-quantum signatures.
