## Key topics

### 1. EIP-8141 signature charging and semantics
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: charge for each arbitrary sig entry](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11976) (lightclient), submitted and merged as an alternative to #11935: to keep large numbers of empty `ARBITRARY` signature entries from being under-charged, a flat 100 gas per entry is added on top of regular calldata costs for all signatures.
- [EIP-8141: approval effects are not reverted by frame revert or atomic-batch unroll](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11955), merged; [define the Receipts message encoding for frame transaction receipts](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11942), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and gas estimation: bounding signatures by price rather than a hard cap keeps multisig and multi-frame transactions possible, with each signature entry paying 100 more gas. Once approval effects happen they are not undone by a later failing frame, which settles the sponsor's risk model.

### 2. Sponsorship and account-related ERCs
**What happened**
- [ERC-1613: move to Draft](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1802) (forshtat), merged. He is withdrawing old stagnant ERCs, but argues that although GSN has not been developed for years, it is still used on chains including Polygon, where Polymarket is among its users, so withdrawing it makes no sense. [Forum discussion](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-1613-gas-station-network/29051), 23:40.
- [ERC-7730: introduce schema semantic versioning](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1838) (forshtat), merged, setting an explicit versioning process for wallet clear-signing descriptors.
- [ERC-8343: Contract Deactivation](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1900) (rya-sge), submitted, a minimal interface for permanently deactivating a token contract and exposing that terminal state onchain; [forum](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8343-contract-deactivation/29049), 22:09.

**Why it matters**
- For dapps: GSN is the meta-transaction sponsorship scheme that predates 4337, and keeping it as a Draft gives apps still using it a standard to cite. 7730 versioning lets wallets parse transaction intent descriptors reliably.
