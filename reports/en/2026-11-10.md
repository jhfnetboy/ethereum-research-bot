## Key topics

### 1. EIP-8141: validation budget and fee semantics
**What happened**
- [EIPs PR #12644: Update EIP-8141: raise MAX_VERIFY_GAS to 300_000](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12644), opened at 08:58. The reasoning: 100k leaves out validation that real apps need. A Groth16 privacy pool spends 236,285 gas in its VERIFY frame (216,463 of it on the proof check), with Nethermind and ethrex agreeing to the gas unit; post-quantum signatures cost 94k to 276k. The benchmark ([ethresear.ch 26224](https://ethresear.ch/t/how-large-can-eip-8141s-validation-budget-be-a-mempool-dos-benchmark/26224)) measured caps from 100k to 500k: at equal failing gas per second the harm to a node is the same at every cap, and the cap only scales how much one peer can push. At about 100 failing transactions per second per peer, 300k keeps the added block-building delay within 25ms on local builder hardware. Nethermind already defaults to 300k for its own mempool. The PR is independent of #12301 (whether the value is a floor or a maximum).
- [EIPs PR #12642: Update EIP-8141: note that the blob fee can lower GASPRICE](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12642), opened at 07:45. The fee budget section says GASPRICE excludes blob fees, which reads as if blob fees never affect it. In fact, when `max_fee` does not cover the full tip, `max_priority_fee` is what is left after `required_max_fee`, and `required_max_fee` includes `blob_gas * blob_base_fee`, so a higher blob base fee lowers GASPRICE for the same transaction. This was found while checking Nethermind's frame tx `effectiveGasPrice` against the formula.

**Why it matters**
- 300k means native AA accounts can verify Groth16 proofs or PQ signatures directly onchain: privacy pools need no relayer and PQ accounts need no extra aggregation layer. It is the frame transaction parameter that matters most for accounts.
- The GASPRICE clarification affects contracts that read `tx.gasprice` and paymasters that issue fee refunds: in a frame transaction carrying blobs, the value may be lower than expected.
