## Key topics

### 1. Transaction privacy: encrypted mempool metadata
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: GhostPool, hiding identity critical metadata in encrypted mempool admission](https://ethresear.ch/t/ghostpool-hiding-identity-critical-metadata-in-encrypted-mempool-admission/24327) (wonj1012), 23:37: encrypted mempools hide contents before inclusion, but most designs still expose sender and nonce, or an equivalent public envelope signer, on admission because dynamic mempool checks rely on them.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: with contents encrypted but the sender public, observers can still identify and target by address, so hiding metadata is what makes encrypted mempools truly protective.
