## Key topics

### 1. Calls: ACDC #171
**What happened**
- [ACDC #171](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdc/171), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #2](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-2/): Glamsterdam targets June 2026; ePBS keeps trustless payments and explores non-validating staked builders; EIP-7688 forward compatible consensus structures, EIP-8080 exits via the consolidation queue and EIP-8061 higher churn limits are CFI; headliner talks for the next upgrade start in January, with [EIP-7805](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7805) FOCIL CFI and a possible headliner.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: FOCIL reaching CFI puts protocol level censorship resistance formally in line for the next upgrade.

### 2. Signatures and payments
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Post quantum signature aggregation, a folding approach](https://ethresear.ch/t/post-quantum-signature-aggregation-a-folding-approach/23639) (tcoratger), 19:31: by Thomas Coratger and Srinath Setty, on aggregating post-quantum signatures efficiently for attestations from hundreds of thousands of validators; [ethresear.ch: Trustless payments](https://ethresear.ch/t/trustless-payments/23635) (BrunoMazorra), 03:50: argues to keep the trustless payments part of EIP-7732.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: post-quantum signatures are large, and aggregation serves validators today and could batch verify user transaction signatures later.
