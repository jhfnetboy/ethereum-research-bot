## Key topics

### 1. Frame transactions in a post-quantum world
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Frame Type for PQ Sig and STARK Aggregation](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11772) (vbuterin), submitted, with a [Magicians thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8288-frame-type-for-pq-sig-and-stark-aggregation/28723) at 05:15; this is EIP-8288. Transactions can declare signatures and STARKs (for privacy or for L2s) as "dependencies", which the mempool and block builder can replace with a recursive STARK proving that they all exist.
- [New EIP: Recent Roots for Frame Transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11726) (soispoke), merged; this is EIP-8272.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts and privacy: post-quantum signatures are large and expensive to verify one by one onchain; aggregating them at the mempool level means each transaction only pays a share of one recursive proof. ZK proofs from privacy protocols can be aggregated the same way. Recent roots let a transaction reference a recent state root, one of the prerequisites for privacy pool withdrawals that do not depend on relayers.

### 2. Cleanup of AA related standards
**What happened**
- forshtat tidied up older proposals: [ERC-7562 to Review](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1804), submitted (ERC-7562 is the account abstraction validation rules for ERC-4337); [EIP-2542 to Withdrawn](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11773), submitted, as superseded by EIP-8141; [ERC-7766 to Withdrawn](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1805), submitted and merged; [ERC-2770 to Withdrawn](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1803), submitted.
- [ERC-1613 back to Draft](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1802), submitted: GSN has not been developed for years but is still used on some chains (including by Polymarket on Polygon), so the author did not think withdrawing it made sense.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and bundlers: ERC-7562 defines what 4337 user operations may do in the mempool, and moving to Review means the rules are stabilizing.

### 3. Other protocol work
**What happened**
- [EIP-8268: encode empty storage root as empty string](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11771) (CPerezz), submitted and merged. Previously an account with an empty storage trie still had to carry the 32-byte empty trie root in the BAL, while most accounts changed in a block are EOAs that only change balance; an empty string makes BALs smaller.
- [EIP-7807: reduce scope to non-headliner core](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11774) (etan-status), submitted and merged.
