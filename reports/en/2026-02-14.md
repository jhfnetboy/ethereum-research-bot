## Key topics

### 1. Transactions: logging ETH flows
**What happened**
- [EIP-7708: add ETH burn logs and improve spec consistency](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11311) (raxhvl), submitted and merged: ETH can be minted, transferred and burned, and every change in circulation or ownership should be logged, focusing on what changed rather than how; previously only transfers were logged correctly.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: wallets and indexers can see ETH movements without tracing internal calls, so smart account ETH history becomes as queryable as ERC-20.
