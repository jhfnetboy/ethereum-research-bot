## Key topics

### 1. Glamsterdam on Hoodi postponed: EIP-8037's gas assumptions
**What happened**
- [EF blog: Glamsterdam on Hoodi delayed](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/10/22/glamsterdam-hoodi-delay). ACDE #248 decided to postpone in order to investigate EIP-8037's interactions with mainnet contracts. The October 26 activation and the 200M gas limit schedule are both removed, no new date is set, and the post lists the client versions Hoodi node operators need to watch.
- [ChainSecurity: it has just been decided to delay the Hoodi fork because of security impacts identified in our blog post](https://x.com/chain_security/status/2113303061078671814), 23:14. They have already found 13 real-world examples and are in responsible disclosure with the affected projects.
- [ChainSecurity blog: Glamsterdam hard fork, when spent gas comes back](https://www.chainsecurity.com/blog/glamsterdam-gas/) (based on the specs as of October 13). Contracts defend against deliberately underfunded calls in three ways: checking the budget before the call, checking whether `gasleft()` is too low after it, or relying on the failure handler itself running out of gas and reverting. Under Glamsterdam:
  - EIP-8037 puts transaction gas above 16,777,216 into a state gas reservoir, which is forwarded in full and not subject to the 63/64 rule, while `gasleft()` reports only execution gas. A cold fresh-slot write costs 22,100 under Osaka but 12,100 execution gas plus 97,920 state gas under Glamsterdam; with the reservoir paying the large part, about 15,000 execution gas can finish recording a failure that used to revert.
  - Clearing a slot created earlier in the transaction refills state gas immediately, restoring up to 97,920 execution gas, which can push the remainder past a check's threshold.
  - When an intermediary contract `REVERT`s, its state gas accounting is restored and creation charges it already paid flow back to the caller.
  - With an empty reservoir, a cold fresh-slot write needs 110,020 execution gas, so a fixed budget that used to suffice may no longer be enough.
- The EF maintains a glamsterdam-gas-invariants list of the gas invariants EIP-8037/8038 break, and affected projects can contact glamsterdam@ethereum.org.

**Why it matters**
- This is the first time the state growth repricing has been stopped by a security issue before a testnet fork. Contract teams should replay any path that continues after a failed call and makes an irreversible decision under both gas schedules, prefer reverting the whole operation, and stop inferring failure causes from remaining gas alone. Operators should watch for new Hoodi client releases.

### 2. Frame transactions and post-quantum
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: commit to elided signature lengths](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12502) was merged (lightclient). Brett pointed out on Magicians that the raw bytes of empty-msg signatures are elided from the sig hash but still billed, so any relayer could pad an arbitrary signature and make the payer pay for it; after the fix, the payer's charge is fully determined by the sig hash.
- [tcoratger: first draft of EIP-8450, a post-quantum public key registry for validators](https://x.com/tcoratger/status/2113183187799015652), 15:18, with a [Magicians discussion thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8450-post-quantum-validator-key-registry/30046) at 14:47.
- [ethresear.ch: Paranoid EIP-8288, heterogeneous recursion with an authenticated class bank](https://ethresear.ch/t/paranoid-eip-8288-heterogeneous-recursion-with-an-authenticated-class-bank/26170), 15:29, letting small aggregates use a smaller proving relation.
- [ethresear.ch: how big is the sender's free option in encrypted mempools](https://ethresear.ch/t/reveal-optionality-in-encrypted-mempools-how-big-is-the-sender-s-free-option-and-a-post-quantum-way-to-remove-it/26169), 12:07, proposing Blind Simultaneous Release (BSR) to remove the reveal option.

**Why it matters**
- In a frame transaction, who pays and how much must be fully fixed by the signature, or sponsors get drained by relayers. Fixes like this are a precondition for native AA in Hegotá to be safe to use.
