## Key topics

### 1. Signatures: replay across verification surfaces
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Explicit Message Lanes for PQ and Hybrid Signatures, avoiding domain separation wormholes](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/explicit-message-lanes-for-pq-hybrid-signatures-avoiding-domain-separation-wormholes/27451) (pipavlo82), 11:18: as AA and post-quantum or hybrid signatures coexist, one cryptographically valid signature can be replayed or reinterpreted across ERC-1271, ERC-7913 and AA verification, a semantic rather than cryptographic failure.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: when smart accounts support several signature schemes, each signature must be bound to its purpose, or a valid signature can be abused elsewhere.
