## Highlights
1. **Nethermind 2.0.0**: archive nodes at about 2 TB and blocks processed 2 to 3 times faster.
2. **AA intents**: a new draft ERC defines AA-compatible solver contracts that support intent execution without modifying existing smart wallets.
3. **PBT verification**: EIP-8297 requires the three reserved bytes of the basic-data leaf to be zero, an issue found while building a cross-client conformance suite.

## Key topics

### 1. Clients and core development
**What happened**
- [Nethermind: client 2.0.0 is out](https://x.com/Nethermind/status/2102431395482103840), 23:14, with archive nodes dropping from about 30 TB to about 2 TB, a choice of how much history to keep, and blocks processing 2 to 3 times faster than the previous release on a fresh node.
- [fede_intern: most engineering teams working on core Ethereum will disappear over the next 3 years](https://x.com/fede_intern/status/2102376691884585308), 19:37, because there is no real money or much status in it and AI is quickly changing what it takes to implement a client (the post is long, and only the visible part is cited here).
- [EIP-8081: decisions from ACDC #187](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12364), merged.
- [EIP-8297: reserved basic-data bytes must be zero](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12366) (CPerezz), found while building a cross-client conformance suite for PBT.

**Why it matters**
- Cutting storage from 30 TB to 2 TB makes running an archive node realistic for individuals and helps decentralize RPC. The funding issue has been raised repeatedly and will affect Hegotá's delivery capacity.

### 2. Account abstraction and intents
**What happened**
- [New ERC: AA-compatible solver](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/2026) (Helkomine), where solver contracts orchestrate an account's existing execution and authorization capabilities to support intent-based execution without modifying existing smart wallets.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets, intent execution can be plugged in through external contracts, lowering integration costs.
