**What happened**
- [Add ERC: WeissChannels, cross chain execution for ERC-4337 accounts](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1486) (eil-docbot), submitted: single signature, atomic, non custodial cross chain execution for 4337 smart accounts, using ephemeral just in time state channels secured optimistically by L1.
