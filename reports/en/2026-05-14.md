## Key topics

### 1. Accounts: EIP-7851 becomes code-controlled
**What happened**
- [EIP-7851: use code-controlled delegation](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11668) (colinlyguo), submitted and merged. The PR explains that once control has moved into delegated wallet code, requiring the original EOA key again to disable residual ECDSA authority does not fit the "let the code decide" model, since after delegation the wallet may no longer use the old key at all.
- [EIP-7928: fix contract creation wording](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11667) (nerolation), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: the decision to disable the old key moves into the smart account's own logic, so wallets can switch off the old private key as part of flows like social recovery or moving to passkeys, without users signing with a key that may no longer be safe.

### 2. Privacy: paying identities instead of addresses
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Closing the Last UX Gap in Crypto, privacy-preserving payments to email, phone and social handles rather than a wallet address](https://ethresear.ch/t/closing-the-last-ux-gap-in-crypto-privacy-preserving-payments-to-email-phone-and-social-handles-rather-than-a-wallet-address/24874), 11:58 (0xjasonw), noting that sending still means asking for a 42-character address, checking the network and acquiring a gas token.
- [Eli Ben-Sasson: why strk20 gives better privacy than Zcash even with less privacy overall](https://x.com/EliBenSasson/status/2054911162802659508), 20:06; with Zcash only the two parties see a transaction, but shielded DeFi is hard.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: if private payments still require exchanging addresses first, the address itself leaks the relationship; private payments keyed to identities put usability and privacy in the same design.
