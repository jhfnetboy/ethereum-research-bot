## Key topics

### 1. Glamsterdam devnet and gas
**What happened**
- [terencechain: glamsterdam-devnet-7 is live](https://x.com/terencechain/status/2077070487981855053), 23:39. Consensus layer: EIP-7688 progressive containers, plus updated payload deadlines and builder lifecycle parameters; execution layer: EIP-2780 and 8037 move state-dependent charges out of intrinsic gas.
- [EIP-8038: remove the circular EIP-8037 dependency and reuse canonical gas symbols](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11927) (jochem-brouwer), submitted. 2780, 8037 and 8038 form the only dependency cycle among Glamsterdam EIPs; the PR makes 8038 a standalone regular gas repricing, without changing any charge value, condition, timing or refund behavior.

**Why it matters**
- For dapps and wallets: devnet-7 is the first devnet running the new gas rules together, so contracts and gas estimation can start testing on it.

### 2. Accounts and wallets
**What happened**
- [EIP-8130: allow initial actors to set an expiry at create and import](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11919) (chunter-cb), submitted. Previously time limits could only be set by a config change after deployment; now each initial actor can carry a `uint48 expiry` (0 means no expiry), committed in the address, so wallets can issue time-limited keys atomically when the account is created.
- [ethrex v21.0.0](https://x.com/ethrex_client/status/2077062775449280848), 23:08: mempool-based state prewarming makes mainnet block execution about 15% faster, and it ships initial Hegotá fork support with EIP-8141 frame transactions.
- [kassandraETH: 4337 UserOps, 7702 delegations and unlinked transaction activity should all be first-class citizens](https://x.com/kassandraETH/status/2076945786298843478), 15:23, and this should be the standard and default for Ethereum wallets now.
- [Magicians: timelock account recovery](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/timelock-account-recovery-a-trust-minimized-recovery-system-for-smart-accounts/29009), 18:06 (iyari), for ERC-4337/7579 smart accounts without guardians: starting a recovery requires staking `LockValue` ETH and waiting out a `LockTime` challenge window, during which the owner can challenge.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: session keys that carry an expiry from birth reduce the risk of leftover long-lived keys, and recovery shifts from trusting guardians to an economic game, an alternative to social recovery.
