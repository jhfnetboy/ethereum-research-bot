## Key topics

### 1. Two paths for native AA
**What happened**
- [Derek Chiang: the AA collaboration between 8130 and 8141 broke down last week](https://x.com/decentrek/status/2099490351337902392), 20:28, and Base and Ethereum will now implement different AA standards; he goes on to reflect on the collaboration and the future of the EVM (the post is long, and only the visible part is cited here). According to Ethereal news #40, Base plans to ship EIP-8130 keystore accounts later this year and says this does not block enabling EIP-8141 in future.
- [EIP-8130: align scope bits, POLICY/OPERATOR semantics and importAccount](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12333) (chunter-cb, merged the next day) and [symmetric two-track epochs, IncrementEpoch and the transient actor tier](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12334), both syncing the spec with Base's canonical implementation repository, spec changes only.
- [ERC-8286: modular accounts for frame transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/2008) (chiranjeev13) was merged.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and SDKs, two native AA transaction types will need support in the near term: 8130 (type 0x79) on Base and, later, 8141 on L1. Account migration and cross-chain consistency need planning ahead, and a modular account standard saves every wallet from reinventing the wheel.

### 2. The security boundary of sponsorship
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: public-mempool gas sponsorship needs escrow, a bond, or trust](https://ethresear.ch/t/public-mempool-gas-sponsorship-needs-escrow-a-bond-or-trust/25995), 18:07 (AnkushinDaniil). If you want a contract to pay gas for someone else's transaction and the public mempool to relay it with no locked money and no offchain trust, you cannot have all three; the post proves this, sorts every workaround into three categories, and explains why EIP-8141 reserves balance.
- [EIP-8141: pin the canonical paymaster withdrawal delay to reorg depth](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12330) (later closed).

**Why it matters**
- Paymaster operators have to accept some form of locked funds, which shapes the cost structure of sponsorship in the public mempool.
