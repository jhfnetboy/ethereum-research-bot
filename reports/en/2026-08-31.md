## Key topics

### 1. Ethlabs Week 10: account abstraction on L1
**What happened**
- Per Ethlabs Week 10 (Barnabé Monnot): AA generalizes every step of an account: choosing its own signature scheme (including passkeys and post-quantum), defining when its transactions are invalid (such as a daily spending cap), and having fees sponsored or paid in ERC-20s. 8130, proposed by Base's chunter and adapted to Base's needs, is like a turnkey solution; 8141 is like a platform with the raw ingredients for new signature schemes, new sponsorship methods and more. The piece notes a set of 8141 building blocks for privacy protocols proposed as separate EIPs for Hegotá, and that modular building blocks keep accounts portable even when Mainnet and Base run different rails. 8141 was SFI in the last 5 minutes of ACDE, but there is still room to fold in 8130's ideas.
- [EIP-8250: move TXPARAM_LEGACY_NONCE off 0x0C](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12219), merged; [EIP-8272: move RECENTROOTREFLOAD off 0xB5](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12220), merged.
- [EIP-8146: restructure and simplify](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12274) (nerolation), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: a platform-style 8141 means wallets or SDKs supply the default configuration themselves, while a turnkey 8130 puts it in the protocol. With L1 choosing 8141 and Base using 8130, account portability becomes central to the cross-chain wallet experience.

### 2. Faster blocks and finality
**What happened**
- Per Ethlabs Week 10: the working assumptions are that Hegotá is the last window for a long time to start reducing block times, and that starting is the hard part, a one-time cost to make the chain able to move from the current block time to a lower one; so the proposal is to do that work in Hegotá along with a first cut from 12 to 10 seconds, and this week the team is talking with the apps, infra and user groups that benefit.
- fradamt shipped a big update to the formalization of decoupled consensus, which moves finality out of block production: pseudocode for the available chain, the finality gadget, and a stabilization gadget that keeps the chain resilient when validators cannot agree; the finality gadget's accountable safety has been formally verified several times independently, and verification of the full protocol is ongoing and has already led to small protocol adjustments.
- ACDT #94 was held in the evening ([agenda](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/all-core-devs-testing-acdt-94-august-31-2026/29519)), with no notes to cite.

**Why it matters**
- For apps: 10-second slots shorten confirmation waits directly, and decoupled consensus stops faster finality and faster blocks from pulling against each other, which matters most for bridges and exchange deposit times.
