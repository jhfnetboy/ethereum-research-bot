### 1. Native AA: the frame transaction pool
**What happened**
- The EIP-8141 Frame Transaction breakout #2 met; per [Ethereal news #17](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-17/) it discussed [transaction pool strategies](https://hackmd.io/@matt/frame-mempool), with the agenda on [Magicians](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/headliner-breakout-eip-8141-frame-transaction-2-march-25-2026/28058).

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: this breakout was the last technical session before the March 26 ACDE decision on EIP-8141 for Hegotá, and safe pool handling of frame transactions was the key concern.

### 2. Transactions: onchain clear signing
**What happened**
- [Magicians: ERC Onchain Clear Signing](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-onchain-clear-signing/28068) (nail), 07:34: Kampela, which builds air gapped hardware signers, proposes making transactions human readable and verifiable on constrained devices without trusted third parties; today Ethereum relies on blind signing, where users must trust the requesting app's description.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: blind signing is behind several large thefts, and smart account calls are even harder to read, so putting readable descriptions onchain lets any wallet verify independently.
