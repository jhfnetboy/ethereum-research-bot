## Key topics

### 1. Calls: ACDC #170
**What happened**
- [ACDC #170](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdc/170), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #0](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-0/): Fusaka ships December 3 at 21:49 UTC with a [mainnet upgrade and incident response plan](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/blob/master/Fusaka/fusaka-mainnet-plan.md); an ePBS devnet is due late December or January; EIP-8045, excluding slashed validators from proposing, is CFI; FOCIL, EIP-8071 and EIP-8062 are out of Glamsterdam, with FOCIL CFI for the next upgrade; [soispoke: FOCIL is SFI](https://x.com/soispoke/status/1994055548937924681), 21:47.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: with FOCIL locked into the next upgrade, protocol level inclusion guarantees have a clear timeline.

### 2. Privacy: shielded pool design
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Shielded pools with onchain retroactive anonymity control](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/shielded-pools-with-on-chain-retroactive-anonymity-control/26735) (DamianStraszak), 12:17: a new mechanism to stop bad actors abusing shielded pools; [ethresear.ch: How CREATE2 prevents initial deposit leakage in privacy systems](https://ethresear.ch/t/how-create2-prevents-initial-deposit-leakage-in-privacy-systems/23545) (pememoni), 02:04; [Add EIP: Private ERC-20](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10842) (jakeolo), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: precomputing a deposit address with CREATE2 lets users enter a privacy system without revealing that they are depositing.
