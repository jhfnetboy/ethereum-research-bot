## Key topics

### 1. ACDE #237 and Hegotá
**What happened**
- ACDE #237 was held in the evening. Per Ethereal news #24: for Glamsterdam, the EIP-7904 compute gas cost increase is likely not needed once benchmarks confirm clients perform at the 100M gas per second target; Hegotá non-headliners proposed include EIP-8188 state tiering by write age, EIP-8182 private ETH and ERC-20 transfers, and EIP-4758 deactivate SELFDESTRUCT.
- [EIP-8081: add EIP-7709 and EIP-8025 as proposed for inclusion, after they were presented at the last ACDE and ACDC](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11720) (jsign), submitted; [add EIP-8253](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11721) and [add EIP-8188](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11722), submitted. EIP-8081 is the Hegotá hardfork meta EIP.
- [EIP-7773: move PFI above DFI](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11712), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: EIP-8182 reaching ACDE means protocol level private transfers entered hard fork candidate discussion for the first time, rather than living only in application level privacy pools.

### 2. Transactions and gas: 7702 authorizations and contract size
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: improve EIP-7702 authorization gas accounting](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11715) (rjl493456442), submitted, covering corner cases such as cancelling an authorization within the same transaction; state gas follows the net state change introduced by authorizations, except that clearing a pre-existing authorization is intentionally not refunded.
- [EIP-7954: raise contract code size to 64KiB](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11540) (Giulio2002), merged.
- [New EIP: Upgrade pre-Spurious-Dragon accounts](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11605) (jochem-brouwer), merged, an alternative to EIP-7610 that converts such accounts as if they had been created after Spurious Dragon; [EIP-8253: edit title](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11713), submitted and merged.
- [EIP-7928: clarify CREATE inclusion and storage no-op handling](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11717), submitted and merged; [EIP-7928: clarify the 7702 insufficient gas edge case](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11699), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: 7702 authorizations change an account's code, and charging by net change keeps users from paying state gas twice when they set and cancel an authorization in one transaction. A 64KiB limit lets full-featured smart wallets and account contracts avoid being split across several contracts.

### 3. State and data
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Storage Roots in Block Access Lists](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11716) (CPerezz), submitted as EIP-8268; [discussion thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8268-storage-roots-in-block-access-lists/28585), 15:29: every BAL entry that records state modifications carries the post-block storage trie root, so partially stateful nodes can verify the state root without holding full storage tries.
- [EIP-8256: Blob streaming](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8256-blob-streaming/28586), 20:36, enshrining rate-limited blob data pre-propagation.
