## Key topics

### 1. Accounts: passkeys and key authorizations
**What happened**
- [dankrad: are passkeys actually like a hardware wallet built into your phone? He is afraid not, though they are still useful for quickly creating hot wallets](https://x.com/dankrad/status/2061047552363917436), 18:30.
- [gakonst: roughly agrees, but still thinks the passkey is the best we have for balancing UX, onboarding and no dependency on third party custody or complex multi party key setups](https://x.com/gakonst/status/2061063926368866420), 19:35.
- [gakonst: Witnesses in Key Authorizations on Tempo let a user log in to an app with one passkey prompt that doubles as an auth challenge signature and an access key authorization](https://x.com/gakonst/status/2061066529672683962), 19:45.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: passkey signatures can be verified by smart accounts (or the EIP-7951 secp256r1 precompile), but their security depends on platform sync and the device rather than offline hardware; pairing them with session key style authorizations cuts down repeated signing prompts.

### 2. Privacy: Linked Accounts
**What happened**
- [gakonst: Linked Accounts is the more radical idea that may give good L1 privacy when combined with stealth addresses and native smart accounts, solving the stealth address sweep problem elegantly](https://x.com/gakonst/status/2061069380323016887), 19:56.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: funds received at stealth addresses are scattered across many one time addresses, and sweeping them costs gas and can reveal links; native account layer support for such structures is where privacy and AA meet.
