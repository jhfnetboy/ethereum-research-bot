## Key topics

### 1. The updated strawmap
**What happened**
- [Vitalik: the updated strawmap](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2073459000398463446), 00:28; two weeks earlier researchers met in Berlin to continue charting the protocol's long-term trajectory, following discussions with client teams in Svalbard in April.
- [nero_eth: what the strawmap focuses on](https://x.com/nero_eth/status/2073469309293150245), 01:09: UX (shorter slots, frame transactions, faster finality, privacy, code size increases, EVM features), scaling (zkEVMs, new types of state, limiting state growth, new node types such as VOPS, a new state trie, data scaling and gas limit increases) and PQ/Lean.
- [Barnabé: compared with the February original](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2073708226701328474), 16:59: quick slots move from Hegotá to J*, further slot time reductions from K* to L*, and decoupled consensus is slotted for I*; [his three-point summary](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2073693924061851850), 16:02: faster finality and blocks, more throughput, post-quantum.
- [dankrad: 3 to 4 years is very slow](https://x.com/dankrad/status/2073532475431887354), 05:20; a fully proven STF, scaling to Gigagas and finality in seconds are exciting, but the aim should be about a year, which he thinks is realistic now with LLMs.

**Why it matters**
- For AA and wallets: frame transactions are one of the UX lines, and together with the post-quantum migration they shape the account layer for the next few years. Pushing quick slots back means that, on this roadmap, Hegotá does not shorten block times.

### 2. Ethlabs Week 2
**What happened**
- Per Ethlabs Week 2 (Barnabé Monnot): using zk to accelerate async interop so assets minted anywhere get wide distribution; L2 to L1 links stay slow, with intents as a stopgap, while L1 to L2 links could see much lower latency with the Fast Confirmation Rule (FCR), which consensus clients now ship natively. PropAMMs came up in three calls, and the team aims for about 10 members near term.
