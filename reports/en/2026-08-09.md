## Key topics

### 1. leanDA
**What happened**
- [tcoratger: Ethereum uses DAS to scale blob data securely, but current DAS relies on KZG commitments that are not post-quantum secure](https://x.com/tcoratger/status/2086153234880594007), 01:11, introducing leanDA from ethresear, an "encode plus prove" post-quantum DAS construction. The original is [ethresear.ch: LeanDA design and benchmark](https://ethresear.ch/t/leanda-design-and-benchmark/25642) (posted August 6) by Long Meng, Benedikt Wagner, George Kadianakis and Francesco Risitano.

**Why it matters**
- Blobs are the main data source for L2s. The post-quantum migration is not only about signatures; DA commitments also need replacing, and leanDA offers a design that can be benchmarked.

### 2. Issuance and security
**What happened**
- [hasufl: charging passive holders to fund projects that invest in the ecosystem, like Lido, Aave and the DATs, is actually very good](https://x.com/hasufl/status/2086279352975597692), 09:32; together they fund client teams, several EF spinouts and the DeFi buildout, and the problem was never too much inflation but too little.
- [gakonst: crypto financial infrastructure has been operating under extreme paranoia about 24/7 bots trying to hack it](https://x.com/gakonst/status/2086159051621363913), 01:34, because it is open to anyone, open source and largely immutable.

**Why it matters**
- The EIP-8363 debate now includes an explicit case for higher issuance, so Hegotá's issuance question is far from settled.
