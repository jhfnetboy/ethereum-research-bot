## Key topics

### 1. Frame transactions: refunds, nonces and payment
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141 PR: explain gas refunds and block limits](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12537) (soispoke). Storage refunds lower what the payer pays, but the block counts execution gas before those refunds, a difference two paragraphs of the spec overlooked. The PR also explains that receipts record the gas charged to the payer.
- [EIP-8266 PR: select expiring-nonce mode under EIP-8250](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12538) (AnkushinDaniil). EIP-8250 replaces `nonce` with `nonce_keys` and `nonce_seq` and gives `MAX_NONCE_SEQ` a single-use meaning, so EIP-8266's sentinel value needs to line up.
- [EIP-8250 PR: exempt single-use sequences from data pricing](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12534) (nerolation).
- EIP-8209: [how a REVEAL transaction pays without a payer](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12539); [fix the max base fee bound and the REVEAL caller](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12532), merged.
- [EIP-7906: rename POST_TX to ASSERT](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12528), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For paymasters and wallets, "how much the user pays" and "how much block space it takes" become two different numbers under frame transactions, so fee estimation and block packing have to treat them separately. Expiring nonces and single-use keys let transactions carry a validity window and be sent in parallel, so wallet nonce management will need to be rewritten around these new fields.
