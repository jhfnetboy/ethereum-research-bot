## Key topics

### 1. Native post-quantum privacy
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Towards Native Post-Quantum Private ETH](https://ethresear.ch/t/towards-native-post-quantum-private-eth/25291), 22:53 (Pierre). The L1 strawmap lists L1-native private post-quantum transfers as a north star. Privacy solutions in production today rely on elliptic curve primitives or unsatisfying hardware assumptions; some argue protecting against harvest-now-decrypt-later (HNDL) attacks is enough, but the post notes that once a cryptographically relevant quantum computer exists, adversaries could carry out undetectable attacks with catastrophic losses, and recovery mechanisms such as turnstiles would require heavy social layer coordination. Using Zcash Sapling (about 620k shielded ZEC) as an example, it shows that delivering post-quantum shielded ETH takes far more than switching proof systems.
- [soispoke: EIP-8081, propose EIP-8272 for Hegotá](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11831), submitted, adding recent roots to the PFI list.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy protocols: commitments, note encryption and zero knowledge proofs in shielded pools all need quantum-resistant primitives, or else once quantum computers arrive an attacker might forge proofs and withdraw from nothing without anyone noticing, as the Zcash Orchard bug showed. Recent roots are part of protocol-level private transactions, and as a PFI they now compete with keyed nonces for a place in Hegotá.

### 2. Frame transactions and the state tree
**What happened**
- [EIP-7906: introduce a POST_TX frame mode as a requirement for the TXTRACE opcode](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11829) (forshtat), submitted and merged; [EIP-7906: introduce an additional TXDIFF opcode](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11830), submitted and merged.
- [EIP-7864: adjust to the PBT (partitioned binary tree) design](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11832) (vbuterin), submitted and merged, in line with the partitioned binary tree direction of EIP-8297.
- [EIP-2780: require EIP-7523 instead of EIP-161](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11828) (chfast), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and paymasters: reading the transaction's own state diff in a frame that runs after execution lets a contract check onchain what the transaction actually did, for example confirming asset changes match expectations before letting it through. This is a new capability frame transactions bring to safety checks and sponsorship.

### 3. Issuance policy
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Properties of issuance offsets and increased penalties under low/zero/negative issuance policies](https://ethresear.ch/t/properties-of-issuance-offsets-and-increased-penalties-under-low-zero-negative-issuance-policies/25292), 22:57 (aelowsson). Ethereum is debating an issuance reduction. If the base reward per increment goes toward zero, the incentive to perform attestation duties disappears; two ways to keep it are an "issuance offset" applied during epoch processing, or increased penalties. The post analyzes both and does not propose a specific reward curve.
