## Key topics

### 1. Glamsterdam compatibility: two alternatives and the EF timeline
**What happened**
- [EIPs PR #12638: Add EIP: Versioned Opcodes for Gas Repricing](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12638), opened at 02:40. New contracts must use the V2 opcode set, charged by the new EIP-8037/8038 gas schedule; deployed contracts keep the legacy schedule; `DELEGATECALL_V2` requires the target to be V2 as well; and the gas introspection opcodes `GAS` and `GASLIMIT` are left out of V2. The discussion started from [Leo Glisic's post](https://x.com/Leo_Glisic/status/2118884109393215604).
- [Magicians: EIP-8487 Separate execution credits and billing credits discussion thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8487-separate-execution-credits-and-billing-credits/30320), opened at 22:07, for the previous day's PR #12637.
- [EF Blog update of 11-09: Glamsterdam gas changes and deployed contracts](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/11/06/glamsterdam-8037-8038): since Friday's post the EF has received 4 reports pointing to deployed contracts that could break under EIP-8037/8038, and it is reviewing each one and contacting the protocols that may be affected. It has also heard a broader concern that changes affecting immutable contracts are not getting the scrutiny they deserve. The EF recommends that ACD wait until the audit findings are public and there has been time to review them before deciding whether to revise the specs or when to schedule the Hoodi upgrade. Several possible revisions are already being prototyped, with notes on which problems each solves and which other invariants it would break, to be presented at ACDE on 11-19.

**Why it matters**
- The two proposals take opposite approaches: EIP-8487 runs two counters inside one transaction, while Versioned Opcodes splits the gas schedule by contract version, so old contracts always see old prices and new contracts cannot introspect gas at all. What the latter means for accounts: smart accounts and 7702 delegate contracts deployed in the future would be V2, so wallet logic that relies on `gasleft()` (for example, billing a paymaster) would need rewriting, while existing delegate contracts keep running at old prices.
- **What to do**: if you maintain contracts or delegate implementations, check them against the [invariants repository](https://github.com/ethereum/glamsterdam-gas-invariants) and write to glamsterdam@ethereum.org if you find a problem. App developers are welcome at ACDT on 11-16.

### 2. Post-quantum: user accounts and consensus-layer priorities
**What happened**
- [Barnabé: ethereum:native will be a post-quantum store of value, and there is work to make it so](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2119764586870038684), 19:10. If alignment is reached, this may be everyone's most important delivery next year; Ethlabs has engaged on PQ user accounts for some time and will keep supporting it.
- [Ben Edgington: PQDC mentioned](https://x.com/benjaminion_xyz/status/2119698227272253505), 14:46. PQDC stands for Post-Quantum Decoupled Consensus, the post-Devcon revision of the faster finality roadmap: make the consensus layer quantum (and AI) safe first while keeping the route to faster finality open. "It's just a reprioritisation, but seems prudent at this time."
- [Ethlabs week 20 update post](https://x.com/ethlabs_org/status/2119704090318188926), 15:10.

**Why it matters**
- The PQ migration of user accounts and that of the consensus layer are separate tracks. For users, the account layer means switching signature schemes (which needs user action), while the consensus layer migration is invisible to them.

## Other updates
- [Magicians: checking that a deployed contract still runs the audited commit](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/checking-that-a-deployed-contract-still-runs-the-audited-commit/30318), 15:43.
