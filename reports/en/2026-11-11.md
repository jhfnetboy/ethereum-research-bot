# Ethereum Research Daily 2026-11-11

Keywords: Glamsterdam, ETH SoV, post-quantum, EIP-8483, EIP-8037

Summary: Glamsterdam is still on hold. The EIP-8037 and EIP-8038 gas repricing could break deployed contracts, the Hoodi fork has been postponed, and the next steps are ACDT on November 16 and ACDE on November 19. Vitalik argues that ETH should be a store of value first rather than a fee-burning token. The post-quantum migration keeps moving, and EIP-8483 has been proposed for Hegotá.

## Key topics

### Glamsterdam and EIP-8037/8038
- [Ethlabs timeline: tracking the Glamsterdam discussion](https://ethlabs.org/writings/tracking-glamsterdam.html) Records the cancelled Hoodi fork, audit progress, and the distinction between pure repricing and changes to gas accounting.
- [EF Blog: Glamsterdam on Hoodi is postponed](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/10/22/glamsterdam-hoodi-delay) The Hoodi fork planned for October 26 was cancelled on October 22.
- [EF Blog: the invariants EIP-8037/8038 will break](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/11/06/glamsterdam-8037-8038) A list of 8 invariants published under Marius van der Wijden's name; the November 9 update says 4 reports involving deployed contracts have been received.
- [glamsterdam-gas-invariants self-check repository](https://github.com/ethereum/glamsterdam-gas-invariants) For contract developers to check the invariants EIP-8037/8038 may break.
- [Ethlabs: willing to consider spec changes if the impact is too wide](https://x.com/ethlabs_org/status/2120082366685454428) Separates pure repricing from changes to gas accounting; Glamsterdam aims to raise the block gas limit from 60M to 200M.
- [EIP-8488: Versioned Opcodes for Gas Repricing](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12638) Proposes that new contracts use V2 opcodes without GAS/GASLIMIT, while old contracts keep the old schedule.
- [Magicians discussion of EIP-8488](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8488-versioned-opcodes-for-gas-repricing/30332) Sam Wilson and others point out compatibility problems with CREATE/DELEGATECALL and with factory and proxy contracts.
- [Dankrad: never breaking existing contracts is not workable](https://x.com/dankrad/status/2119332003610776025) One of the core developer positions on Glamsterdam's compatibility constraints.
- [Marius van der Wijden: the team is investigating with auditors](https://x.com/vdWijden/status/2119328227365707994) A reply to criticism of the 8037/8038 compatibility issue.
- [Kassandra: suggests making most opcodes cheaper](https://x.com/kassandraETH/status/2119443330673054009) Argues for handling repricing by lowering prices rather than raising them.
- [ACDC #191: only a short 8037/8038 update on November 12](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2291) Per the EF timeline, the two EIPs are not on the formal agenda.
- [ACDT #103: audit results presented on November 16](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2299) Open Q&A with the auditors and the EF; app developers are encouraged to join.
- [nero_eth: a reminder to join ACDT on November 16](https://x.com/nero_eth/status/2120070435836485826) Related to the ACDT #103 presentation of audit results.
- [ACDE #249: next steps discussed on November 19](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2285) Discussion of the audit results and what follows, with no decision committed in advance.

### ETH as a store of value
- [Vitalik's main post: ETH should be a store of value first](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2119401255919604064) Puts SoV ahead of the fee-burning narrative while keeping the EIP-1559 burn.
- [Vitalik: the fee-burn valuation floor was never solid](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2119401300723118086) Notes that most of the fees burned in 2021 to 2023 came from crypto-internal use cases.
- [Vitalik: optimizing for fee burning conflicts with decentralization](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2119401338375430542) Argues that optimizing burn to support today's market cap would require large R&D spending and heavily interventionist governance.
- [Vitalik: grounding SoV in post-quantum security, privacy and censorship resistance](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2119401376753262713) Mentions concrete work such as formal verification, ossification, Kohaku, FOCIL and lightweight nodes.
- [Francesco: the technical and monetary narratives are not either-or](https://x.com/fradamt/status/2119516974825005214) Sees Vitalik's framing as close to a consensus the community had largely already reached.
- [Barnabé: fast slots and SoV do not conflict](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2119410779350872138) A follow-up to Vitalik's store-of-value argument.
- [Barnabé: once the post-quantum transition is done, no asset has better properties](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2120127994710876490) Ties the SoV argument to the post-quantum roadmap.
- [Julian Ma: ETH is the sanctuary asset in the AI age](https://x.com/_julianma/status/2120198500919161258) A follow-up to this week's SoV narrative.
- [binji previews a long and controversial reply](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2119416671924273169) The Ethlabs researcher plans to respond to Vitalik's SoV argument.

### The post-quantum roadmap
- [EIP-8483: Post-Quantum Material Registration](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12609) Lets a validator's withdrawal address register 32 bytes of post-quantum material, preparing for a future PQ migration fork.
- [jtraglia asks to discuss including EIP-8483 in Hegotá at ACDC #191](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2291) The comment was posted at 03:54 on 2026-11-11.
- [EIP-8479: Post-Quantum PeerDAS and Variable-Size Blobs](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12604) A proposal for a post-quantum data layer and variable-size blobs.
- [Ethereal news #46: this week's EIP roundup](https://x.com/EtherealnewsHQ/status/2120254586766426457) Includes EIP-8479 and other post-quantum proposals.
- [Vitalik: the current PQ roadmap brings efficiency gains of its own](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2119694446136844637) Mentions fewer validators on the hot path, erasure-coded P2P, and faster finality with PQ signature aggregation.
- [ethresear.ch: measuring PQ signature aggregation in leanVM](https://ethresear.ch/t/folding-vs-full-verifier-recursion-for-pq-signature-aggregation-in-leanvm-measur/26227) Hash-based folding did not beat leanVM's full-verifier recursion.
- [PQ Transaction Signatures Breakout #18](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2298) Agenda in ethereum/pm #2298; the call is on November 11 at 21:00.
