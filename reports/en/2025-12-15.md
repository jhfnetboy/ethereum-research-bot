## Key topics

### 1. Calls: ACDT #64
**What happened**
- [ACDT #64](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdt/064), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #3](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-3/): before a 75M gas limit, clients must build and test getBlobsV3 and a max blobs flag, ready for BPO3 releases; a proposal has clients rather than validators set the gas limit; ePBS builders in Glamsterdam are non-validating with 1 ETH staked; Hegotá's consensus upgrade is renamed Heze because Heka is not in the IAU star catalog.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: raising the gas limit from 60M to 75M grows block capacity directly and lowers fees under congestion.

### 2. Accounts: modules and delegation
**What happened**
- [ERC-7780: add module types 8, 9 and 10](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1355) (leekt), merged after submission on November 18, 2025; [EIP-7819: fix inconsistent SETDELEGATE opcode naming](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10922) (lisenokdonbassenok), submitted; [ERC-7674: move to Review](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1404) (Amxx), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: ERC-7780 extends module categories for ERC-7579 modular smart accounts, so wallets can compose validation, execution, hooks and more by type.
