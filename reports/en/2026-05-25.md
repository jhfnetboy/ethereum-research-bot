## Key topics

### 1. Privacy primitives: EIP-8272
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Recent Roots for Frame Transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11726) (soispoke), submitted as EIP-8272.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy and accounts: private transactions usually prove against a recent root of a commitment tree, and if validation had to read mutable storage, nodes could not safely prevalidate them in the mempool; with recent roots kept in a system contract that frame transactions can reference, private transactions can propagate in the public mempool like ordinary ones, without relayers.

### 2. Censorship resistance and consensus
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Formalizing FOCIL in Lean 4](https://ethresear.ch/t/formalizing-focil-in-lean-4/24950), 21:56 (rahul). FOCIL (EIP-7805) is the censorship resistance headliner for Hegotá, and its selling point is "1-out-of-N honesty among IL committee members": one non-equivocating member listing a transaction is enough to force its inclusion; the post examines that claim in Lean 4.
- [EIP-8243: Batching Attestations at Source discussion thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8243-batching-attestations-at-source/28606), 21:17 (raul), letting operators running several validators in the same slot committee publish one pre-aggregated attestation instead of N.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: FOCIL is what keeps private transactions from being censored, so formally verifying its inclusion property tests the premise that private transactions will get onchain.
