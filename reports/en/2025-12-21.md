## Key topics

### 1. Transaction safety: address poisoning
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Exploring dynamic casing checksums to mitigate address poisoning](https://ethresear.ch/t/exploring-dynamic-casing-checksums-to-mitigate-address-poisoning/23716) (Mitsuhamizu), 08:59: poisoning works because people check only prefix and suffix, so the receiver shares a short lived code and the sender's wallet derives a dynamic casing fingerprint from Keccak256(address plus code) that fails for a pasted lookalike.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: it needs no protocol change and ships in wallets alone, a more practical defense than educating users.
