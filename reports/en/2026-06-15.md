## Key topics

### 1. ACDT #83
**What happened**
- Per Ethereal news #28, ACDT #83 (21:00, with execution and consensus layer breakouts): separate builder deposit and exit contracts are the correct design (EIP-8282 builder execution requests), with an estimated 1 to 2 months of security review for the new system contracts.
- RPC Standards #28 was held at 22:00, with no notes to cite.

**Why it matters**
- For the Glamsterdam timeline: the system contract review is on the critical path, so the mainnet date depends on when the review finishes.

### 2. Private token standards
**What happened**
- [Magicians: pERC20 Private Token Standard](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/perc20-private-token-standard/28796), 22:17 (Cyimon), EIP number 8287, described as a fungible token standard for the EVM that is private by default; this revision mainly adds an extended ERC-20 approve to the earlier design. [Cross-posted on ethresear.ch](https://ethresear.ch/t/perc20-private-token-standard-draft-approve-extended/25200), 22:44.
- [Eli Ben-Sasson: Starknet's privacy pool TVL is about $250K, with 25 tokens in the same pool](https://x.com/EliBenSasson/status/2066495464367685720), 19:18; DeFi integration is at a very early stage.

**Why it matters**
- For private tokens: approve is the basis of DeFi composability, and private tokens without allowances are hard to plug into DEXs and lending. Many tokens sharing one pool also means a larger anonymity set.
