## Key topics

### 1. Gas: in-protocol futures
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: On in-protocol gas futures](https://ethresear.ch/t/on-in-protocol-gas-futures/23698) (Julian), 17:16: fee volatility hurts UX, forcing users to hold buffers for spikes and businesses to factor it into deployment choices, so the post explores gas futures inside the protocol.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: predictable fees let apps and sponsors quote fixed prices to users, a precondition for sustainable AA sponsorship.
