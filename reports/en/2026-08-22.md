## Key topics

### 1. The native AA design split goes public
**What happened**
- [lightclient: half of 8130 is application-layer specifics that should not be defined in the protocol](https://x.com/lightclients/status/2090862398928552188), 01:03. 8141 supports permissionless innovation and does not force wallets and users into specific use patterns, which matters because we cannot assume we will always be around to tweak the account system.
- [Hudson Jameson: remember the account abstraction wars, what came of that](https://x.com/hudsonjameson/status/2090877651024036330), 02:04. Some compromise EIPs were accepted, but smart wallets using AA do not seem to have taken off; did the fighting wash away the best EIPs, or is it an adoption issue.
- [EIP-8141: account block execution gas before refund (EIP-7778)](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12226) (AnkushinDaniil), submitted. EIP-8141 adopts EIP-8037's two-dimensional gas model and activates in the same fork as EIP-7778; EIP-8037 accounts the block execution dimension before the storage refund, so a refund lowers the payer's cost without freeing the block capacity the transaction occupied, the DoS bound EIP-7778 exists to keep.
- [EIP-8141: fix the calldata floor to use the EIP-7976 uniform token count](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12209), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For wallet teams: 8141 hands validation logic to account code, which is flexible but leaves wallets to implement it, while 8130 fixes authenticators and the Keystore at the protocol layer, which is simpler to integrate but limits the patterns. Next week's ACDE and AA breakout will take up the split.

### 2. Issuance and the sync committee
**What happened**
- [dapplion: just wrote an EIP to reduce ETH issuance by 3% by removing the sync committee](https://x.com/dapplion/status/2091132020013375982), 18:54, with [PR 12228](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12228). It removes the sync committee, its aggregate, subnets, domains and rewards, and the Altair light client protocol built on it; `SYNC_REWARD_WEIGHT` is removed and not redistributed, so consensus issuance falls by 2/64.

**Why it matters**
- Light clients and bridges that rely on the sync committee would need replacements, the main cost of the proposal.
