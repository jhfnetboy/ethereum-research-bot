## Key topics

### 1. ACDE #234
**What happened**
- [ACDE #234](https://forkcast.org/calls/acde/234), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #19](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-19/): Glamsterdam's bal-devnet-3 launched, epbs-devnet-1 has several issues under investigation, and glamsterdam-devnet-0 goes next week if epbs-devnet-1 is stable, otherwise separate execution and consensus devnets continue; Hegotá non headliner proposals opened with native account abstraction discussion and a breakout planned; history retention minimums (2 weeks to 1 year) and an SFI definition clarification were discussed.

### 2. Accounts: Wallet Title Deeds
**What happened**
- [Add ERC: Wallet Title Deeds](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1658) (benaadams), submitted; [Magicians thread ERC-8221](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8221-wallet-title-deeds/28182), 08:56; per Ethereal news #19 it was presented at ACDE #234 as NFT controlled smart accounts.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: binding control to an NFT lets a whole account be transferred or inherited with its assets and history intact.
