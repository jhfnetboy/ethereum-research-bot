## Key topics

### 1. Post-quantum privacy and accounts
**What happened**
- [ERC-8441: hybrid post-quantum stealth address scheme](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/2059) was merged (namnc). It defines ERC-5564 `schemeId` 3, whose payment secret combines an ML-KEM-768 encapsulation with a secp256k1 ECDH secret, so an adversary who later gains a quantum computer still cannot tell from the announcement log who was paid. Spending stays on secp256k1, and the deployed ERC-5564 announcer and ERC-6538 registry are used unchanged.
- [tcoratger: a first working version of leanXMSS in Lean4, available in EthLib](https://x.com/tcoratger/status/2115575401720533221), 05:44; it is the XMSS implementation planned for EIP-8450.
- [ethresear.ch: STVA, secure transaction verification and authorization](https://ethresear.ch/t/secure-transaction-verification-and-authorization-stva/26192), 01:39; [Magicians: scoped capability tokens for STVA](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/scoped-capability-tokens-for-stva/30124), 12:02.
- [Magicians: contracts can't read events, a gap that keeps coming up in ERC drafts](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/contracts-cant-read-events-a-gap-that-keeps-coming-up-in-erc-drafts/30120), 01:28; since mid-September at least four ERC threads hit the same problem, where a fact lives only in an event but a contract needs it.

**Why it matters**
- The threat to stealth addresses is "harvest now, decrypt later": today's announcement logs could be traced back by a future quantum computer. ERC-8441 swaps only the key exchange used for privacy, so wallets can protect receiving privacy first without changing spending logic.

### 2. ACDC #190
**What happened**
- [ACDC #190](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdc/190/) (as summarized by Ethereal news #45): Alex Stokes is stepping down as ACDC facilitator, Ben Edgington takes over as lead and Will Corcoran as deputy. Hegotá consensus layer scoping is finished: EIP-8015 (remove deposit and eth1data fields) is SFI; EIP-8334, EIP-8383, EIP-8148 and EIP-8411 are CFI; EIP-8205 (withdrawal credentials preregistration) is DFI, to be solved through a post-quantum deposit contract.
- [Ben Edgington: my first speaking appearance on ACD, December 15, 2017](https://x.com/benjaminion_xyz/status/2115833952737116301), 22:51.
- [Barnabé quotes "our ambition is to see a faster Ethereum with shorter slot times and post-quantum security"](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2115842379257172121), 23:25, and discusses where a new consensus client should put its emphasis.

**Why it matters**
- With the consensus layer scope set, Hegotá's open questions sit mainly on the execution layer (frame transactions, keyed nonces, SETCODEFROM and so on).
