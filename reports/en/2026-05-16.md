## Key topics

### 1. Privacy and compliance
**What happened**
- [ERC-8262: Zero-Knowledge Compliance Oracle](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8262-zero-knowledge-compliance-oracle/28543), 07:32 (Hydepwns), with [ERCs #1747](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1747) submitted: users prove at transaction time that they meet a jurisdiction's requirements, with no identity revealed, no TEE and no trusted third party; a regulator checks the proof and never sees the trade.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: compliance is the biggest obstacle to institutions using privacy tools; ERC-8262 replaces disclosure with proofs, giving tools like privacy pools a path to regulated users.
