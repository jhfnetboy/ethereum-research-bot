## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: EIP-8130 and EIP-8141
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Account Abstraction by Account Configuration](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11186) (chunter-cb), merged: accounts configure signing keys with account specified verifiers, onchain contracts defining signature algorithms, validated by the protocol.
- [VitalikButerin: account abstraction has been discussed since EIP-86 in early 2016, and EIP-8141 is the omnibus that solves every remaining AA problem](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2027774090627715377), 22:53.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: EIP-8130 puts validation into configurable verifier contracts, which is easier to check statically in the mempool than EIP-8141's general frames, giving native AA two distinct routes.

### 2. Accounts: EOA key deactivation
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Private Key Deactivation Aware ecRecover](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11288) (colinlyguo), merged: immutable ERC-20s with ERC-2612 permit such as DAI rely on the ecRecover precompile, so a deactivated EOA key could still authorize via old signatures unless ecRecover knows about deactivation; [EIP-7851: move to Draft](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11316) (colinlyguo), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: disabling transaction signing is not enough; offline signatures such as permit must also stop working, or an EIP-7702 account that changed verification could still have tokens moved by its old key.
