## Key topics

### 1. Gas accounting
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: apply the EIP-7976 calldata floor to block-level gas accounting](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11908), merged. Its reproduction test measures the calldata floor budget under Amsterdam rules with `block_gas_limit = 2 * TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT = 33,554,432`.

**Why it matters**
- For block builders: if the calldata floor applies only per transaction and not in block-level accounting, a block's actual size can exceed the design bound; the fix aligns 8037 with 7976's bound.
