## Key topics

### 1. Block building and speed
**What happened**
- [binji: with MPBC, multiple builders help fill the same block](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2100223651723579885), 21:01, where before one builder picked the transactions and a missed one had to wait. According to Ethereal news #40, Blockspace Forum's MPBC is live, expanding the most valuable block with transactions from multiple builders, initially append only.
- [binji: get EIP-8198 into Hegotá, "Ethereum, but faster"](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2099916096362910109), 00:39; [terencechain: Prysm's Hegotá tier list](https://x.com/terencechain/status/2099910131760996708), 00:16.
- [ethresear.ch: when data binds execution, a dynamic simulation of EIP-7999's multidimensional fee market](https://ethresear.ch/t/when-data-binds-execution-dynamic-simulation-of-eip-7999-s-multidimensional-fee-market/26018), 21:58 (M1kuW1ll), adding block-level demand variation and the fee update rules to the earlier equilibrium analysis.

**Why it matters**
- Several builders contributing to one block reduces the impact of a single builder missing or censoring transactions, in line with FOCIL. Multidimensional fees would change how execution gas and blobs are priced relative to each other, affecting L2 costs.
