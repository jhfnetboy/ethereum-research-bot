## Key topics

### 1. The Lean Ethereum fork roadmap and lean transactions
**What happened**
- [EF: the Lean Ethereum plan, fork by fork](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2118150051574747523), posted at 08:15. The strawmap targets completing Lean Ethereum by 2029. H* is the last fork without lean features, I* ships leanVM and lean transactions, J* adds lean attestations, DA sampling and PCS, and everything is done by L*.
- [Thomas Coratger: Mission 2028 is built around formal verification, and EthLib is moving forward](https://x.com/tcoratger/status/2118264044930932851), 15:47.

**Why it matters**
- Lean transactions mean a new generation of account signatures and transaction formats, on the same track as EIP-8141 frame transactions and PQ signature aggregation (EIP-8288). For wallet and contract account developers, I* is the fork to prepare for.

**What to watch**
- The concrete EIP for lean transactions, and whether it merges with frame transactions or runs alongside them.

### 2. Accounts and signature aggregation: EIP-7851, EIP-8288
**What happened**
- [EIPs PR #12630: EIP-7851 syncs related clarifications from EIP-8298 before archiving](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12630), merged at 22:26. EIP-7851 is "code-controlled EOA delegation" and EIP-8298 is the SETCODEFROM code reuse instruction; the title says the sync happens "before archiving".
- [EIPs PR #12628: EIP-8288 aggregates only currently executable transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12628), opened at 15:06. It recommends that aggregators prove only transactions executable at the current head, and that builders prefer recursive inputs that cover more dependencies. The trigger was core developer concern that the many mempool transactions below the base fee would produce aggregate proofs that are mostly thrown away.
- [EIPs PR #12599: EIP-8450 addresses post-merge review comments](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12599), merged at 14:43 (EIP-8450: Post-Quantum Consensus Key Registry, by kevaundray).

**Why it matters**
- Since 7702 there have been several competing proposals for how an EOA upgrades to a contract account and how that is revoked. EIP-7851 heading to the archive shows the discussion converging on a few designs. EIP-8288 decides how PQ signatures get aggregated in the mempool, which directly affects transaction fees for future PQ accounts.

### 3. Privacy tooling: interoperable key derivation, the Nulo wallet, Privacy Pools v2
**What happened**
- [Kassandra publishes the seed phrase derivation scheme for Tornado Cash and Privacy Pools V1](https://x.com/kassandraETH/status/2118037514061750720), 00:47. This is why kohaku-cli and the Ambire wallet find the same privacy protocol records from the same seed phrase. [kohaku-cli has been updated to v0.1.5](https://x.com/kassandraETH/status/2118310181993885759), 18:51, and she calls for using privacy primitives in almost every L1 action.
- [Aztec: the Nulo wallet launches on Chrome and Firefox](https://x.com/aztecnetwork/status/2118039435493376125), 00:55. It is open source and shows the privacy status of each transfer before sending; ETH and USDC can be bridged into Aztec through Shield.
- [EF: Privacy Pools v2 lets users do privately anything a public address can do](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2118210449292329203), 12:15.
- [Magicians: Stealth ABBE over BLS12-381, broadcasting private contract state through 4844 blobs](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/stealth-abbe-over-bls12-381-zero-metadata-broadcast-of-private-contracts-encrypted-state-via-eip-4844-blobs/30282), opened at 03:58.

**Why it matters**
- When privacy protocol records can be recovered across wallets, users are not locked into a single wallet. To use it today: kohaku-cli works with Tornado Cash, Railgun and Privacy Pools; Ambire shares the Tornado Cash and Privacy Pools V1 derivation scheme with kohaku-cli; on Aztec, use Nulo.
