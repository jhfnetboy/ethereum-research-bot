## Key topics

### 1. Signatures, batches and blobs in EIP-8141
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: pin the P256 signature canonicality (low-s)](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11984) (svlachakis), submitted and merged. The SECP256K1 path already pins the recovery id and EIP-2 low-s, but P256 had no equivalent rule, and the P256VERIFY precompile (EIP-7951) accepts both `s` and `n − s`; a signature entry's raw bytes are part of the transaction's identity, so leaving this open allows malleability.
- [EIP-8141: statically exclude VERIFY frames from atomic batches](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11987) (lightclient), submitted and merged, clarifying the wording of the ban on VERIFY in batches.
- [EIP-8141: specify blob support for frame transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11985) (svlachakis), submitted. The fee side already existed (`blob_fees` in `tx_fee`, `TXPARAM` `0x05`/`0x07`); missing were inclusion validity, block-level blob gas accounting and the networking form. Most of it is EIP-4844/EIP-7594 by reference, with the payer as the blob-fee payer.
- [EIP-8141: specify EIP-3529 storage refund accounting for frames](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11940), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For passkey wallets: with a unique P256 encoding, one signature cannot produce two transaction hashes, making tracking and replacing transactions more reliable. For rollups and data submitters: frame transactions can carry blobs with the payer paying blob fees, so the sponsorship model also applies to data submission.

### 2. Glamsterdam gas parameters
**What happened**
- [EIP-2780: fold transfer log cost into TX_VALUE_COST](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11997) (misilva73), submitted and merged: `TRANSFER_LOG_COST` (1756) is removed and `TX_VALUE_COST` goes from 4244 to 6000, covering both the recipient and the EIP-7708 transfer log; the cost of a create transaction with value changes, and specs and tests need updating.
- [EIP-8038: document parameter provenance and drop EIP-7904 from requires](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11996), submitted and merged, with no gas value changes; [EIP-8037: rename "regular gas" to "execution gas"](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11998), submitted and merged.
- [EIP-8015: also remove start index and BeaconState fields](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11910) (etan-status), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For wallet gas estimation: the intrinsic cost of value-bearing transactions comes from these parameters, so estimates need the new values once Glamsterdam ships.
