## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: cleaning up EIP-8141
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: frames cleanup](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11621) (lightclient), submitted: after two months of frequent changes the spec's coherence had declined; this mainly improves readability while fixing a few issues found along the way, such as adding a skipped status for frames.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: EIP-8141 is already CFI for Hegotá, and client teams need a coherent spec to implement; the cleanup signals a move from design debate to implementation.

### 2. Transactions and gas
**What happened**
- [EIP-7904: convert to Informational (no compute repricing needed)](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11622) (misilva73), submitted: rerunning the analysis with EIP-7928 BAL optimizations stable, every examined compute operation and precompile meets the 100 Mgas/s target at its current gas cost.
- [EIP-8038: align with EIP-8037 and clarify spec and rationale](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11623) (misilva73), submitted.
- [EIP-7708: add gas costs for system emitted transfer and burn logs](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11627) (danceratopz), submitted: TRANSFER_LOG_COST = 1,756 (LOG3 equivalent) and BURN_LOG_COST = 1,381 (LOG2 equivalent); [EIP-7708: use EIP-8246 to simplify](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11634) (marioevz), submitted; [EIP-2780: add missing log costs for CREATE with endowment](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11586) (danceratopz), merged.
- [EIP-7773: CFI EIP-8246 for Glamsterdam](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11625) (chfast), submitted; [EIP-8246: Move to Review](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11626) (chfast), submitted; [EIP-8131: add empirical impact report](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11628) (nerolation), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For users: no compute price increases means Glamsterdam's gas changes focus on state and access costs; with EIP-8246 removing the burn corner case, EIP-7708's ETH log rules get simpler too.
