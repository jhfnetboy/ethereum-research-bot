## Key topics

### 1. Protocol-level public goods funding
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Validator Redirected Revenue](https://ethresear.ch/t/validator-redirected-revenue/25248), 15:34 (clesaege). The post argues Ethereum is stuck in a coordination failure: everyone benefits from shared improvements but no one wants to pay when others can free-ride, creating a persistent deadweight loss. It proposes two protocol-level changes for a validator-driven funding mechanism, where validators configure a percentage of staking rewards to be redirected.

**Why it matters**
- For the ecosystem: this ties directly into the debate over core dev team funding in the previous days; implemented at the protocol level, funding would shift from a foundation to voluntary configuration by stakers.
