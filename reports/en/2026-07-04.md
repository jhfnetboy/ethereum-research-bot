## Key topics

### 1. Standardizing PropAMMs
**What happened**
- [Magicians: ERC-8324 Priority Update Registry (PUR)](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8324-priority-update-registry-pur/28921), 03:14 (quintus), with the [ERC PR](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1852) submitted. PUR is a single shared contract for PropAMMs on the EVM and similar uses; an instance has been live on Ethereum since May, handling almost all PropAMM volume across multiple builders, and the author hopes to standardize it across EVM chains.
- [EIP-7928: SSTORE access cost check](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11854) (nerolation), merged, clarifying how the stipend and cold state are tracked.

**Why it matters**
- PropAMMs rely on market makers updating quotes with priority inside a block; a shared registry gives builders and market makers one interface, which bears directly on L1 execution quality and block building.
