## Key topics

### 1. Upgrades: Fusaka goes live
**What happened**
- [VitalikButerin: fusaka](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/1996335948137926695), 04:49; [TimBeiko: Fusaka has finalized](https://x.com/TimBeiko/status/1996340447116194012), 05:07; [VitalikButerin: PeerDAS in Fusaka is sharding, with consensus on blocks without any node seeing all the data](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/1996342458234605895), 05:15; [raulvk: Fusaka kicked in at epoch 411392 and all 128 column subnets are distributing data](https://x.com/raulvk/status/1996369557494128903), 07:02.
- [ethereumfndn: an ongoing issue with the Prysm consensus client on mainnet, and Prysm operators must reconfigure](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/1996467022482591877), 13:30.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: Fusaka also brings the EIP-7825 per transaction gas cap and the EIP-7918 blob fee floor, changing fee models for wallets and L2s.

### 2. Calls: ACDE #225
**What happened**
- [ACDE #225](https://forkcast.org/calls/acde/225), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #1](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-1/): Prysm will ship a fix and a post mortem next week; for Glamsterdam, EIP-7708 ETH transfer logs, EIP-7778 block gas accounting without refunds, EIP-7843 SLOTNUM, EIP-8024 SWAPN and friends and EIP-8070 sparse blobpool are CFI; EIP-7819 SETDELEGATE, EIP-7932 secondary signature algorithms, EIP-8030 P256 transactions, EIP-7619 Falcon512, EIP-6404 and EIP-6466 are declined; ACDC will decide whether FOCIL is set SFI early.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: with SETDELEGATE, secondary signature algorithms and P256 transactions declined, Glamsterdam brings no new protocol level account or signature features, pushing AA work to Hegotá.
