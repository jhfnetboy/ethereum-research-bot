## Key topics

### 1. Transactions: FOCIL censorship resistance
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Hegotá headliner proposal FOCIL (EIP-7805)](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-proposal-focil-eip-7805/27604), 21:33; [soispoke: FOCIL is now officially proposed as a Hegotá headliner](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2016158081877471448), 21:35; [_julianma: FOCIL is a zero to one network primitive that can also be a building block for more](https://x.com/_julianma/status/2016163188266484055), 21:55.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: FOCIL lets multiple validators force transactions in, so no single builder can censor users, which is also the base for encrypted mempool and native AA transactions to land reliably.

### 2. Scaling and proving
**What happened**
- [VitalikButerin: the scaling hierarchy is computation over data over state, since computation can be parallelized, aided by builder hints or replaced by a proof](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2015965780727496907), 08:51; [ethresear.ch: Measuring Per-Opcode Proving Time](https://ethresear.ch/t/measuring-per-opcode-proving-time/23955) (linoscope), 19:32.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: per opcode proving time is the basis for repricing gas in the zkEVM era, and slow to prove operations may cost more.
