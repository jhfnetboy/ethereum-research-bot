## Key topics

### 1. Privacy and native AA: the three gates for frame transactions
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Frame Transactions and the Three Gates to Privacy](https://ethresear.ch/t/frame-transactions-and-the-three-gates-to-privacy/24666), 20:06 (Nero_eth): taking EIP-8141 as given, it shows frame transactions could eliminate relayers from privacy protocols, since withdrawers from protocols like Tornado Cash and Railgun use fresh addresses with no ETH for gas and today rely on centralized, censorable relayers; but public mempool admission rules, FOCIL enforcement and the statelessness roadmap each draw different boundaries around supported transactions, and the post maps where they intersect and conflict for privacy transactions.
- [EIP-7701: move to Withdrawn](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11528) (forshtat), submitted, with the withdrawal reason "Superseded by EIP-8141".

**Why it matters**
- For privacy and accounts: frame transactions let contract logic validate and pay for gas, so a private withdrawal can pay its own way; but if the mempool or FOCIL will not accept such transactions they still never reach the chain, so protocol rules must leave a path for them. Withdrawing EIP-7701 shows native AA has consolidated around EIP-8141.
