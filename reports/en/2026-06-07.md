## Key topics

### 1. Multi-block access list warming
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Multi-block Access List Warming](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11776) (nerolation), submitted; this is EIP-8289. It adds a 256-block rolling warm access multiset, committed via a SHA-256 SMT root in the block header, so the EIP-2929 access list carries across blocks: a median gas saving of about 14% per block, for about 10 MB of extra node state.

**Why it matters**
- For users and apps: popular contracts and addresses are accessed again and again in consecutive blocks, and cold access fees are part of each transaction's gas, so warming across blocks makes frequent interactions cheaper.
