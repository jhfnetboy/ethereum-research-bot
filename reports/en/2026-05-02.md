## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: EIP-8130 payer signature replay
**What happened**
- [EIP-8130: prevent cross sender payer signature replay](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11591) (pochenai), submitted: the payer signature hash includes a from field, but the spec did not say what goes there on the EOA path where from is empty; encoded as empty, two EOAs with identical transaction data would share a payer hash, letting the second reuse the signature and drain the payer's gas deposit, so the recovered sender address must be substituted.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: sponsored transactions are only safe if signatures bind to a specific sender; a bug like this on mainnet would cost paymasters funds directly.

### 2. Transactions and gas: gas overflow in multidimensional fee markets
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Gas overflow for multidimensional fee markets](https://ethresear.ch/t/gas-overflow-for-multidimensional-fee-markets/24766), 00:42: analyzes "universal overflow" for EIP-7999 as a minimal way to keep the legacy scalar retained gas pattern around CALL while avoiding pricing all EVM gas at the most expensive resource; it credits Vitalik for the main idea.

**Why it matters**
- For users: multidimensional fees give execution, state growth, calldata and blobs separate base fees, but contracts that reserve gas as a single number must keep working, and overflow is the key to that compatibility.

### 3. Account and payment standards
**What happened**
- [Add ERC: Keyed Transfer With Authorization](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1715) (BrianBland), submitted: ERC-20 transfer authorizations split nonces into (uint192 nonceKey, uint64 nonceSequence), mirroring ERC-4337's semi abstracted encoding, so repeat payments to one counterparty use one storage slot per (payer, key).
- [Add ERC: Generic Relayer Architecture for Smart Accounts](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1716) (seansing), submitted, moved over from the EIPs repo.
- [Add ERC: x402 Payment Receipt Binding for ERC-8004 Agents](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1717) (hilarl), submitted.
