## Key topics

### 1. The CROPS list for Hegotá and post-quantum migration
**What happened**
- [soispoke: CROPS Hegotá wishlist](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2069051519878463989), 20:34: censorship resistance via FOCIL (EIP-7805, SFI'd); open source, with everything open source in protocol upgrades; privacy via frame transactions (EIP-8141, CFI'd), keyed nonces (EIP-8250) and recent roots (EIP-8272); plus security items.
- [tcoratger: recommending riva_labs' overview of Ethereum's post-quantum migration at the execution layer](https://x.com/tcoratger/status/2069098911420006585), 23:43, a thorough look at the design space and tradeoffs for post-quantum transaction signatures on Ethereum.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy and accounts: frame transactions let accounts define their own validation, keyed nonces let privacy pool withdrawals avoid sharing one nonce sequence, and recent roots let a transaction reference a recent state root to prove membership; together they allow withdrawing from a privacy pool without relayers. The execution layer post-quantum migration likewise hinges on whether accounts can switch signature schemes, which also relies on the validation flexibility of frame transactions.

### 2. Gas specs and scaling
**What happened**
- [EIP-8038: reverse the SSTORE clear refund to prevent net-profitable round trips](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11823) (misilva73), submitted and merged. When a slot is returned to its original value within one transaction (for example `x → 0 → x`), the `STORAGE_CLEAR_REFUND` granted on the clear was never reversed on restore, leaving a net refund of 9,380 gas despite no net state change.
- [EIP-7778: block gas accounting without refunds, with a rule for where refunds go](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11824), submitted.
- [ethresear.ch: A native zkEVM scales bandwidth, not just execution](https://ethresear.ch/t/a-native-zkevm-scales-bandwidth-not-just-execution/25254), 00:29 (mikeneuder). A native zkEVM is usually pitched as execution scaling, with validators downloading and verifying a proof for the whole block; the post argues it changes the bandwidth constraint as well.

**Why it matters**
- For contracts: left unfixed, the SSTORE refund bug could be used to extract gas under the new Glamsterdam pricing.
