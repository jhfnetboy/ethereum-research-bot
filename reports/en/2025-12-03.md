## Key topics

### 1. Signatures: the EIP-7932 registry
**What happened**
- [EIP-7932: remove the RLP transaction wrapper and add a pythonic registry definition](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10754) (SirSpudlington), merged after submission on November 8, 2025; [EIP-6404: remove the copy of the EIP-7932 algorithm registry](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10865) (SirSpudlington), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: EIP-7932 stops defining its own transaction wrapper and becomes a pure signature algorithm registry for other formats to reuse, easing post-quantum signatures.

### 2. Accounts: associated accounts
**What happened**
- [Magicians: ERC-8092 Associated Accounts](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8092-associated-accounts/26858) (katzman), 00:51: a standard way for two addresses to publicly declare, prove and revoke a relationship, across chains and platforms.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: account links help usability, but public declarations tie together identities that were separate, a trade off for users.
