## Key topics

### 1. Calls: ACDT #62
**What happened**
- [ACDT #62](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdt/062), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #1](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-1/): the next safe gas limit is 75M, as block receipts may exceed the message cap near 83M; 100M becomes possible after [EIP-7975](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7975) eth/70 partial block receipt lists, maybe in late January or February; [EIP-7872](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7872), a max blob flag for local builders, should land before the next BPO phase; switching the Engine API to SSZ needs benchmarks.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: the path is now clear from 60M to 75M to 100M, easing fee pressure for users.

### 2. Transactions: anti MEV
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: MEVless Protocol](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10855) (Lawliet-Chan), submitted, with [Magicians: EIP-8099 MEVless](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8099-mevless-an-anti-mev-protocol/26800), 13:09: targets sandwich and frontrunning attacks by constraining ordering so producers cannot see transaction contents while ordering.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: like encrypted mempools, hiding contents during ordering is the root fix for shielding users from MEV.
