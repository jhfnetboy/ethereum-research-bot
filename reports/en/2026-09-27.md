### 1. Ethereum after Hegotá
**What happened**
- [Vitalik: the cryptographic world computer](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2104160561374343176), 17:45, an attempt to express concisely the true meaning of basically everything planned starting from the fork after Hegotá: Ethereum is really not just a blockchain anymore, but a hybrid (the post is long, and only the visible part is cited here).
- [Vitalik: Lean consensus will be amazing](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2104186531829445083), 19:28.
- [barnabemonnot: we can probably get 8-second slots and 2 to 3 minute finality by early 2028 if faster slots get done in Hegotá](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2104243489508577382), 23:15; [binji: EIP-8198 can start shortening slots in 2027](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2104246153705652708), 23:25.

**Why it matters**
- This is the overview for the 2027 to 2028 roadmap: accounts (frames, aggregation), privacy, proofs and consensus evolving together. Developers can plan long term around faster confirmation, native AA and mempool aggregation.

### 2. Post-quantum account migration
**What happened**
- [New EIP: quantum freeze and account recovery](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12383) (colinlyguo), proposing, after community discussion, a minimal framework for freezing legacy ECDSA authorization and recovering unmigrated accounts.
- [StarkWare: Google significantly lowered its estimate for breaking elliptic-curve crypto and used ZK to prove its circuit works without revealing it](https://x.com/StarkWareLtd/status/2104180087340122610), 19:03.

**Why it matters**
- Freezing legacy signatures would affect every EOA. For wallets, moving to accounts that can switch signature schemes (frame transactions or smart accounts) is the safest preparation.
