## Key topics

### 1. Native AA and transaction formats
**What happened**
- [EIP-8130: adjust the account config interface, small clean up](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11428) (chunter-cb), submitted and merged.
- [EIP-6404: update the secp256k1 algorithm to 0x00 from 0xFF](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11430) (SirSpudlington), submitted; the PR notes it breaks EIP-6404 test cases that cannot be fixed until PR 11357 merges.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: EIP-8130 is the native AA track running alongside EIP-8141, and its account config interface sets how wallets declare their verification; the algorithm ID governs how signature schemes are encoded in SSZ transactions, leaving room for post-quantum schemes later.

### 2. Gas: EIP-8037
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: align with EIP-7928 gas validation phases](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11414) (nerolation), merged at 15:29 after being submitted on March 16.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: once state gas matches the BAL gas validation phases, clients can decide uniformly whether a transaction can pay before parallel execution.
