## Key topics

### 1. Scaling: blob streaming
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Scaling the DA layer with Blob Streaming](https://ethresear.ch/t/scaling-the-da-layer-with-blob-streaming/24202) (QED, fradamt, Julian), 21:30: alongside the existing critical path blob lane, enshrine continuous, sampled pre propagation of blob data as a protocol mechanism; pre propagation already happens via the blobpool but with weak guarantees.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: L2 costs come mostly from blobs, so more blob capacity with steadier propagation keeps pushing L2 fees down.
