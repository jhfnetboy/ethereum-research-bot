## Key topics

### 1. Privacy: local models and EIP-8288
**What happened**
- [Vitalik: a self-experiment](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2106537633056969024), 07:11. The goal is personalized recommendations from his health and travel data without leaking private information to frontier models, starting with a local model.
- [EIP-8288 PR: bind block_deps_hash to the aggregation circuit's digest](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12417) (AnkushinDaniil).

**Why it matters**
- This points the same way as zkAPI two days earlier: using AI and paid services without handing over identity and data. EIP-8288 gives privacy apps an aggregated verification path for their proofs at the protocol level, and these PRs are filling in the block-level commitment details.
