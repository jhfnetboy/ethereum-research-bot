## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: atomic batching
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: add support for atomic batching](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11395) (derekchiang), submitted: a SENDER_ATOMIC mode where consecutive SENDER_ATOMIC frames revert together, and an explicit rule that SENDER and SENDER_ATOMIC frames run only once sender_approved and payer_approved are both true.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: multi step flows such as approve plus swap either all succeed or all revert, leaving no half finished approval, a protocol native version of what EIP-7702 batching offers.
