## Key topics

### 1. Security: the eth.limo DNS attack
**What happened**
- [Vitalik: eth.limo warned him of an attack on their DNS registrar, so do not visit vitalik.eth.limo or other .eth.limo pages until they confirm things are back to normal; the blog can be read via IPFS directly](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2045413438659416215), 15:05.

**Why it matters**
- For users: ENS content lives onchain and on IPFS, but most people reach it through a DNS gateway, which makes the gateway's registrar a single point of failure; like the CowSwap frontend incident days earlier, it shows the entry layer matters for security too.

### 2. Native AA and gas
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: mix the transaction type into the sighash](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11544) (derekchiang), submitted.
- [EIP-7906: add the missing slot_key parameter and name FRAMEPARAM as a similar opcode](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11546) (forshtat), submitted and merged.
- [EIP-8037: update state gas refund rules for account SELFDESTRUCT](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11543) (abc-123-c), submitted.
- [potuz_eth: doesn't understand the timeline's complaints about Tempo's privacy solution, which is practical for its users, and its approach to AA is also good](https://x.com/potuz_eth/status/2045241416432152700), 03:42.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: including the transaction type in the signature hash stops a signature from being replayed across transaction types, a basic safety requirement for any new type.
