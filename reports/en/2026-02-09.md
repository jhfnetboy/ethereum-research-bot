## Key topics

### 1. Accounts: signatures after key deactivation
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Private Key Deactivation Aware ecRecover](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11288) (colinlyguo), submitted, with the [Magicians thread EIP-8151](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8151-account-code-restricted-ecrecover/27690), 13:24: per the [EIP-7851 discussion](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-7851-deactivate-reactivate-a-delegated-eoas-key/22344/14), immutable ERC-20s implementing ERC-2612 permit, such as DAI, rely on ecRecover, so after an EOA delegates and deactivates its key, the old key could still move assets through permit.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: EIP-7702 upgrades EOAs into smart accounts, but the upgrade is incomplete while the key can still sign permits; making ecRecover aware of deactivation is a precondition for moving EOAs to post-quantum or multisig schemes.

### 2. Gas: state cost and compute repricing
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: add quantization, reservoir model, and refine gas accounting](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11292) (misilva73), submitted: cost_per_state_byte is quantized to its top 4 significant bits to avoid frequent small changes as the gas limit moves, and a reservoir model replaces revert based TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT enforcement by splitting execution gas into gas_left and state_gas_reservoir.
- [EIP-7904: new methodology and preliminary numbers](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11244) (misilva73), merged after submission on February 3: renamed from General Repricing to Compute Gas Cost Increase, it now raises costs only for 13 compute operations and precompiles below 60 Mgas/s.
- [ethresear.ch: Solving Spam, State Bloat, and Fee Volatility with a Single Parameter](https://ethresear.ch/t/solving-spam-state-bloat-and-fee-volatility-with-a-single-parameter/24088) (lavender), 14:08: proposes a minimum base fee for execution gas, since after scaling demand often sits below the 50% target and the base fee converges to zero.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: pricing state and compute separately and raising only slow operations is the safety precondition for higher Glamsterdam gas limits, while a base fee floor sets the cost of spam after scaling.
