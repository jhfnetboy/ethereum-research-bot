## Key topics

### 1. Frame transactions and the gas limit
**What happened**
- [EIP-8077: define source and nonce for frame transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12139) (nerolation), submitted, clarifying the interaction with EIP-8141 and EIP-8250. EIP-8077 is the eth protocol extension that announces transactions together with their nonce.
- [EIP-8261: convert to an informational gas limit schedule recommendation](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12140) (barnabasbusa), merged. There are no consensus changes and no enforcement; the EIP-1559 ±1/1024 elasticity rule remains the only gas limit validity rule, and blocks above or below the schedule stay valid. The schedule is an optional `GAS_LIMIT_SCHEDULE` field in the CL `config.yaml`, mirroring EIP-7892's `BLOB_SCHEDULE`. [A clarification of epoch-based activation](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12142) (nflaig) was merged the same day.

**Why it matters**
- Frame transactions separate sender and payer and can use keyed nonces, so a node deduplicating and ordering by nonce at the P2P layer needs to know whose nonce it is. With the gas limit schedule only a recommendation, each fork's gas limit increase is still reached by validators voting it up gradually.
