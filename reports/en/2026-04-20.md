### 2. Transactions and gas
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: per-dimension block gas limit check](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11536) (misilva73), merged, submitted on April 17; [EIP-8037: SSTORE restoration refund rollback semantics](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11548) (spencer-tb), merged.
- [EIP-7708: add CREATE and CREATE2 to the transfer log list](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11474) (s1na), merged.
- [EIP-7928: change the block access list index to uint32](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11550) (nerolation), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For users: EIP-7708 makes ETH transfers emit logs like ERC-20 transfers, so wallets and explorers can see every ETH movement, contract creation included, without tracing internal calls.
