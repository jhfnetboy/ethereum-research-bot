## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: a signatures list
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: add a signatures list to the outer tx](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11481) (lightclient), merged. The PR explains that an important goal of 8141 is forward compatibility with signature aggregation, especially for PQ signatures; those signatures are large and aggregation may become very important as users migrate, so a `signatures` field is added to the outer transaction object.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: post-quantum signatures can be dozens of times larger than ECDSA, and carrying them in every transaction would raise block space use and gas costs sharply; with signatures in their own list they can later be aggregated at the block level, keeping user costs manageable when accounts migrate to post-quantum signatures.
