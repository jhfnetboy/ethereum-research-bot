## Key topics

### 1. Calls: ACDT #65
**What happened**
- [ACDT #65](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdt/065), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #7](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-7/): BPO3 is not urgent at current usage and needs getBlobV3 plus partial cell messages in every consensus client; [bal-devnet-2](https://notes.ethereum.org/@ethpandaops/bal-devnet-2) targets late January with EIP-8024 SWAPN, DUPN and EXCHANGE, EIP-7708 ETH transfers emitting a log, EIP-7778 block gas accounting without refunds and maybe EIP-7843 SLOTNUM; bal-devnet-3 plans four more; ePBS discussed minimum bid increments, withdrawals and builder onboarding at the fork.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions and gas: EIP-7708 lets wallets see ETH transfers without tracing internal calls, and EIP-7778 stops refunds from affecting block gas accounting.

### 2. Transactions: a post-quantum mempool
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Recursive STARK based bandwidth efficient mempool](https://ethresear.ch/t/recursive-stark-based-bandwidth-efficient-mempool/23838) (vbuterin), 17:15: many user objects must be broadcast to a final builder, and with elliptic curve SNARKs unusable after quantum, the post aggregates them with recursive STARKs to save bandwidth.
- [Magicians: Explicit Message Lanes for AA plus PQ, preventing domain separation wormholes](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/explicit-message-lanes-for-aa-pq-preventing-domain-separation-wormholes/27419), 11:29.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: post-quantum signatures are large, and native AA transactions each carrying one would swamp P2P bandwidth, so recursive aggregation is key to viable post-quantum accounts.
