## Key topics

### 1. State growth in the 8130 Keystore
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Keystore expired actor pruning](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12240) (chunter-cb), submitted and marked WIP; EIP-8396 per Ethereal news #37. It has two parts: a layout enshrined at launch, fixing an actor's `actor_config`, `policy_manager` and `policy_commitment` as a co-located, stride-aligned 3-word record with a `CLEANABLE` marker bit in the `actor_config` reserved region; and a best-effort reaper that, each block, walks a bounded number of record heads from a deterministic block-seeded position and clears expired actors.
- AA breakout #2 was held in the evening (per Ethereal news #37), with no notes to cite.

**Why it matters**
- Session keys, subscriptions and just-in-time grants are all short-lived actors; if they stay in state forever, state grows faster the more native AA is used. Pruning requires fixing the storage layout from day one, one of the places where 8130's protocol-layer design is heavier than 8141's.

### 2. ePBS and gas
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: ePBS, distilled](https://ethresear.ch/t/epbs-distilled/25800), 00:28 (mikeneuder), linking the Gloas builder, validator and beacon chain specs; the [author's post](https://x.com/mikeneuder/status/2091943286554657224) at 00:38 says it shows ePBS in its final form and how MEV markets will evolve once it goes live.
- [EIP-8037: decouple SYSTEM_MAX_SSTORES_PER_CALL from request bounds](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12238) (chfast), submitted and merged. The value was justified as matching `MAX_WITHDRAWAL_REQUESTS_PER_BLOCK`, but that constant counts records dequeued, dequeuing writes no record slots, and the system contract added by EIP-8282 has a larger per-block bound; the value is now stated as a margin with an explicit re-derivation condition.
- [EIP-8246: a concrete example of L2 burn usage](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12237) (chfast), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- System calls pay no gas, so their storage write bound sets their worst-case cost under 8037 state gas, and constants like this need a derivation that can be rechecked.
