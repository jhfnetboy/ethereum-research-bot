## Key topics

### 1. The short-term path to native privacy
**What happened**
- [Vitalik: short-term things being done to shift Ethereum toward native privacy: AA plus FOCIL (making privacy protocol txs, among others, first class with strong inclusion guarantees), keyed nonces, and access layer work (Kohaku, private reads)](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2056992175318073477), 13:55.
- [soispoke: Hegota is for hardness, with censorship resistance from FOCIL and native, trustless privacy from frame transactions and keyed nonces](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2057025495003250859), 16:07.
- [EIP-8266: Expiring Nonces for Frame Transactions discussion thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8266-expiring-nonces-for-frame-transactions/28575), 12:56.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy and accounts: this splits privacy into three layers: whether a transaction gets included (FOCIL), whether a contract account can originate it without a relayer (AA and keyed nonces), and whether a wallet leaks information when reading chain data (the access layer). Account abstraction is a precondition for privacy here, not a separate feature.

### 2. Transactions and gas
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: calldata floor accounting alignment and call-frame refill clarification](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11706) (misilva73), submitted: the EIP-7623 calldata floor is applied at the transaction level to align block and transaction gas accounting, the floor and refund interaction and receipt accounting are clarified, and an "Integration with EIP-7778" subsection is added.
- [nero_eth: Glamsterdam ships EIP-7928 block-level access lists, bringing parallelization to the EVM](https://x.com/nero_eth/status/2057027628788269457), 16:16.
- [growthepie: yesterday more addresses on Ethereum L1 triggered smart wallet upgrades than some chains have active](https://x.com/growthepie_eth/status/2057078639020101792), 19:39.

**Why it matters**
- For users: how the calldata floor is accounted decides what data heavy transactions finally pay and how much a refund can offset, which wallets need for gas estimation.
