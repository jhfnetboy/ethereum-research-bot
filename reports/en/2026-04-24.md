## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: EIP-8141 mempool rules
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: relax mempool rules to not require a specific factory](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11567) (derekchiang), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: if the public mempool only accepts accounts deployed by one factory, protocol rules limit which account implementations wallets can choose; relaxing this lets smart accounts with different implementations use the public mempool.
