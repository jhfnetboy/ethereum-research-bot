## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: the stateless view and history
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Frame Transactions Through a Statelessness Lens](https://ethresear.ch/t/frame-transactions-through-a-statelessness-lens/24538) (CPerezz), 04:57: once zkEVMs replace re-execution with proof verification, nodes no longer need about 280 GB of state to take part in consensus, leaving only builders, RPC providers and searchers holding state; the post examines how AA and EIP-8141 mempool strategies work for such nodes.
- [lightclients: account abstraction has an 11 year history in Ethereum; the link tree he started in 2020 is now updated through 2026 with help from fvictorio_nan](https://x.com/lightclients/status/2038650711328817311), 23:13.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: a frame transaction's VERIFY reads state while stateless nodes have none, so whether the mempool can relay smart account transactions depends on bounding the state validation needs and shipping it with the transaction.

### 2. Transaction and account ERCs
**What happened**
- [Add ERC: Smart Batching](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1638) (oxshaman), submitted: a composable batch encoding for smart accounts where each parameter declares how to get its value at execution time (literal, staticcall or balance query) and what constraints it must meet, enabling dynamic transactions.
- [Add EIP: Block-scoped transient storage opcodes](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11467) (monokh), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: parameters resolved at execution time let unknown amounts such as swap output flow into the next step, cutting failures and extra transactions from bad estimates.
