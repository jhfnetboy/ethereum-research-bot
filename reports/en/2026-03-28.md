## Key topics

### 1. Native AA and PQ: ephemeral key rotation
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Native Ephemeral Key Rotation via Frame Transactions](https://ethresear.ch/t/native-ephemeral-key-rotation-via-frame-transactions/24526) (ABaiocchi), 22:49: following an earlier post on quantum safety through ephemeral key pairs and account abstraction, it builds key rotation into the EIP-8141 frame transaction format with no separate AA layer, decoupling signing authority from onchain identity via a singleton registry and adding a rotation frame through native bundling.
- [Magicians: EIP-8215 Hash-Committed Account (HCA)](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8215-hash-committed-account-hca/28094), 17:26, proposing a new account form for a structural gap in post-quantum migration.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: rotating keys every transaction means an exposed ECDSA key cannot be exploited by a quantum computer in time; with EIP-8141 now CFI, such designs have a real path to deployment.

### 2. Consensus: shorter slots
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Quick Slots](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11426) (CarlBeek), merged after submission on March 19; [EIP-8198: use SLOT_SCHEDULE for slot time dependent constants](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11458) (smartprogrammer93), submitted; [adietrichs: a push for shorter slot times, parameterizing slot time and improving it continuously like the gas limit](https://x.com/adietrichs/status/2037643132280385968), 04:29.

**Why it matters**
- For transactions: shorter slots mean shorter waits for inclusion, and parameterization avoids designing each reduction as a separate fork feature.
