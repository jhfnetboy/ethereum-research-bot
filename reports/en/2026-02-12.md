## Key topics

### 1. Hegotá headliners: AA and encrypted mempools
**What happened**
- [ACDE #230](https://forkcast.org/calls/acde/230), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #11](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-11/): execution layer headliner candidates are the [LUCID encrypted transaction pool](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-lucid-encrypted-mempool/27658) (presented, breakout February 18), [EIP-8141 frame transaction](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-proposal-frame-transaction/27618) (execution spec implementation and tests in progress), [EIP-8105 universal enshrined encrypted mempool](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-proposal-eip-8105-universal-enshrined-encrypted-mempool-eem/27448) and [EIP-7807 SSZ execution blocks](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-proposal-ssz-execution-blocks/27619); client teams will write up preferences, including no execution layer headliner, for the next ACDE with a decision at the one after.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts and transactions: two of the four candidates are encrypted mempools and one is native AA, so the Hegotá pick decides whether Ethereum next prioritizes frontrunning privacy or account experience.

### 2. Glamsterdam and networking
**What happened**
- ACDE #230 set bal-devnet-3 scope: eth_simulateV1, [EIP-8037](https://forkcast.org/eips/8037/) state creation cost, [EIP-7954](https://forkcast.org/eips/7954/) contract size and EIP-7975 eth/70 partial block receipt lists (needed for a gas limit above 83M); eth/71 joins a later devnet.
- [Add EIP: eth/71 Block Access List Exchange](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11307) (nerolation), submitted, with the [Magicians thread EIP-8159](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8159-eth-71-block-access-list-exchange/27725), 14:09; [EIP-8024: switch to branchless normalization and extend EXCHANGE](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11306) (frangio), submitted.
- [ladislaus0x: the first L1-zkEVM breakout call happened, with progress across eight workstreams](https://x.com/ladislaus0x/status/2021961042348163181), 21:54.
