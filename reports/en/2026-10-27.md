## Key topics

### 1. Privacy and app-layer input
**What happened**
- [ethbeat: we reviewed 16 privacy protocols to understand what they actually hide, who they hide it from, and what is still visible](https://x.com/ethbeat_org/status/2115090782549905429), 21:38, publishing the full methodology for feedback.
- [EF: who shapes what ships is often a question of input](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2115103383426384313), 22:28; the App Enablement team is reaching out to protocols, especially those less active on ACD calls.
- [Magicians: app-layer input on EIPs and the roadmap, staking and oracles](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/app-layer-input-on-eips-and-the-roadmap-staking-and-oracles/30104), 22:06 (CPSTL). Teams were asked how they would design for censorship resistance, open source, privacy and security (CROPS) without business or technical limits, what stops them, and which single change would help most; the post lists the EIPs they named, grouped by theme.
- [ethresear.ch: Proof of One, one-person-one-identity polling with encrypted ballots](https://ethresear.ch/t/proof-of-one-one-person-one-identity-polling-with-encrypted-ballots-design-and-reference-code-requesting-critical-review/26184), 14:42, with reference code and a request for review.

**Why it matters**
- Users and wallets can only compare privacy protocols if "what is hidden" is measured the same way. App teams affected by EIPs, as with this gas repricing, now have a direct feedback channel.

### 2. Account and gas specs
**What happened**
- [EIP-7906: say where storage-kept nonce changes appear](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12562) was merged.
- [EIP-7928: specify BAL integer encoding](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12571) was merged.
- [ChainSecurity: Glamsterdam got pushed back, but Devcon is next week](https://x.com/chain_security/status/2115091885660463242), 21:42; bring your call paths and they will walk through what the upgrade changes for your protocol on site.
- [lightclient: it will probably take an exploit of similar severity on Ethereum before people take funding the protocol seriously](https://x.com/lightclients/status/2115104431494799656), 22:32.

**Why it matters**
- Nonces do not only live in the account field and can also be kept in storage; spelling out where those changes appear in assertions tells wallets which field to check.
