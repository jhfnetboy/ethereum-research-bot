### 2. Gas: closing calldata floor gaps
**What happened**
- [EIP-7981: add access list cost to the EIP-7623 floor formula](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11141) (nerolation), submitted and merged: access list data cost joins intrinsic gas and its tokens join the floor calculation, preventing a bypass of EIP-7623 calldata floor pricing.
- [Add EIP: Add Auth Data to EIP-7623 Floor](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11148) (nerolation), submitted, with the [EIP-8131 thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8131-add-auth-data-to-eip-7623-floor/27567), 17:01.
- [Add ERC: Wallet Call Gas Limit Override Capability](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1485) (ajhodges), submitted: adds gasLimitOverride to EIP-5792 so apps can set per call gas limits in wallet_sendCalls.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: access lists and EIP-7702 authorization lists both carry data, and without counting toward the floor they are cheap data channels; closing them makes worst case block size truly bounded.
