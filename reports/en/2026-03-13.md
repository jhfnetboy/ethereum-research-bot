### 2. Native AA: EIP-8141 approval bits
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: simplify approval bits so accounts do not have to manipulate them](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11404) (derekchiang), submitted: accounts can pass the TXPARAM result directly to APPROVE to set the approval scope, without ruling out other logic.
- [EIP-8141: fix bit indices](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11402) (fjl), submitted and merged; [EIP-7906: use opcodes instead of transaction types or precompiles](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11403) (forshtat), submitted and merged.
- [EliBenSasson: ZK lets us build privacy, native AA lets us make it seamless](https://x.com/EliBenSasson/status/2032420736317428046), 18:37.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: the simpler smart account logic is, the less room wallets have for mistakes, and the approval bits added the day before are simplified further here.
