## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: expiring nonces
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Expiring Nonces for Frame Transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11692) (nerolation), submitted as EIP-8266.
- [EIP-7928: clarify an edge case around 7702 with insufficient gas](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11699) (nerolation), submitted, with an execution-specs test case.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: ordinary nonces must increase strictly, so one stuck transaction blocks everything after it; expiring nonces let a transaction lapse after a timeout so a nonce is no longer a permanent queue slot, which suits privacy protocols and concurrent signing from several devices.

### 3. Proofs and security
**What happened**
- [ethbeat: for the first time L2BEAT regenerated all ZK circuits used by the Lighter perp DEX from source, so users no longer have to trust the Lighter team to perform a permissionless emergency exit](https://x.com/ethbeat_org/status/2056731750378684898), 20:40.
- [ethresear.ch: EVM Verification of WHIR over a 31-bit Field](https://ethresear.ch/t/evm-verification-of-whir-over-a-31-bit-field/24902), 11:12 (alxkzmn). WHIR is a hash-based component that is plausibly post-quantum sound; opening a committed polynomial of size 2^22 over KoalaBear at 100 bits of provable soundness costs about 5,646,080 gas as an EVM transaction.
- [StarkWare: Starknet can already handle 1,000 private TPS and will soon reach 10,000 while being fully post-quantum secure](https://x.com/StarkWareLtd/status/2056738189587501135), 21:06.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy and security: privacy protocols and rollups are only as safe as their circuits, and users truly need not trust a team only when an independent party can reproduce the circuits from source; WHIR's onchain cost decides when post-quantum proofs can spread on L1.
