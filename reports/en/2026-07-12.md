## Key topics

### 1. Ethlabs Week 3
**What happened**
- Per Ethlabs Week 3 (Barnabé Monnot): two hires closed; mainnet as the liquidity hub with ETH at its center needs seamless, secure interop; the team is following EEZ (synchronous composability, led by the Gnosis and Zisk teams) while itself staying on the async lane, where beyond intent-based bridging (OIF), zk tech and fast finality are what make slow bridges faster.
- With the Hegotá non-headliner deadline in mind, the team is preparing a faster-slot proposal; after 6-second slots were declined for Glamsterdam, carlbeek proposed quick slots (EIP-8198), aimed at preparing the ground for future slot time reductions.

**Why it matters**
- For apps: faster slots directly improve L1 UX and markets, and fast finality shortens L1 to L2 waits.

### 2. Inclusion list research
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Sybil attacks on AUCIL](https://ethresear.ch/t/sybil-attacks-on-aucil/25447), 10:28 (AbhiMan1601). Inclusion lists are a censorship resistance primitive under PBS; FOCIL relies on fixed per-slot participants with no explicit pricing, while AUCIL is auction-based and relies on economic incentives.
