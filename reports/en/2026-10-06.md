### 2. Account and transaction specs: SETCODEFROM, assertions, frames
**What happened**
- [EIP-8298 PR: add public mempool rules](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12427) was merged (Marchhill).
- [EIP-7906 PR: price TXTRACE and TXDIFF views at warm read cost](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12437) was merged, and [another PR sets limits on a single transaction's diff](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12438).
- [EIP-8141 PR: deploy the frames expiry verifier as a standard contract](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12387) was merged (lightclient).
- EIP-8288: [a zero data_hash stands for the transaction signature hash](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12435), [mode 1 wrappers must check the dependency union](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12436), and [recursive_stark_gas is charged to the transaction](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12439).
- [EIP-5920 PR: price value with ACCOUNT_WRITE under EIP-8038](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12428).

**Why it matters**
- Once SETCODEFROM enters the public mempool, it needs the same protection against mempool invalidation attacks as 7702 authorizations, and clear rules are what let wallets broadcast it safely. EIP-7906's pricing decides whether assertions are cheap enough to turn on by default.
