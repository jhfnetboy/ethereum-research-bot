## Key topics

### 1. The EF board and Glamsterdam scope
**What happened**
- [EF blog: board update](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/07/29/ef-board-update): pcaversaccio is co-founder and lead of SEAL 911, a member of the EF Silviculture Society, and author of The Ethereum Cypherpunk Manifesto; the board now consists of Aya Miyaguchi (President), Vitalik Buterin (Founder), Patrick Storchenegger (Swiss counsel) and pcaversaccio, who joins for an initial one-year term on a voluntary basis. [EF announcement](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2082437165233951223), 19:04; [pcaversaccio: unconditional privacy](https://x.com/pcaversaccio/status/2082439323274952926), 19:13.
- [EIP-7773: promote CFI to SFI](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12022) (poojaranjan), submitted: based on ACDC #183 decisions, all Core EIPs are promoted to SFI and the CFI section is removed from the upgrade Meta EIP.
- [lightclient: two days left to vote for the Glamsterdam mascot](https://x.com/lightclients/status/2082509615531688015), 23:52.

**Why it matters**
- For app teams: once this change merges, every Glamsterdam EIP is listed as SFI in the upgrade Meta EIP and the scope is essentially locked.

### 2. EIP-8141 implementation and mempool
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: replacement, eviction and payer exposure for the public mempool](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12007), merged; [transaction log set and unrolled-batch log semantics](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12008), merged.
- [EIP-8141: clarify floor repricing, signature validation and frame.value gas](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12026) (AnkushinDaniil), submitted. He is implementing 8141 in Nethermind and cross-testing against ethrex on a devnet; three passages can be read more than one way, and two have already caused real divergences. For example, the calldata floor constant is not defined in this EIP, while EIP-7976 redefines the same constant from 10 to 16.
- [EIP-8250: make keyed nonce changes additive](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11968) and [EIP-8272: make recent root changes additive](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11970), merged.
- [EIP-8038: restate the gas schedule as an access/write/creation component model](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12028) (misilva73), submitted and merged. [EIP-8337 Validated EVM Code](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11922), merged as a Draft.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: two clients have already diverged on 8141, showing frame transactions need more rounds of cross-testing before Hegotá, and Glamsterdam's EIP-7976 calldata repricing directly affects the floor fee of frame transactions.

### 3. Post-quantum wallets
**What happened**
- [EF: Ethereum Builders Live on the PQ1 hardware wallet](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2082496755929669880), 23:01, with Freedom Factory CEO mhaas_eth discussing its quantum-resistant, fully open source hardware and software. Per Ethereal news #33, PQ1 provides post-quantum signatures (SPHINCS+C10) for ERC-4337 smart accounts, with dual secure enclaves and clear signing, at a $179 preorder, shipping Q4 2026.
- [binji: a three-person team built the dgen1 phone from scratch and has now built the first quantum-proof Ethereum wallet](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2082222841848541355), 04:53.

**Why it matters**
- For users: post-quantum signatures do not have to wait for a protocol upgrade; they can be used now through 4337 smart accounts, swapping the signer for a post-quantum scheme while keeping the account address.
