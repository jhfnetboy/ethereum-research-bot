## Key topics

### 1. Accounts and post-quantum: signature migration
**What happened**
- [VitalikButerin: the quantum resistance roadmap; four parts of Ethereum are quantum vulnerable today, consensus BLS signatures, data availability KZG commitments and proofs, EOA ECDSA signatures and application ZK proofs, to be tackled step by step](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2027075026378543132), 00:35; [tcoratger: the consensus layer relies on BLS aggregation and how hash based replacements work](https://x.com/tcoratger/status/2027106705767583886), 02:41.
- [EIP-7932: add support for public key and signature separation](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11357) (SirSpudlington), submitted: moves secp256k1 to 0x0 from 0xff, restricts algorithm types above 126, adds merge_detached_signature and fixes a public key collision with secp256k1; [Magicians: Post Quantum migrations, crypto agility and how to prevent EIP-7932 from failing](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/post-quantum-migrations-crypto-agility-and-how-to-prevent-eip-7932-from-failing/27836) (SirSpudlington), 05:28, listing many routes doing the same thing, from pure ERC-4337 to EIP-6404 SSZ transactions.
- [Magicians: EIP-8175 Composable Transaction thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8175-composable-transaction/27850), 23:50; [Magicians: the real blocker for post-quantum Ethereum is not the math but aggregation](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/the-real-blocker-for-post-quantum-ethereum-isn-t-the-math-it-s-aggregation-and-we-don-t-have-it-yet/27839), 07:06.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: EOA ECDSA is the most direct quantum risk to user funds, and the algorithm registry, composable transactions and frame transactions all compete as the migration path, a split that makes wallet support hard.

### 2. Privacy and ZK security
**What happened**
- [zksecurityXYZ: the first two known exploits of live ZK circuits came from Groth16 verifiers deployed without completing the trusted setup; one was white hat rescued for about $1.5M and the other drained for 5 ETH](https://x.com/zksecurityXYZ/status/2027417243059896544), 23:15; [ameensol: people write privacy by default roadmaps but will not implement a Poseidon precompile, which Solana, Starknet and Stellar have](https://x.com/ameensol/status/2027279608056528982), 14:08.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: privacy protocols depend on ZK circuits and verifier deployment mistakes lose funds directly, while a Poseidon precompile sets the gas cost of verifying privacy proofs onchain.
