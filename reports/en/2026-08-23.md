## Key topics

### 1. Whether to keep the sync committee
**What happened**
- [Magicians: EIP-8390 remove the sync committee](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8390-remove-the-sync-committee/29486), 16:30 (dapplion). It removes the sync committee, its aggregate, subnets, domains and rewards, and the Altair light client protocol built on it; `SYNC_REWARD_WEIGHT` is removed and not redistributed.

**Why it matters**
- It is another issuance-lowering proposal after EIP-8363, this time by removing a protocol duty rather than changing the issuance curve.

### 2. Account delegation
**What happened**
- [New ERC: execution delegation framework](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1962) (F4ever), submitted; per Ethereal news #37 it is ERC-8391.

**Why it matters**
- Delegated execution is a common need for agents and session keys, and ERCs like this bear on both 7702 accounts and native AA.
