### 2. Accounts and signing
**What happened**
- [EIP-8130: `authenticateActor` returns scope, policyType, policyTarget](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11794) (chunter-cb), merged.
- [pcaversaccio: Sign-In with Ethereum is actually a terrible idea](https://x.com/pcaversaccio/status/2065075322920603769), 21:14: it trains people to sign messages all the time and creates signing fatigue, when a signature should be treated as a dangerous, special event.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: 8130 actors carry call policies, so wallets and dapps can learn what a key may do with one query, which helps show session key permissions. Signing fatigue is fertile ground for phishing signatures; clear signing and fewer pointless signature requests are two ways out.

### 3. State structure
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Partitioned Binary Tree](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11799) (weiihann), submitted; this is EIP-8297. It replaces the hexary Patricia tries with one binary tree keyed by 32 bytes, merging account and storage tries and holding contract code; account data is split into independent leaves grouped by 256 for locality; the high 4 bits of each key are a zone id for account headers, contract code or storage.
- [Magicians: EIP-8296 Fixed-Cutoff State Tiering](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8296-fixed-cutoff-state-tiering/28772), 12:09.
- [EIP-8188: clarify reverts and bytes added](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11797), submitted and merged.
