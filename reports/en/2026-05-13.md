## Key topics

### 1. EOA key deactivation: EIP-7851 and EIP-8151
**What happened**
- [EIP-7851: simplify ECDSA authority deactivation](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11656) (colinlyguo), submitted and merged. Based on forum and core dev feedback, it removes the 7-day grace period and becomes a minimal EIP-7702 based protocol change: a reserved `DEACTIVATE_ADDRESS` in the existing 7702 authorization flow changes an active delegation from `0xef0100 || delegate_address` to `0xef0101 || delegate_address`.
- [EIP-8151: align ecRecover with EIP-7851](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11657) (colinlyguo), submitted and merged; [EIP-7851: a few tweaks to 7851 and 8151](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11661) (lightclient), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: after a user upgrades an EOA to a 7702 smart account, the original private key still has full authority, and a leak loses the whole account. EIP-7851 lets users switch that old key off, a key step toward a real smart account security model for 7702 accounts.

### 2. Native AA: EIP-8141 and EIP-8130
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: support atomic batching with any frames](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11652) (derekchiang), submitted and merged, while disallowing batching in validation prefixes for public mempool transactions.
- [EIP-8141: add an EXPIRY_VERIFIER frame for tx expiry](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11662) (nerolation), submitted.
- [EIP-8130: update gas and verifier validation](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11654) (chunter-cb), submitted and merged: sender authentication and intrinsic costs move under the signed `gas_limit`, bounding payer exposure; payer authentication is metered separately as `payer_auth_cost`, so the payer's verifier choice cannot reduce gas available to calls. [EIP-8130: add metadata sink](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11655), submitted for discussion only.

**Why it matters**
- For account transactions: atomic batching makes the operations in one transaction all succeed or all revert, and banning batching in validation prefixes keeps it cheap for nodes to judge validity, protecting the public mempool.

### 3. Transactions and gas: EIP-8037 and EIP-8038
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: update parameters and rationale](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11616) (misilva73), merged: recalibrated to a 150M reference block gas limit and a 120 GiB per year state growth target, raising `CPSB` from 1174 to 1530; `STATE_BYTES_PER_STORAGE_SET` goes from 32 to 64 and `STATE_BYTES_PER_NEW_ACCOUNT` from 112 to 120 to include the leaf key.
- [EIP-8037: fix bugs from bal-devnet-6](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11611), merged, including a refund rule for new-account state gas when a contract creation transaction reverts.
- [EIP-8038: align with 8037](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11623), merged; [EIP-8038: quick fixes](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11663), submitted and merged.

**Why it matters**
- For users: EIP-8037 decides how much state gas new accounts and storage writes cost, which sets the cost of deploying a smart wallet or sending to a fresh address for the first time.
