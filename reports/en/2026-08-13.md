## Key topics

### 1. EIP-8141 and ACDE #243
**What happened**
- ACDE #243 (as summarized by Ethereal news #35): on native account abstraction, client teams reached rough consensus for EIP-8141 frame transactions, with L2 concerns to be addressed in a breakout before a decision at the next ACDE on making it a headliner; Platåberget (devnet-8) is live with the Glamsterdam upgrade in about a week; repricing numbers are finalized and a 200M gas limit is safe after Glamsterdam, with the EIP-8261 gas limit schedule optional; Hegotá non-headliner presentations covered EIP-7923, EIP-3298, EIP-5920, EIP-8358, EIP-8374, EIP-8368, EIP-8355 (ML-DSA verification precompiles), EIP-8372, EIP-8375 and EIP-8219, with client preferences due September 10.
- [EIP-8141: allow concurrent pending frame transactions on disjoint keyed-nonce sets](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12162) (AnkushinDaniil), submitted. The generic one-pending-transaction-per-sender limit exists because transactions under one nonce form a single sequence where only one can be included, and one change to the sender's validation inputs invalidates all of them; EIP-8250 removes the first cause for disjoint non-zero nonce-key sets.
- [EIP-8141: recognize a canonical passkey wallet for no-simulation admission](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12161) (AnkushinDaniil), submitted. The current no-simulation set covers only default code, the expiry verifier and the canonical paymaster, all `SECP256K1`; a passkey wallet delegated to a P256 signer still goes through full simulation even though its validation is a single P256 check.
- [EIP-8141: enumerate the validation-prefix state dependency set for revalidation without re-simulation](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12160) and [forbid approval scope on frames inside an atomic batch](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12155) (AnkushinDaniil), submitted; [dispatch a precompile targeted by a frame](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12157) (Marchhill), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: keyed nonces let one account have several pending transactions at once (for example a privacy withdrawal and an ordinary transfer that do not block each other), and no-simulation admission makes transactions from biometric phone wallets cheaper to propagate. With 8141 in headliner discussion, native AA has a real chance of landing in Hegotá.

### 2. Multidimensional gas and hash functions
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: designs for EVM gas accounting in EIP-7999](https://ethresear.ch/t/designs-for-evm-gas-accounting-in-eip-7999/25696), 06:22 (aelowsson). EIP-8037 makes state creation a separately metered resource but keeps its pricing simple so as not to delay Glamsterdam; the post discusses how to account for EVM gas in EIP-7999's unified multidimensional fee market.
- [Justin Drake: goodbye Poseidon; the EF is abandoning Poseidon for L1 and pivoting to SHA or BLAKE](https://x.com/drakefjustin/status/2087905684180418733), 21:14, calling it the ideal conclusion of an 8-year, 8-figure post-quantum rabbit hole.
- [An update to the normalized state gas limit (EIP-8372)](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12159), merged.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy apps: the hash function L1 settles on affects the cost of future onchain proofs. Poseidon is cheap inside circuits while SHA and BLAKE are more mature outside them, so privacy protocols should watch the proof system choices that follow.
