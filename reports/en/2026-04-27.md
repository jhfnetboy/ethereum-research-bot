### 3. CL presets and the EVM
**What happened**
- [Magicians: should consensus layer protocol presets be exposed to the EVM?](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/should-consensus-layer-protocol-presets-be-exposed-to-the-evm/28361), 04:16: since EIP-4788 contracts can verify BeaconState fields with SSZ proofs, but presets such as slashing quotients live only in client code, so contracts hardcode them or rely on governance maintained tables; the post proposes a preset_root commitment in BeaconState.
- [Add EIP: Independent CL/EL Sync](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11557) (potuz), merged; [Add EIP: Fallback Proposers](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11572) (soispoke), submitted.
