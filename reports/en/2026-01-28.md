## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: account configuration
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Account Abstraction by Account Configuration](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11186) (chunter-cb), submitted: accounts assign onchain verifier contracts to signing keys, the protocol natively validates well known verifiers such as K1, P256, BLS and WebAuthn, and other algorithms are verified through sandboxed pure function contracts.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: unlike EIP-8141's general frames, 8130 restricts validation to known verifiers and pure functions, which is easier for mempools to handle safely at the cost of flexibility.

### 2. Privacy: stealth addresses and inclusion lists
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Stealth Address + Sub Accounts for 7702 Account](https://ethresear.ch/t/stealth-address-sub-accounts-for-7702-account/23964) (0xkoiner), 16:03: the root key can be an existing 7702 account, spending and viewing keys live in a KMS, HSM or MPC, and discovery needs ERC-5564 metadata (a 1 byte viewTag and a 33 byte ephemeral public key).
- [ethresear.ch: zkFOCIL Implementation and Benchmarking Report](https://ethresear.ch/t/zkfocil-implementation-benchmarking-report/23966) (shreyas-londhe), 20:36: implements and benchmarks the zkFOCIL linkable ring signature, protecting the inclusion list committee from coercion without exposing individual validators.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: stealth addresses let 7702 users receive funds without exposing their main address, and zkFOCIL keeps the censorship resistance mechanism from becoming an attack surface on validators.
