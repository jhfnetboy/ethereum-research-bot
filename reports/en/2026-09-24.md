# Ethereum Research Daily 2026-09-24

Keywords: ACDE #246, EIP-8250 and EIP-8272 CFI, EIP-7906 transaction assertions, repricing EIP-8131 and EIP-8279, Daisugi post-quantum testnet, CHAMP mempool peer protection

Summary: ACDE #246 moved 10 EIPs to Considered for Inclusion (CFI) in Hegotá, including three companions to frame transactions: keyed nonces (EIP-8250), recent roots (EIP-8272) and transaction assertions via a state diff opcode (EIP-7906), plus refund removal (EIP-3298) and the repricings EIP-8131 and EIP-8279; EIP-8304 was declined, and EIP-8372 and EIP-8368 wait for 30 days of Glamsterdam mainnet data. asanso launched Daisugi v0.1, a post-quantum testnet using SPHINCS hash-based signatures.

## Highlights
1. **Native privacy bundle**: alongside EIP-8141, EIP-8250 and EIP-8272 are CFI, as is EIP-7906 transaction assertions.
2. **Gas rules**: EIP-3298 refund removal and the two bounding repricings EIP-8131 and EIP-8279 are CFI.
3. **Data-driven**: one of EIP-8372 and EIP-8368, or neither, will be chosen after 30 days of mainnet data.
4. **Post-quantum**: Daisugi v0.1 uses SPHINCS signatures and non-native AA, with frame transactions and LeanSPHINCS aggregation next.

## Key topics

### 1. Hegotá scope at ACDE #246
**What happened**
- [EIP-8081: decisions from ACDE #246](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12377), merged: CFI for 3298, 7668, 7906, 7979, 8131, 8163, 8250, 8253, 8272 and 8279; DFI for 8304; 8372 and 8368 stay PFI until 30 days of Glamsterdam mainnet data accumulate, when devs will CFI one or DFI both.
- [nixorokish: features moved to "considered" for Hegotá today](https://x.com/nixorokish/status/2103164113841250376), 23:46, including three frame transaction EIPs (transaction assertions via a state diff opcode, keyed nonces and recent roots), removing bloom filters, bumping the nonce of zero-nonce storage accounts and removing the storage-clear refund.
- [soispoke: keyed nonces and recent roots were just CFI'd, one more step toward native privacy](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2103140368535683098), 22:11; [ethrex: both run alongside EIP-8141 on its public privacy testnet, where you can try them now](https://x.com/ethrex_client/status/2103144915693678855), 22:29.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and privacy apps, Hegotá's native privacy bundle has largely taken shape (frames + keyed nonces + recent roots + transaction assertions). Refund removal and repricing change the cost structure of contract gas, and contracts that rely on SSTORE-clearing refunds should be assessed early.

### 2. Post-quantum and networking
**What happened**
- [asanso: Daisugi v0.1, a post-quantum Ethereum testnet, is live](https://x.com/asanso/status/2102829025211060529), 01:34, using hash-based SPHINCS signatures with non-native account abstraction, with frame transactions and LeanSPHINCS signature aggregation coming next.
- [tcoratger: Ethereum is moving to post-quantum proof systems like leanVM and Flock, removing the strict need for arithmetic-friendly hashes like Poseidon](https://x.com/tcoratger/status/2102873051562197497), 04:29.
- [ethresear.ch: CHAMP, hardening the mempool with chain-anchored, multi-dimensional peer protection](https://ethresear.ch/t/champ-hardening-the-mempool-with-chain-anchored-multi-dimensional-peer-protection/26074), 17:58 (cskiraly), shielding a small set of high-quality peers from random churn.
- [ethresear.ch: Snappy with a memory, about 40% less gossip traffic](https://ethresear.ch/t/snappy-with-a-memory-40-less-gossip-traffic/26078), 19:28, from networking research for fast finality.

**Why it matters**
- Frame transactions let accounts switch to post-quantum signatures without a fork per scheme, and Daisugi is a testing ground for that path.
