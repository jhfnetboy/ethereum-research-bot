## Key topics

### 1. Accounts: transactions with a selectable signature algorithm
**What happened**
- [New EIP: Scheme-Agile Transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11438) (Giulio2002), merged: a `0x05` EIP-2718 SchemedTransaction lets EOAs pick a signature algorithm via `scheme_id`, launching with secp256k1 (same address derivation as today), P256/secp256r1 (passkeys, WebAuthn, Secure Enclave) and Falcon-512 (post-quantum, FIPS 206); fields match EIP-1559 with `v, r, s` replaced by `scheme_id` plus signature data, non secp256k1 addresses carry a scheme prefix to avoid cross scheme collisions, and post-quantum signatures, about 10x larger, pay a gas surcharge.
- [Magicians: EIP-8225 ECRECOVER Native Key Awareness](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8225-ecrecover-native-key-awareness/28332), 21:52.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: this path lets EOAs change signature schemes directly, without smart accounts, in contrast to EIP-8141 and EIP-8164; whether users can sign ordinary transactions with passkeys or post-quantum keys depends on which path the protocol picks.

**What happened**

**Why it matters**
