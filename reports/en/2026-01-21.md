## Key topics

### 1. Gas: EIP-8037 enters CFI
**What happened**
- [EIP-7773: CFI EIP-8037](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11117) (nixorokish), merged after submission on January 20; [EIP-7778: clarify that user gas accounting remains unchanged](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11138) (yperbasis), submitted and merged, restoring the user gas accounting section removed in PR 11099.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: EIP-8037 prices state creation separately so the gas limit can keep rising with controlled state growth, and EIP-7778 drops refunds from block gas accounting while leaving what users pay unchanged.

### 2. Signatures: the PQTS breakout
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Post Quantum transaction signature (PQTS) breakout kickoff](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/post-quantum-transaction-signature-pqts-breakout-room-kickoff-call/27554), 21:00: the first session on February 4 at 22:00 covers a PQTS overview, goal setting and community alignment.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: transaction signatures are what users touch directly, and the breakout coordinates how accounts move from ECDSA to post-quantum schemes.
