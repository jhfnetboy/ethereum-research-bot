## Key topics

### 1. Account authority: the Resolution draft
**What happened**
- [Magicians: EIP-TBD: Resolution, Non-Self-Authorizing State Transitions](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-tbd-resolution-non-self-authorizing-state-transitions/29846), posted at 11:37 by chugarchugarr, reached 11 posts the same day. It pulls out a boundary shared by recovery, agents, provenance and adjudication: candidate transition, evidence, admissibility, authority, resolution and successor state, each a separate step. The invariant is that no successor state may hold more authority than can be derived from the admitted evidence, the committed procedure and the current authority state.

**Why it matters**
- In social recovery or AI agents acting on accounts, problems often come from assuming that a valid proof automatically grants the right to change state. The framework is a useful reference for designing recovery modules and agent permissions, but for now it is an unnumbered concept draft.
