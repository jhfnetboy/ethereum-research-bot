## Highlights
1. **8130 authorization**: scope bits are pure grants, with combination semantics checked at the point of use.
2. **Payment split**: self-pay versus sponsorship is defined by whether the payer account equals the sender account.
3. **Signing**: `operational := admin || (SENDER && !POLICY)`, and `verifySignature()` requires operational authority.
4. **Post-quantum**: attestation aggregators prove post-quantum signatures into succinct aggregate proofs.

## Key topics

### 1. EIP-8130's authority model
**What happened**
- [EIP-8130: scope grants, PAYER split, operational ERC-1271, account lock](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11918) (chunter-cb), submitted and merged.
  - Authorization: admin is the predicate `scope == 0x00` (config-change authority and delegation), with no separate CONFIG grant; scope bits are `SENDER` (`0x01`), `POLICY` (`0x02`), `NONCE` (`0x04`), `SELF_PAYER` (`0x08`) and `SPONSOR_PAYER` (`0x10`), with bits 5 to 7 spare; `POLICY` means calls are gated through the actor's `manager`.
  - Payment: `PAYER` splits into `SELF_PAYER` (pay the account's own gas when payer equals sender) and `SPONSOR_PAYER` (act as `payer_auth` for a different sender), so there can be a dedicated gas-only key, or a sponsor-only paymaster hot key over a locked treasury.
  - Signing: the `SIGNER` bit is removed; signing is an encoding of operational authority, and a `POLICY` actor is never operational and must not sign around its gate.
  - Account lock: the lifecycle is simply lock then unlock, with no re-lock or cancel while locked; the mempool may grant higher rate limits to accounts that are `LOCKED` with `unlock_delay` above a threshold.
  - Nonces: the 2D `nonce_key`/`nonce_sequence` stays, and nonce-free transactions use a `replay_id` that excludes fees and auth blobs to allow fee-bump replacement.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: these scopes let session keys, gas-only keys and policy-bound agent keys be expressed at the protocol layer, without contract logic for each case. For paymasters: `SPONSOR_PAYER` grants sponsorship only, so a hot key holds sponsorship rights only. For the mempool: a locked account's configuration cannot change suddenly, so nodes can relax rate limits, which is 8130's approach to DoS protection.
