## Key topics

### 1. Transaction confirmation: the Fast Confirmation Rule
**What happened**
- [barnabemonnot: a year of backtesting shows 96% of past blocks would have been fast confirmed in a single slot, so deposits from Ethereum to L2s or exchanges could be available in about 12 seconds; the other 4% are usually confirmed fast too](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2060231270622167369), 12:26.

**Why it matters**
- For users: deposits today usually wait for finality (about 13 minutes); fast confirmation cuts trustworthy arrival time to one slot without changing consensus rules, improving bridge and exchange UX.

### 2. Transactions and gas: EIP-8279
**What happened**
- [Magicians: EIP-8279 Block Access List Byte Floor discussion](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8279-block-access-list-byte-floor/28662), 17:26 (Nerolation), for [PR #11739](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11739) submitted the day before.
- [ethresear.ch: Imperfect Commitment in Maximal Extractable Value Auctions](https://ethresear.ch/t/imperfect-commitment-in-maximal-extractable-value-auctions/25001), 21:50 (nuconstruct), studying builders who defect from an honest auction after seeing bids and payloads.

**Why it matters**
- For users: BALs are a core Glamsterdam change, and counting them toward the data floor keeps worst case block size in check while a new block data structure is introduced.

### 3. Post-quantum
**What happened**
- [zeamETH: now at devnet5 interop, the culmination of the post-quantum consensus stack it has been building for Ethereum mainnet](https://x.com/zeamETH/status/2060344683319177565), 19:57.
- [StarkWare: the 2018 STARK paper was already titled "post-quantum secure"; STARKs avoided trusted setups and curve based proof assumptions early](https://x.com/StarkWareLtd/status/2060256649839813114), 14:07.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts and consensus: BLS signatures aggregate while post-quantum signatures behave differently, so multi client interop is a necessary step in proving a new signature machinery works.
