# Ethereum Daily 2026-11-28

Keywords: Glamsterdam EIP-8037, Hoodi on December 15, EIP-8476 7702 opt-out, Hegotá FOCIL decision, post-quantum account migration

Summary: ACDC #192 agreed to ship Glamsterdam with its current spec, keeping the EIP-8037 state creation repricing, and scheduled the Hoodi testnet upgrade for 2026-12-15 19:12 UTC. On the account side, EIP-8476 (a permanent EOA opt-out from EIP-7702 delegation) and the seed-proof recovery update to EIP-8425 were merged, while mainnet bytecode tests led to two proposed restrictions on EIP-8298 SETCODEFROM. Whether Hegotá keeps FOCIL or goes all in on post-quantum work will be decided at the ACDC call around December 10.

Coverage: roughly 2026-11-25 08:40 to 2026-11-28 09:00 (Asia/Bangkok UTC+7). There were no separate reports for 11-26 and 11-27, so this issue covers both. All times are UTC+7.

## Key topics

### 1. Glamsterdam keeps EIP-8037: the gas repricing ships as is
**What happened**
- [Ethlabs posted its position on Glamsterdam](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/ethlabs-position-on-glamsterdam/30518) on Magicians at 2026-11-26 03:26 (caspar), recommending going ahead with the current spec. Most of the disruption comes from repricing state creation, which it calls a necessary step toward 1000x scaling. Pulling EIP-8037 would give up Glamsterdam's roughly 3x higher gas limit and only push the problem to a later fork. The new state gas reservoir is a secondary source of breakage, and changing the EIP would help only some affected projects at the cost of more months of delay. [Ethlabs' X post](https://x.com/ethlabs_org/status/2125705589229527045) (2026-11-26 04:38) gives the background: an October audit found the repricings break assumptions some deployed contracts rely on, and the rollout was paused.
- [ACDC #192 meeting thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/all-core-devs-consensus-acdc-192-november-26-2026/30388) (2026-11-26): EL teams reached rough consensus to ship the current spec, though the auto summary notes lingering concerns about breakage and the usability of the GAS opcode. The CL side adopted the non consensus breaking PR 5770 (bringing RLP into the CL to verify the payload block hash) and deferred PR 5820. [EIPsInsight's notes](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/all-core-devs-consensus-acdc-192-november-26-2026/30388/4) (2026-11-26 23:50) confirm the Hoodi upgrade for 2026-12-15 19:12 UTC (epoch 143616).
- On the spec: [PR #12717, which would have replaced the reservoir with a single gas_left and moved EIP-8037 to Review](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12717), was closed without merging at 2026-11-26 21:32, in line with the no spec change decision. [PR #12732](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12732) (2026-11-28 05:09, tcoratger, open) proposes dropping the EIP-7610 storage collision text from EIP-8037.
- Requests from app developers: [milonite asked on the ACDC agenda](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2305) (2026-11-26 20:55) that, if 8037 changes at all, gas refills come back for transactions that do not consume state ([PR #12249](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12249)). [gretzke asked on the ACDE #250 agenda](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2316) (2026-11-26 08:08) to discuss a new opcode for reading the reservoir; that call is on December 3.
- New draft: [Versioned Opcodes for Gas Repricing merged as a Draft](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12638) (2026-11-27 09:20, lglisic60). New contracts would have to use V2 opcodes that carry the EIP-8037/8038 gas schedule, existing contracts keep the legacy schedule, DELEGATECALL_V2 requires a V2 target, and gas introspection opcodes such as GAS and GASLIMIT are left out of V2.
- [HarryR noted in the EIP-8037 thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8037-state-creation-gas-cost-increase/25694/54) (2026-11-27 18:48) that pricier state creation strengthens the incentive behind the "1 wei trick": leaving the smallest unit behind when forwarding a balance so the balance slot stays in state.
- [growthepie estimates](https://x.com/growthepie_eth/status/2126339765909729504) (2026-11-27 22:38) that Glamsterdam raises the gas limit 3.3x, taking Ethereum from 24.9 TPS today to around 80 TPS in early 2027.

**Why it matters**
- This is the most direct change at the account and gas level. Creating new storage slots, new accounts and deploying contracts gets noticeably more expensive, and contracts that rely on fixed gas amounts (for example, using GAS to size subcalls or forwarding a fixed stipend) may stop working. Ethlabs' view is that this breakage cannot be fully engineered away by changing the EIP.
- Wallets and apps should test on Hoodi: gas estimation, paymaster budgets and the cost of a first transfer to a fresh address (which creates a new balance slot) will all change.

**What to watch**
- Whether ACDE #250 on December 3 takes up the reservoir introspection opcode, how the Hoodi upgrade goes on 12-15, and the mainnet date ([jihoonsong sees January as plausible](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/a-better-pipeline-toward-pq-and-censorship-resistant-ethereum/30522) if the EL spec does not change).

### 2. Account migration, post-quantum and privacy: 7702 opt-out, seed recovery and a tighter SETCODEFROM
**What happened**
- [EIP-8476 Permanent EOA Delegation Opt-Out](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12600) was merged as a Draft Core EIP at 2026-11-27 09:17 (nicolashw). Using a narrowly constrained, previously invalid form of set-code transaction, an EOA writes the 3-byte marker 0xef0200, after which no delegation authorization, including a clearing one, can replace it. The EVM treats the marker as empty code (EXTCODESIZE is 0), so contracts still see an ordinary EOA. The motivation is that exchange hot wallets, institutions and treasuries do not need 7702, and delegated execution can undermine the assumptions behind their offchain controls. It only removes delegation; a leaked key can still sign normal transactions.
- [EIP-8425 Quantum Freeze and Account Recovery update](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12722) merged at 2026-11-27 22:12 (colinlyguo). Per [the author's Magicians post](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8425-quantum-freeze-and-account-recovery/29770/32) (2026-11-27 20:41), pre-freeze recovery commitments and their separate cutoff are gone in favor of EIP-8298 migration. Seed proofs are bound to (chain_id, account, nonce, source, code_hash), seed recovery ends permanently once regular code is installed, and the freeze now also covers EIP-8141 SECP256K1 entries while keeping P256VERIFY and EIP-8141 P256. Whether and when to activate a freeze remains a separate community decision.
- [CPerezz tested EIP-8298 SETCODEFROM against mainnet bytecode on ethresear.ch](https://ethresear.ch/t/a-state-backed-validation-of-setcodefrom/26300) (2026-11-27 15:11), with the matching [PR #12716](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12716) (open). Two problems surfaced: the frame running SETCODEFROM keeps executing its old code while everyone else already sees the new code ("show one code, run another", illustrated with sudoswap v1's router, which recognizes pairs by the first 73 bytes of their code), and borrowed code can replace your own. The proposal defers the code change and only allows adopting from the account's own code.
- [ilitteri's experimental comparison of post-quantum execution paths for Hegotá](https://ethresear.ch/t/paths-to-post-quantum-execution-in-hegota-an-experimental-comparison/26304) (2026-11-27 17:03): ethrex implemented four ways to integrate leanSPHINCS on the same baseline. The inline PROOF frame option had the smallest implementation footprint without aggregation and the lowest cumulative EL churn through aggregation, and segregating witnesses did not materially cut bandwidth. The post says bogota-devnet-2 already exercises EIP-8141, EIP-8298 and EIP-8151. This gives data to lightclient's [PROOF frame PR #12714](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12714).
- Validators and keys: [potuz's Post-Quantum Material Registration draft](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12609) (updated 11-27, open) lets a withdrawal address register 32 bytes of PQ material per validator. The ERCs repo merged [ERC-8517 PQ validator seed derivation](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/2214) (2026-11-27 20:48) and [ERC-8499 hash-based key derivation from BIP-39](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/2172) (2026-11-27 20:44), and discussion opened on [ERC-8519, a PQ validator and builder keystore](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8519-post-quantum-validator-and-builder-keystore/30525) (2026-11-27 20:56).
- Privacy: [ERC-8441 hybrid post-quantum stealth addresses](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8441-hybrid-post-quantum-stealth-address-scheme/29923/11) got an independent TypeScript implementation that matches all 29 official test vector rows (2026-11-26 18:41), and author namncc said the ERC is being revised to add KEM agility. [siriusthemaster's ethresear.ch post](https://ethresear.ch/t/stealth-addresses-after-secp256k1-falls-payer-clawback-signature-derived-keys-and-a-recovery-statement-for-erc-8441-outputs/26305) (2026-11-27 20:35) argues that ERC-5564 schemeId 1 stealth outputs are worse off than never-used EOAs against a quantum adversary, who could link and steal unspent outputs from public data alone, and lists two holes wallets can close today (payer clawback and keys derived from ECDSA signatures).

**Why it matters**
- Native accounts are gaining the option to say no: EIP-8476 gives EOAs that do not want 7702 a permanent switch, a way for custodians and institutional wallets to shrink their attack surface.
- EIP-8298, EIP-8425 and EIP-8141 together form the main path for moving to post-quantum signatures without changing addresses. A tighter SETCODEFROM means account code switches take effect one step later, and wallets designing migration flows need to follow the new rules.
- Users who receive funds through stealth addresses face a "harvest now, link later" privacy risk. ERC-8441 only protects discovery; spending still uses secp256k1.

**What to watch**
- Whether PRs #12716 and #12714 get merged, the Magicians discussion and client feedback on EIP-8476, and the KEM agility revision of ERC-8441.

### 3. Rescoping Hegotá: FOCIL and post-quantum compete for the same people
**What happened**
- [fradamt](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/thoughts-on-pqai-hegota-and-focil/30421/4) (2026-11-25 23:07) and [caspar](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/thoughts-on-pqai-hegota-and-focil/30421/6) (2026-11-26 12:53) argued that to make Ethereum secure against quantum and AI threats (pqai) as fast as possible, Hegotá should drop FOCIL for now, since both draw on the same small group of high-context core devs. [nflaig disagreed](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/thoughts-on-pqai-hegota-and-focil/30421/7) (2026-11-26 19:07): transaction supply chain centralization keeps growing, scaling is limited without FOCIL, and Lodestar has already merged a complete implementation to its trunk. [trent_vanepps said](https://x.com/trent_vanepps/status/2125649806039334917) (2026-11-26 00:56) Ethlabs has withdrawn EIP-8198 Quick Slots from consideration, while [potuz argued](https://x.com/potuz_eth/status/2125674861557129250) (2026-11-26 02:35) that a PQ fork adds new attack surface and the "ai" in pqai is unnecessary.
- [barnabasbusa proposed "FOCIL light" on the ACDC agenda](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2305) (2026-11-26 20:20): EIP-7805 would enforce inclusion only for legacy, 2930, 1559 and 7702 transactions, and frame transactions could appear in inclusion lists but omitting them would always be excused. He added that until then ECDSA must not be deprecated, because EOAs are the only censorship resistant path.
- [jihoonsong proposed scheduling by the research, development and testing pipeline](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/a-better-pipeline-toward-pq-and-censorship-resistant-ethereum/30522) (2026-11-26 19:47): PQ consensus (PQ Gasper or PQ Decoupled Consensus) is still in research, and PQ preregistration could ship shortly after Glamsterdam. His [ethresear.ch post](https://ethresear.ch/t/focil-and-ethereums-pq-transition/26292) (2026-11-25 19:30) explains that FOCIL keeps working through the PQ transition, and an 8 KiB inclusion list can carry one transaction with a leanSPHINCS signature (7,740 B).
- Progress: [PQ-CL Breakout #5](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/pq-cl-breakout-5-november-27-2026/30499/4) (EIPsInsight notes, 2026-11-28 01:56) reports that PQ devnet-1 launched with all 8 CL clients, and the next devnet will test segmented proof broadcast. [terencechain said](https://x.com/terencechain/status/2126317166454870476) (2026-11-27 21:08) participation on devnet-1 is near perfect. ACDC #192 made no decision on FOCIL, and the full Hegotá scope is expected at the ACDC call around December 10. Meanwhile the FOCIL spec keeps moving: [the EIP-7805 rebase was merged](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12470) (2026-11-28 00:03).

**Why it matters**
- FOCIL decides whether transactions get protocol-level censorship resistance, and whether frame transactions (AA accounts) and EOAs are treated equally on that front. Under FOCIL light, frame transactions would have no inclusion guarantee during the transition, and ECDSA accounts would not be deprecated for now.

**What to watch**
- [PQ-CL Breakout #6](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/issues/2321) on December 1, and the Hegotá scope decision at the ACDC call around December 10.
