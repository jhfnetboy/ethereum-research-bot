## Key topics

### 1. Native AA and PQ transactions: the route debate
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Scheme-Agile Transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11438) (Giulio2002), submitted; [Magicians thread EIP-8202](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8202-schemed-transaction/28044), 05:13: no new opcodes, account abstraction or execution frames, just one new transaction type reusing EIP-1559 fields with v, r, s replaced by scheme_id and signature_data; the author argues frame designs still have unresolved mempool validation issues and frames can be layered on later.
- [Magicians: Frame Transactions vs. SchemedTransactions for Post-Quantum Ethereum](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/frame-transactions-vs-schemedtransactions-for-post-quantum-ethereum/28056) (Giulio2002), 23:03: with Falcon and Dilithium seen as the viable replacements, the debate has moved to which transaction architecture delivers them; 41 posts and over 1,300 views.
- [ladislaus0x: ACD plans to decide on EIP-8141 for Hegotá this Thursday; a PQ secure consensus layer with a quantum vulnerable execution layer does not solve the problem](https://x.com/ladislaus0x/status/2036075412116107630), 20:39.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: SchemedTransaction only swaps signatures, while frame transactions also bring native AA, gas sponsorship and batching; the choice decides whether wallets after Hegotá move to protocol level smart accounts or keep relying on ERC-4337 and EIP-7702.

### 2. Privacy and ePBS
**What happened**
- [EIP-8184: add a subsection on competition for the top of block and adjust constants](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11440) (anderselowsson), submitted and merged, updating the LUCID encrypted mempool; [_julianma: join the discussion on whether Ethereum needs an encrypted mempool now](https://x.com/_julianma/status/2036110381802811569), 22:58.
- [ethresear.ch: Why a Variable Payload Deadline Only Helps by ~6%](https://ethresear.ch/t/why-a-variable-payload-deadline-only-helps-by-6/24488), 16:09; [nero_eth: ePBS should not adopt a variable payload deadline in its current form; the theoretical gain is about 6% gas limit headroom](https://x.com/nero_eth/status/2036027958406566066), 17:31.
- [ACDT #75](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdt/075), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #17](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-17/): ePBS adds a cached PTC window (previous plus next 2 epochs) to state; a variable PTC deadline does not change consensus and can be added after the upgrade.
