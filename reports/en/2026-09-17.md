## Key topics

### 1. Glamsterdam heads to testnets
**What happened**
- [EF blog: Glamsterdam testnet announcement](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/09/17/glamsterdam-testnet-announcement). Sepolia activates at epoch 353,024, slot 11,296,768 (October 6, 13:53:36 UTC); Hoodi and mainnet are undecided. The headliners are ePBS (EIP-7732) and block-level access lists (EIP-7928). On gas, EIP-8037 raises and separately meters the cost of creating state and EIP-8038 updates state access costs, alongside changes to intrinsic transaction gas (EIP-2780), calldata, access lists and block gas accounting without refunds (EIP-7778). The announcement warns that contracts relying on fixed gas stipends, hardcoded gas limits or remaining-gas assumptions may need changes, and links a repricing impact guide.
- ACDC #187 at 21:00 (per Ethereal news #40): Sepolia upgrades October 6 with client releases by September 29; Hoodi targets October 27, depending on Sepolia; for Hegotá, EIP-8015 (remove deposit and eth1data fields) is CFI, 7 EIPs (8146, 8237, 8243, 8321, 8341, 8367 and 8375) are DFI, and EIP-8411 fast execution payload broadcast was Proposed for Inclusion (PFI).
- [EIP-8365: BLS withdrawal credential retirement](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12097) was merged as a draft, proposing to retire the 0x00 withdrawal credential type.

**Why it matters**
- For dApps and wallets, this is the first repricing on a public testnet. Once Sepolia activates, regression-test gas estimation, calls that rely on the fixed 2300 stipend and the like, and account-creation costs (state gas) right away.

### 2. Faster blocks
**What happened**
- [barnabemonnot: after a month of outreach, Ethlabs ships a major piece on faster L1 blocks](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2100575903461839032), 20:21; [binji: publishing an article that took a lot of work via the Ethlabs account](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2100523127864275047), 16:51; [Derek Chiang: the two did not handwave but made a careful case for quick slots (EIP-8198)](https://x.com/decentrek/status/2100585746340147342), 21:00.
- [Julian Ma: fast confirmations can speed up L1-to-L2 deposits and ZK proofs can speed up L2-to-L1 withdrawals, but slot time is the smallest unit of time affecting everything](https://x.com/_julianma/status/2100574109482557865), 20:14.
- [ethresear.ch: EIP-8411, what segmented payload diffusion is made of](https://ethresear.ch/t/eip-8411-what-segmented-payload-diffusion-is-made-of/26025), 10:41 (cskiraly). Payload propagation is fast enough today largely because blocks are small and datacenter builders and high-bandwidth nodes carry it; EIP-8411 uses message segmentation so pieces pipeline through the network, and the only consensus change is a Merkle tree commitment in the bid.

**Why it matters**
- Shorter slots speed up every confirmation-dependent experience, and a larger gas limit needs segmented propagation to go with it.
