**Why it matters**
- Shadow Accounts are what account-level privacy looks like as a product: privacy is no longer a separate app but a mode inside the same app. On Ethereum, the counterpart is the swappable signature schemes that frame transactions bring to accounts. StarkWare's score is an outside view, useful as a yardstick while tracking Hegotá's progress.
