## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: EIP-8141 Frame Transaction
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Frame Transaction](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11202) (fjl), submitted and merged; [EIP-8141: add the missing elision of VERIFY frame data](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11205) (fjl), submitted and merged; [Magicians thread EIP-8141](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8141-frame-transaction/27617) (matt), 10:35: a new transaction whose validity and gas payment are defined abstractly, so accounts no longer rely on one ECDSA signature and can define and interpret any cryptographic signature scheme.
- [Magicians: Hegotá headliner proposal Frame Transaction](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-proposal-frame-transaction/27618), 10:49; [lightclients: introducing EIP-8141 Frame Transaction](https://x.com/lightclients/status/2016754618429010070), 13:05.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts and signatures: splitting validation, execution and payment into frames makes smart accounts native transactions that can use passkeys or post-quantum signatures with third party gas, without 4337 bundlers or the EntryPoint.

### 2. Native AA: EIP-8130 gas payments
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Token Gas Payments for EIP-8130](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11194) (chunter-cb), submitted: lets EIP-8130 AA transactions pay gas in ERC-20 tokens through the extensions field, while EIP-8130 itself was still in PR.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: users can transact without holding ETH, removing one of the biggest onboarding barriers for wallets.

### 3. Gas: EIP-8037 failure modes
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: Failure modes in EIP-8037 and state-gas scaling](https://ethresear.ch/t/failure-modes-in-eip-8037-and-state-gas-scaling/23975) (aelowsson), 20:29: two equilibrium failure modes arise when users do not want to spend 50% of their budget on state creation, and the note presents corrective state gas scaling measures.

### 4. Calls: ACDE #229
**What happened**
- [ACDE #229](https://forkcast.org/calls/acde/229), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #9](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-9/): EIP-7928 BAL parallel execution, batch reads and parallel state root are mandatory optimizations; EIP-7610 is CFI, while EIP-7872 and EIP-7949 were declined but clients should standardize; Hegotá headliner candidates are EIP-8141 frame transactions, the EIP-8105 encrypted pool, EIP-7807 SSZ execution blocks and consensus layer EIP-7805 FOCIL, with a February 4 deadline.
