## Key topics

### 1. The EIP-8130 payer security fix
**What happened**
- [EIP-8130: bind payer field into payer signature hash](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11808) (chunter-cb), submitted and merged. The payer hash (the `AA_PAYER_TYPE` payload) used to exclude `payer`, so a payer authorization was pinned only to the transaction body and the resolved `sender`, not to which account funds it; validation (`actor_config(payer, actorId)` plus PAYER scope) succeeds for any account that authorizes the same actor. Now the payer's signature is bound to the account being charged.
- Also submitted and merged the same day: [EIP-8130 metadata field](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11805), [policy clarification](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11806), [relax delegate import restriction](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11809).

**Why it matters**
- For sponsorship and enterprise accounts: one payer key may be authorized as PAYER by several accounts, and if the signature is not bound to the paying account it could be used to charge a different one. This kind of cross-account replay is the typical risk of separating the payer in native AA.

### 2. EIP-8037 state gas accounting
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: resolve EIP-7928 conflict and route state gas refills to origin](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11807) (misilva73), submitted. First, the account creation portion of `CREATE`/`CREATE2` is charged unconditionally before the create frame and refilled on exit if the target already existed or creation failed, avoiding the target existence read that EIP-7928 defers; `CALL*` keeps its conditional charge. Second, a frame-local record of where state gas came from is added, so refills route to the origin.

**Why it matters**
- For contract factories and smart account deployment: charging up front and refilling later means deployment transactions must reserve more gas, especially after 8037 raises the cost of state creation.

### 4. Wallet experience
**What happened**
- [julianma: gasless payments have not been a priority for wallet teams because they do not align with their incentives](https://x.com/_julianma/status/2066864199171485805), 19:43: most revenue comes from power users who already hold gas tokens, not new users; clear signing is a different story.

**Why it matters**
- For AA adoption: paymasters and the 8130 payer mechanism can already deliver gasless payments technically, and the bottleneck is more about wallet business incentives.
