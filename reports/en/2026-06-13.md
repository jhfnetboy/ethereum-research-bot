## Key topics

### 1. Security hardening of privacy protocols
**What happened**
- [zooko: thanks to Anthropic for helping protect Zcash users](https://x.com/zooko/status/2065541979979784341), 04:09. At Shielded Labs' request, Anthropic ran a security audit of Zcash with Mythos and found no more serious bugs in the Zcash protocol; Shielded Labs and others continue security hardening work.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy infrastructure: after the Orchard bug, AI-assisted audits become an extra tool, but they do not replace formal verification of circuits.

### 2. Client performance
**What happened**
- [fede_intern: a few days after starting the new benchmarkoor BAL suites, ethrex is already among the top three clients in both benchmarks](https://x.com/fede_intern/status/2065727826045665632), 16:27, currently leading the compute suite at about 1.95 Ggas/s of gas-weighted aggregated throughput. [ethrex posted the same](https://x.com/ethrex_client/status/2065497822250590295), 01:13.

**Why it matters**
- For the gas limit: EIP-7904 concluded no compute repricing is needed precisely because of client throughput after the BAL optimizations, and per-client benchmark data directly supports raising the gas limit later.
