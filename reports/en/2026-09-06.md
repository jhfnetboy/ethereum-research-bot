## Key topics

### 1. Progress on EIP-8141
**What happened**
- [Vitalik: a lot of important progress on frames has been quietly happening over the last few months](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2096370186094076098), 05:49, recommending the write-up and the updated EIP.
- [Vitalik: a positive consequence of the detailed thinking about transaction formats](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2096378061377900881), 06:20, not just 8141 but also "future of state" discussions such as UTXOs, PBT, keyed nonces and the recursive STARK mempool.
- [Derek Chiang: the 8141 authors recently achieved a design breakthrough](https://x.com/decentrek/status/2096587220195192981), 20:12, realizing that things they thought had to be in the transaction envelope did not.
- [nero_eth: frame transactions let users choose their signature scheme, such as passkeys or post-quantum, and enable signature aggregation, key rotation and atomic batching](https://x.com/nero_eth/status/2096466132785856933), 12:10; [soispoke: frames build on a decade of native AA work and are the stepping stone to privacy](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2096594725348278436), 20:41.
- [EIP-8141: define MAX_VERIFY_GAS as a shared mempool floor](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12301) (AnkushinDaniil). The rationale is that the real cost of validating a frame transaction differs several-fold across clients, signature and pairing libraries, storage backends and partially stateless nodes, so a hardcoded per-node maximum is the wrong shape.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets, frame transactions offer a single native AA path in which signature schemes, batching and sponsorship happen in protocol. Whether the validation budget is a floor or a ceiling decides whether complex validation, such as privacy proofs, gets relayed widely.
