## Key topics

### 1. Accounts and gas accounting
**What happened**
- [EIP-8037: apply the EIP-7976 calldata floor to block-level gas accounting](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11908) (jochem-brouwer), submitted; the author considers it an 8037 bug, especially in relation to maximum block size and calldata repricing, and includes a reproduction test.
- [New ERC: account-level transfer with authorization](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1869) (yongqjn), submitted, with a [Magicians thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8335-account-level-transfer-with-authorization/28977) at 10:12. It implements ERC-3009's `transferWithAuthorization` on the smart account: the owner signs an EIP-712 authorization offchain, any relayer can submit it to the account, and the account checks it with its own signature validation.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and payments: authorized transfers no longer depend on whether a token supports 3009, so an account can offer gasless transfers of any token, which is especially relevant to agent payments such as x402. The 8037 fix keeps calldata-heavy blocks within the intended size bound.
