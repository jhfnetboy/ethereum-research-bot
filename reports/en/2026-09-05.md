## Highlights
1. **Faster finality**: the first proof of concept of decoupled consensus, with about a 4x improvement in time to finality.
2. **Payload broadcast**: a new EIP replaces the whole-block `execution_payload` topic with chunked execution payload propagation.
3. **Formal verification**: 9 participants and their agents made 28 submissions, yielding a formally verified MODEXP implementation.

## Key topics

### 1. Faster finality and faster blocks
**What happened**
- [benjaminion: faster finality is coming](https://x.com/benjaminion_xyz/status/2096154357712494719), 15:31, a quick proof of concept on a network a decent fraction of mainnet's size that improved time to finality about 4x, mainly to benchmark decoupled consensus.
- [barnabemonnot: when you read "decoupled consensus", think faster finality and faster blocks](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2096160340887097420), 15:55, with networking setting the speed limit.
- [New EIP: fast execution payload broadcast](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12299) (kamilsa, merged October 1), replacing the `execution_payload` topic with chunked propagation; it later became EIP-8411.

**Why it matters**
- Faster blocks and confirmation directly shorten waits for deposits and bridging, and a larger gas limit needs faster payload propagation to go with it.
