## Key topics

### 1. Transaction privacy: an enshrined encrypted mempool
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Universal Enshrined Encrypted Mempool](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10943) (jannikluhn), submitted; [ethresear.ch discussion](https://ethresear.ch/t/universal-enshrined-encrypted-mempool-eip/23685), 20:47, and [Magicians: EIP-8105](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8105-universal-enshrined-encrypted-mempool/27201), 20:50: scheme agnostic, transactions stay encrypted until included, preventing frontrunning.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: transaction contents stay hidden from builders until inclusion, delivering privacy and MEV protection at the protocol layer instead of private relays.

### 2. Signatures: the ML-DSA precompile
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Precompile for ML-DSA signature verification](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10557) (simonmasson), merged after submission on October 17, 2025.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: ML-DSA is NIST's standard post-quantum signature, and a precompile lets smart accounts verify it at affordable gas.

### 3. Gas and block structure
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: EIP-8077 nonce gap simulation report](https://ethresear.ch/t/eip-8077-nonce-gap-simulation-report/23687) (leobago), 22:12: a Monte Carlo study of how often one sender's consecutive blob transactions land in different shards of a sharded blob mempool; [EIP-7928: remove BAL from the EL block](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10937) (nerolation), submitted and merged; [EIP-7907: add codesize to the account tuple and update gas per word](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/10758) (charles-cooper), merged after submission on November 9, 2025.
