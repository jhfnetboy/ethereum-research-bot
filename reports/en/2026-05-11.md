## Key topics

### 1. Transactions and gas
**What happened**
- [EIP-2780: rework as resource-based intrinsic transaction gas](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11645) (misilva73), submitted. The total is unchanged: a standard transfer's 21,000 is `TX_BASE_COST + COLD_ACCOUNT_ACCESS + TX_VALUE_COST + TRANSFER_LOG_COST`, each priced to the resource the transaction actually consumes.
- [New EIP: Gas Limit Schedule](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11644) (barnabasbusa), submitted as EIP-8261; [EIP-8261 discussion thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8261-gas-limit-schedule/28494), 22:02. The block gas_limit becomes a hard-fork-scheduled, consensus-enforced parameter, removing client flags and validator preferences as well as the EIP-1559 ±1/1024 elasticity rule; "gas parameter only" (gpo) forks are modeled on the BPO forks of EIP-7892.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets and users: EIP-2780 breaks the transfer cost into explainable parts, so repricing one resource later does not require touching the whole 21,000. EIP-8261 makes gas limit changes predictable, so wallets and apps no longer need to track slow drift from validator votes.

### 2. Account abstraction
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: extended feature set](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11643) (pedrouid), submitted, proposing to fold in downstream features such as guarantors (a payer primitive that makes transactions admissible to the public mempool even when sender validation may fail, enabling safe ERC-20 paymaster repayment) and flexible nonces, turning EIP-8141 from a new transaction type into a complete native AA upgrade.
- [growthepie: Ethereum smart wallet upgrades reach new highs](https://x.com/growthepie_eth/status/2053839543032291696), 21:08.
- [ERC-7730: add intent mutability specification](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1738), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: guarantors address a core problem for native AA in the public mempool: who pays for a failure when nodes cannot confirm in advance that sender validation will pass.
