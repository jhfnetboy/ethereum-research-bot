## Key topics

### 1. Post-quantum migration for accounts and the frame devnet
**What happened**
- [Magicians: EIP-8425 quantum freeze and account recovery](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8425-quantum-freeze-and-account-recovery/29770), 00:19 (colinlyguo). Continuing the "quantum recovery pathway" discussion, it asks whether there should be an EIP for freezing and recovering unmigrated accounts, for example building on Vitalik's quantum emergency recovery proposal; preparing and testing implementations across clients in advance could shorten emergency response time. One concern: accounts whose public keys were exposed through signatures but whose owners have lost access cannot migrate, and their funds could fall to quantum attackers.
- [Ethereal news mini #2: ethPandaOps' Hegotá frames-devnet-0 is live, Nethermind 2.0.0, and asanso's Daisugi post-quantum testnet](https://x.com/EtherealnewsHQ/status/2103487897609646467), 21:12.

**Why it matters**
- For account holders, any address that has sent a transaction has an exposed public key. Frame transactions offer a path to post-quantum signatures, and EIP-8425 addresses what happens to accounts that did not migrate in time. frames-devnet-0 is where wallets can start testing frame transactions.

### 2. A faster Ethereum
**What happened**
- [barnabemonnot: after a month of work, EIP-8198 quick slots was merged as a feature spec in the consensus specs](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2103237576496681227), 04:38, thanking spec maintainers jih2nn and jtraglia.
- [fradamt: one step closer to 4 to 8 times faster finality](https://x.com/fradamt/status/2103501375279706556), 22:06; there is now a formally verified proposal for a decoupled consensus protocol in I*, not yet a full spec (up next) but including all the key parts; [barnabemonnot's introduction](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2103527152591204363), 23:48.
- [ethresear.ch: Ethereum's TCB, part 1, the client](https://ethresear.ch/t/ethereums-tcb-part-1-the-client/26086), 17:11 (asn), looking at formal verification through which components, specs, tools and assumptions are trusted rather than proven.

**Why it matters**
- Slot and finality times decide how long exchange deposits, bridging and L2 deposits take; merging the spec means clients can start implementing.
