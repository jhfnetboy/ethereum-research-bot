## Key topics

### 1. Protocol: BAL DoS limits
**What happened**
- [EIP-7928: cap max items in BAL](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11234) (nerolation), submitted: capping what a BAL can realistically contain helps against DoS, with a matching execution spec change.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: a BAL cap bounds worst case validation cost, which keeps parallel execution reliable as the gas limit rises.
