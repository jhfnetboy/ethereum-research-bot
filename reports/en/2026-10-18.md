### 1. The capacity math of Glamsterdam
**What happened**
- [nero_eth: with Glamsterdam, the gas limit goes from 60M to 200M, about 3.3x](https://x.com/nero_eth/status/2111717098271732040), 14:12, and from there 300M is a realistic next step.
- [Barnabé: close to 3.5x more L1 capacity in the next few months, plus more room for blobs](https://x.com/barnabemonnot/status/2111527644223127741), 01:39, if the last testnet fork goes well in ten days.
- [EIP-8151 PR: empty failure output and EIP-8038 access gas](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12540) (colinlyguo, merged the next day).

**Why it matters**
- Capacity rises while state writes get more expensive (EIP-8037), so simple transfers and read-heavy transactions benefit most, and transactions that create new accounts or new storage become relatively more expensive. EIP-8151 restricts ecRecover based on account code, which bears directly on the signature semantics of 7702 accounts, and its gas follows EIP-8038.
