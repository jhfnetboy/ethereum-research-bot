## Key topics

### 1. The transaction assertion API converges: ASSERT and TXTRACE
**What happened**
- [EIP-7906 PR: rename POST_TX to ASSERT](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12528) (lightclient). The reasoning: every EIP-8141 frame mode is one word describing what happens; POST_TX describes where the logic sits, while ASSERT describes what the frame intends to do.
- [EIP-7906 PR: remove TXDIFF](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12530) (lightclient), moving some of the lost functionality into TXTRACE. `contracts_deployed` becomes `codes_changed`, which can also support future opcodes like SETCODEFROM, and `nonces_changes` is added.
- [EIP-8279 PR: remove the unsafe storage-value refund](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12526) (nerolation).
- [New EIP PR: Non-Self-Authorizing State Transitions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12529), the Resolution draft posted on Magicians on October 3.
- [Magicians: ERC-8420 AA-compatible solver](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8420-aa-compatible-solver/29989), 15:23. Users submit an envelope carrying the wallet's signed transaction, it stays compatible with existing smart wallets, and it draws on ERC-7521.

**Why it matters**
- For wallet developers, the ASSERT frame will be the entry point for attaching outcome rules, and the fields it can observe (code changes, nonce changes) determine which rules can be written. Designing `codes_changed` to cover SETCODEFROM means assertions should also be able to detect an account's code being swapped out, the same class of risk as the Safe implementation replacement in the Bybit incident.

### 2. Glamsterdam heads to Hoodi, and privacy tooling
**What happened**
- [EF Blog: Glamsterdam Hoodi testnet announcement](https://blog.ethereum.org/en/2026/10/16/glamsterdam-hoodi-announcement). Activation is at epoch 132,352, slot 4,235,264 (17:42:48 UTC on October 26), and Hoodi's gas limit rises to 200M at the same epoch. The headliners are ePBS (EIP-7732) and BALs (EIP-7928). On gas, EIP-8037 raises and separately meters the cost of creating state, and EIP-8038 updates state access costs. Contracts that rely on fixed gas stipends, hardcoded gas limits or assumptions about remaining gas may need changes.
- [EIP-8081: add Sepolia and Hoodi dates](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12522) and [record the ACDC 189 decisions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12521). [EIP-2780 adds benchmark reports and updates its rationale](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12525), merged.
- [Vitalik: paranoid mode](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2111046730959433793), 17:48, combining zkAPI, Tor, cover traffic and local LLM prompt rewriting in an OpenAI-compatible daemon. [EF: why these new privacy primitives are essential](https://x.com/ethereumfndn/status/2111131384832303512), 23:25.

**Why it matters**
- Hoodi is Glamsterdam's last public testnet before mainnet, and the 200M gas limit takes effect together with the new pricing. For dapps, this is the last window to check gas estimation.
