## Key topics

### 1. Accounts: ERC-4337 moves to Review
**What happened**
- [ERC-4337: move to Review](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1014) (drortirosh), merged after submission on April 17, 2025; [ERC-7674: move to Review](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1404) (Amxx), merged after submission on December 15, 2025; [Add ERC: Bound Signatures](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1427) (wjmelements), merged after submission on December 24, 2025, an alternative to ERC-2098.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: ERC-4337 is today's main smart account standard, and Review means the interface is settling, so wallets and bundlers can rely on it with more confidence.

### 2. Calls: ACDC #172 and ACDE #227 decisions
**What happened**
- [ACDC #172](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdc/172), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #6](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-6/): [Hegotá headliner proposals](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8081-hegota-network-upgrade-meta-thread/26876) are due February 4 and need a Magicians post and an ACD presentation; an [EIP champion guide](https://github.com/ethereum/pm/pull/1864) was proposed.
- [EIP-7773: status updates from ACDE #227](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11041) (nixorokish), submitted and merged: EIP-7997 to CFI, EIP-8058, EIP-7668 and EIP-7745 to DFI.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts and transactions: the deadline decides whether native AA (EIP-8141), FOCIL and encrypted mempools can compete for Hegotá headliner slots.
