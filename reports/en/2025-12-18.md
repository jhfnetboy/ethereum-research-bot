## Key topics

### 1. Calls: ACDE #226
**What happened**
- [ACDE #226](https://forkcast.org/calls/acde/226), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #3](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-3/): Glamsterdam targets June 2026; repricing for 60 MGas/s is CFI, including EIP-7904 general repricing, EIP-7976 higher calldata floor cost, EIP-7981 higher access list cost, EIP-8038 state access gas increase and EIP-2780 lower intrinsic transaction cost; EIP-2926, EIP-7686, EIP-7923 and EIP-7973 are declined; the rest go to the January 5 ACDE; the Hegotá name is chosen.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: EIP-2780 lowers simple transfer costs while pricier calldata and access lists hit data heavy transactions, so everyday transfers get cheaper overall.

### 2. Accounts: Tempo Transactions
**What happened**
- [gakonst: Tempo Transactions, an EIP-2718 transaction built on 2025 AA lessons, work without relays and are a more censorship resistant way to use passkeys](https://x.com/gakonst/status/2001354566819025266), 01:11.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: building passkeys, sponsorship and other AA features into a transaction type instead of bundlers is the direction of Ethereum's native AA debate.

### 3. Transactions: conditional transactions
**What happened**
