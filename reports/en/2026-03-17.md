## Key topics

### 1. Privacy: enshrined private transfers
**What happened**
- [Add EIP: Private ETH and ERC-20 Transfers](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11373) (RogerPodacter), merged: a protocol enshrined privacy pool system contract defining a canonical validity layer for private ETH and ERC-20 transfers with public deposits and withdrawals; fixed address, hard fork only upgrades, no admin keys, a shared note tree and nullifier set, and a recursive proof architecture.
- [ethereum: zkPass is a privacy preserving identity protocol on Ethereum that proves facts about users without revealing the data](https://x.com/ethereum/status/2033945733775454273), 23:37; [EliBenSasson: privacy is not yes or no but a question of how much and what kind](https://x.com/EliBenSasson/status/2033930525056840180), 22:36.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: a shared pool with no admin keys and hard fork only upgrades gives all users one anonymity set, instead of fragmented pools each with a small set.

### 2. Native AA: EIP-8141 mempool policy
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: add mempool policy](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11415) (lightclient), submitted: the key change versus ERC-7562 is relying on a canonical paymaster contract, which removes the complex staking based reputation system and can gain features over time.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: ERC-4337's reputation and staking rules are the hardest part of bundler implementations; a canonical paymaster contract makes it easier for the public mempool to accept sponsored transactions safely.

### 3. Consensus and confirmation
**What happened**
- [_julianma: a fast confirmation rule explainer](https://x.com/_julianma/status/2033851796574154808), 17:23; per [Ethereal news #16](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-16/), the rule assumes a synchronous network and an adversary with at most 25% of stake, falls back to finality, cuts L2 and exchange deposits to about 13 seconds and rolls out in the coming months; [binji_x: what 60x faster confirmation means for users](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2033870230049476762), 18:37; [benjaminion_xyz: it is not fast finality, but an incredible upgrade for most applications](https://x.com/benjaminion_xyz/status/2033875896340250960), 18:59.
- [ethresear.ch: Why Ethereum Needs a Dynamically Available Protocol](https://ethresear.ch/t/why-ethereum-needs-a-dynamically-available-protocol/24418), 20:56: a two layer design for the next consensus protocol, with a fast available chain from a small random committee and a separate trailing finality mechanism, fully decoupled.
