## Key topics

### 1. Both native AA tracks move on the same day
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: add SIGDATACOPY so the stack requirement for SIGPARAM is static](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12187) (lightclient), submitted and merged. A dynamic stack size for `SIGPARAM` when copying `ARBITRARY` sigdata had been flagged as problematic several times, so the PR splits that behavior into a separate instruction.
- [EIP-8141: pin the initial accessed_addresses set for frame transactions](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12113) and [relax the banned opcode list](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12167) (Marchhill), merged; see August 6 and August 14 for the content.
- [EIP-8130: adoption profiles, bounded auth gas and timestamp normalization](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12185) (chunter-cb), submitted. 8130 is specified once but adopted under one of two profiles: the L1 profile makes the intrinsic gas schedule normative (the gas schedule is the spec, changeable only by hard fork) and uses a permissive authenticator acceptance policy.

**Why it matters**
- For wallets: reading signature data through a separate instruction makes validation contract gas more predictable. 8130's profiles answer L2 demands by letting L2s have their own authenticator policies, which is the key question in whether 8141 and 8130 can converge.
