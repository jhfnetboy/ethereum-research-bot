## Key topics

### 1. Transactions: sponsorship and the original sender
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Resolving technical debt from the ORIGIN opcode](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/resolving-technical-debt-from-the-origin-opcode/27353) (Helkomine), 15:26: revives the withdrawn EIP-2711 to let others pay gas while keeping the original sender, which it says EIP-7702 still does not provide.

**Why it matters**
