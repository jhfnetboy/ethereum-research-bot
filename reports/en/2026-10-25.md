## Key topics

### 1. Letting contracts tell OOG from ordinary failure
**What happened**
- [New EIP: CALLSTATUS instruction](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12586) (svlachakis). `CALLSTATUS` (`0xA5`, 2 gas) pushes the completion status of the current frame's most recent call or create as three bits: reverted, halted, and halt somewhere below. `0` is success, `1` reverted or not started, `2` halted exceptionally or address collision, `4` succeeded after a halt below, and `5` reverted after a halt below; a value of 2 or more means gas forwarded somewhere along the call line was consumed by an exceptional halt. The motivation: the transaction sender can cause an OOG by choosing the gas, while today contracts can only judge from `GAS` arithmetic after the call.
- [EIP-8141: charge the EIP-8037 legacy share as extra execution gas](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12583) was merged, and [svlachakis's PR of the same name](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12587) was merged too.

**Why it matters**
- ChainSecurity advised against inferring failure causes from remaining gas, and CALLSTATUS offers an explicit signal instead. If this EIP is adopted, contract logic that continues after a failed call could simply check `CALLSTATUS ≥ 2` to decide whether to revert or record the failure.

### 2. Post-quantum accounts and consensus
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: a hash-only rollup, OP Stack L2 accounts as Lamport-style hash chains with no ECDSA inside the L2](https://ethresear.ch/t/a-hash-only-rollup-op-stack-l2-accounts-as-lamport-style-hash-chains-no-ecdsa-inside-the-l2/26172), 06:24 (by the founder of bb84; chain ID 42069000, live on mainnet since September 30). An account is the tip of a keccak-256 hash chain, spending reveals the next preimage, and a commit-reveal step binds each preimage to one transaction. The only assumption inside the L2 is keccak-256 preimage resistance, which Grover reduces to about 2^128 work. ECDSA still appears at the L1 boundary.
- [tcoratger: digging into the intersection of EIP-8453 decoupled consensus and post-quantum](https://x.com/tcoratger/status/2114117844396822869), 05:12, starting from why we cannot simply shorten epochs or slots.
- [Eli Ben-Sasson: why hash-based cryptography is safest against superintelligence](https://x.com/EliBenSasson/status/2114307230560649286), 17:45, since all other forms of cryptography imply secure hashes.
- [StarkWare: Solana users can already store SOL in a post-quantum vault, yet Solana passes just 2 of 8 cryptographic agility checks](https://x.com/StarkWareLtd/status/2114345992497823955), 20:19, because an opt-in application protects some funds without changing the cryptography of native accounts.

**Why it matters**
- Hash-chain accounts show a minimal post-quantum account model that needs no signatures, at the cost of a commit step before every spend.
