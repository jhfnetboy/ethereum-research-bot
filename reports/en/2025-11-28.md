## Key topics

### 1. Privacy: Private ERC-20
**What happened**
- [Magicians: Private ERC-20, zero knowledge burns for token privacy](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8093-ppob-on-erc20-contract-storage/26763) (jakeolo), 17:50: extends EIP-7503 proof of burn to ERC-20, so tokens sent to cryptographically unspendable addresses can be privately re-minted; [Add ERC: Private ERC-20](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1379) (jakeolo), submitted.

**Why it matters**
- For privacy: burning and re-minting cuts the onchain link between sender and receiver, but the anonymity set depends on burn addresses looking like normal ones.
