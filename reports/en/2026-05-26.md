## Key topics

### 1. Account abstraction: ERC-4337 goes final and native AA's privacy combination
**What happened**
- [ERC-4337: move to Final](https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/pull/1773) (yoavw), submitted.
- [soispoke: if we ship FOCIL (EIP-7805), Frame Transactions (EIP-8141), Keyed Nonces (EIP-8250) and Recent Roots (EIP-8272) in Hegota, we get native, trustless, censorship resistant private transactions on Ethereum next year](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2059269087381852618), 20:43.
- [soispoke: new EIP-8272, Recent Roots for Frame Transactions, by soispoke, nero_eth and Vitalik](https://x.com/soispoke/status/2059265180459004391), 20:27; [EIP-8272 discussion thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8272-recent-roots-for-frame-transactions/28621), 20:15: a root source writes roots to a system contract keyed by `(source_id, slot)`, and frame transactions reference them during validation without reading mutable storage.
- [binji: getting to a natively private Ethereum for payments, with four Hegota features that could change the game](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2059287972806025720), 21:58.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: ERC-4337 going final means the contract level AA interfaces are stable for wallets, bundlers and paymasters to rely on long term; meanwhile the native AA direction is now clear, with frame transactions letting contract accounts originate transactions, FOCIL guaranteeing inclusion, and keyed nonces and recent roots letting privacy pool transactions run concurrently and be validated in the mempool.

### 2. Privacy: wallets and reviews
**What happened**
- [Vitalik: kassandraETH, ncsgy and others have worked for nearly a year on Kohaku, whose goal is security (and trustlessness) plus privacy (read and write) at the access layer](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2059239031133634783), 18:43; [binji: ncsgy made a quantum resistant transaction on an Ethereum L1 testnet with Kohaku for about $0.07](https://x.com/binji_x/status/2059262367356125555), 20:16.
- [ethbeat: L2BEAT releases its Privacy page, scrutinizing the CROPS properties of Ethereum's privacy protocols](https://x.com/ethbeat_org/status/2059276126522904764), 21:11.
- [donnoh_eth: recommending sekubalias's piece on why just using a privacy protocol is not enough to protect your privacy](https://x.com/donnoh_eth/status/2059286218416755013), 21:51.
- [aztecnetwork: privacy on Ethereum is already here, and developers without a cryptography background can build private contracts on Aztec](https://x.com/aztecnetwork/status/2059289183655711213), 22:02; [Nethermind: shipped a multi-asset fee payment contract for Aztec to testnet](https://x.com/Nethermind/status/2059277899157340307), 21:18.

**Why it matters**
- For users: onchain privacy only covers writes, while a wallet's RPC requests for balances and history also expose addresses and IPs; Kohaku puts read and write privacy into the wallet together, and the L2BEAT privacy page lets users compare the trust assumptions of privacy protocols.
