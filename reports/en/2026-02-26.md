## Key topics

### 1. Native AA: the Hegotá headliner race and alternatives
**What happened**
- [ACDE #231](https://forkcast.org/calls/acde/231), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #13](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-13/): the execution layer headliner will be decided next ACDE, with [EIP-8141](https://forkcast.org/eips/8141/) frame transactions or no headliner leading and Geth, Erigon, Besu and Nimbus having posted preferences; for encrypted pools, LUCID is the sole proposal after [EIP-8105](https://forkcast.org/eips/8105/) was withdrawn; the EraE execution history archive format supersedes Era1.
- [Add EIP: Composable Transaction](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11355) (rakita), submitted: a new EIP-2718 transaction type with extensible capability based signatures and native sponsorship, a simpler alternative to EIP-8141 with no new opcodes, execution frames or per frame gas accounting, where the payer co signs and is charged for gas.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts and gas: EIP-8141 is the most complete but most complex, while EIP-8175 only solves swappable signatures and sponsorship; client preferences between them decide whether Hegotá has an execution layer headliner.
