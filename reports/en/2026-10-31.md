## Key topics

### 1. Account and signing security
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: transaction nonce check boundary](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12603) was merged (raxhvl). In this breaking fix, the sender nonce is accepted only up to `MAX-1`, because the check must leave room for the nonce increment of this transaction, matching the bogota execution specs; implementers need to review their boundary checks.
- [ERC-8481: agent pre-sign risk report](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8481-agent-pre-sign-risk-report/30145), 20:56 (TOLL), with 34 posts that day. AI agents now prepare and sign transactions with no person reading each one, and the resulting losses are mostly not exotic and visible before signing: an unlimited approval to a plain wallet or a week-old contract, a transfer to a lookalike address sharing first and last characters, or buying a copy of a real token.
- [ERC-8478: x402 payee verification for ERC-8004 agents](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8478-x402-payee-verification-for-erc-8004-agents/30137), 01:03.
- [Magicians: ERC draft, account authority lifecycle for STVA](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-draft-account-authority-lifecycle-for-stva/30138), 12:43; [ethresear.ch: independent simulation result for STVA](https://ethresear.ch/t/erc-draft-independent-simulation-result-for-stva/26198), 04:36.
- [SEAL: the first signals came up in a group chat of independent security researchers](https://x.com/_SEAL_Org/status/2116543285141344259), 21:50; exchanges, analytics firms, issuers, law enforcement and researchers all have to trust each other enough to act in minutes.

**Why it matters**
- After the Binance incident, signing security became this week's ERC theme, from human multisigs (STVA) to AI agents (ERC-8481), all aiming to show verifiable, readable risk information before signing. Wallets and agent frameworks can start by building three checks into the signing flow: unlimited approvals, lookalike addresses and copycat tokens.

### 2. Consensus client diversity
**What happened**
- [LambdaClass: ethlambda v1.0.0 is here](https://x.com/class_lambda/status/2116226842084692167), 00:52. The Lean Ethereum consensus client now has full beacon chain support including Gloas, runs on mainnet, Sepolia, Hoodi and Platåberget, and its Sepolia validators transitioned to Glamsterdam without a single issue. It is built in Rust.
- [ladislaus0x: back-to-back consensus client announcements are rare, Silver yesterday and ethlambda today](https://x.com/ladislaus0x/status/2116249603389743583), 02:23; [trent_vanepps: two new consensus clients in one week, and the lambda team is also exploring bundling its EL and CL into a single client](https://x.com/trent_vanepps/status/2116545398898561205), 21:58.

**Why it matters**
- Lighthouse holds about 49% of the consensus layer (per clientdiversity.org as cited by Ethereal news #45, data may not be accurate), and new clients are the long-term way to reduce single-client risk. Stakers should note that new clients still need security reviews and testing, so try them on testnets first.
