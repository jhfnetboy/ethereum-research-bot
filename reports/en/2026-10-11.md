## Key topics

### 1. ERC-8403: an account as one hash
**What happened**
- [ethresear.ch: ERC-8403, an account as one hash, and post-quantum keys](https://ethresear.ch/t/erc-8403-an-account-as-one-hash-and-post-quantum-keys/26147), 11:24 (AnkushinDaniil). The account stores one 32-byte root. Each authority (a signing key, or a rule such as a spend limit or an unlock time) is a leaf, `leaf = keccak256(authority_id || predicate)`. A transaction carries a membership proof that the leaf is under the account's root, and a witness that the leaf's holder approved the transaction. The post then asks what the account looks like with post-quantum keys on top of EIP-8288.

**Why it matters**
- Today each account model defines adding and removing keys its own way, and wallets have to support each one. With a single "root plus leaves" model, wallets implement one key management scheme, and switching to post-quantum keys just means swapping leaves. The account also uses only one storage slot, in line with state becoming more expensive after Glamsterdam.
