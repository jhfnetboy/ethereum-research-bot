## Key topics

### 1. Accounts: refactoring EIP-7932
**What happened**
- [EIP-7932: separate the precompile and add autonomous and detached signature support](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11571) (SirSpudlington), submitted: to work better with EIP-8141 and EIP-8164, signatures are formalized as autonomous or detached and the recovery functions are renamed.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: EIP-7932 is the protocol level signature algorithm registry that frame transactions and native key delegation use to recognize new algorithms; clear signature formats are what let wallets and clients handle post-quantum and other new schemes consistently.
