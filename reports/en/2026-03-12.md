## Key topics

### 1. ACDE #232
**What happened**
- Call notes are from [Ethereal news #15](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-15/): Glamsterdam's bal-devnet-3 launch was delayed over complexity concerns about EIP-8037's higher state creation gas cost; the Hegotá EL headliner will be decided at the next ACDE, defaulting to none, with EIP-8141 frame transactions the only remaining candidate, backed by Geth and Erigon and needing more teams to be selected; EIP-8184 LUCID encrypted transaction pool and EIP-7807 SSZ execution blocks were declined for inclusion.
- [fede_intern: recommends watching the last ACD, which he calls pure politics based on interests rather than technology](https://x.com/fede_intern/status/2032138220260012480), 23:54.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: whether native AA makes Hegotá depends on more client teams agreeing to implement it, making this a pivotal week for EIP-8141.

### 2. Native AA: EIP-8141 approval bits
**What happened**
- [EIP-8141: add approval bits to frame](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11401) (fjl), submitted and merged: adds configuration bits to a frame's mode that constrain what it can APPROVE, letting users tell their own account what to approve while still signing the transaction sighash directly; [EIP-8141: clean up frame access opcodes](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11400) (fjl), submitted and merged, adding dedicated opcodes for reading frame input data.
- [EIP-7773: SFI the Glamsterdam EL EIPs](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/11399) (spencer-tb), submitted, covering EIP-7708, 7778, 7843, 7928, 7954, 7976, 7981, 8024 and 8037.

**Why it matters**
- For accounts: approval bits scope each frame's permissions, so wallets can clearly separate paying gas from executing and paying, narrowing how a signature can be misused.
