## Key topics

### 1. Calls: ACDT #66
**What happened**
- [ACDT #66](https://forkcast.org/calls/acdt/066), 21:00, per [Ethereal news #8](https://ethereal.news/ethereal-news-weekly-8/): [bal-devnet-2](https://notes.ethereum.org/@ethpandaops/bal-devnet-2) targets late January with EIP-7843 SLOTNUM, EIP-8024 SWAPN, DUPN and EXCHANGE, EIP-7708 ETH transfer logs and EIP-7778 refund free block gas accounting; CFI scope is final, including the [EIP-8037](https://forkcast.org/eips/8037/) state creation gas increase, and Marius van der Wijden's [explainer](https://notes.ethereum.org/@MariusVanDerWijden/SJD3CGdSbx) notes it allows gas limit increases without repricing state operations each time, with app developer feedback needed on the dynamic cost.

**Why it matters**
- For gas: a dynamic state creation cost means contract deployment and account creation prices move with the gas limit, so wallets and apps must adjust estimates.
